# Chapter 1 Brief

## Chapter

- Number: 1
- Working title: `Tiếng chợ`
- POV: Nguyễn Minh Khanh, close third-person limited
- Primary location: family home/votive-paper shop + market crossroads
- Arc(s): Volume 1 — `Những chuyện tưởng đã kết thúc`

## Purpose

Make the reader believe in Khanh's ordinary home, family, market and post-Japan life before asking them to believe anything supernatural. Establish the book's voice: lived Northern Vietnamese normality, family warmth, practical humor, oral ghost-story culture and Khanh's skeptical-but-not-dismissive mind.

The chapter should end with a very small bodily unease, not a case trigger or paranormal reveal.

## Starting state

- character: Khanh has already been home roughly 2–3 days after returning from Japan and is resting before resuming work in Hanoi.
- relationship: family relations are basically good and familiar; Hùng/Khanh are not highly verbal; Lan is practical and busy.
- knowledge: Khanh knows the family/shop world and his grandfather's reputation from childhood, but has no current mystery to solve.
- object/location: the family shop is active and approaching a busy Rằm tháng Giêng period; the market starts before dawn.
- threat/stakes: none beyond ordinary reintegration and Khanh's old bodily fear associations.

## Ending state

- character: Khanh feels more physically re-entered into home life but is reminded that his body can still react before his rational mind does.
- relationship: reader understands Lan/Hùng/Khanh family rhythm and sees Mai as a competent adult hometown friend, not a romance setup or sidekick.
- knowledge: reader understands Khanh is a software engineer returning from Japan, the shop is ordinary family commerce, and strange stories are normal conversation here rather than automatic fact.
- object/location: home/shop/market are established as living recurring spaces.
- threat/stakes: one small ordinary-but-unsettling interruption of a familiar pattern leaves a trace of unease; no active supernatural claim.

## Scene sequence

1. **Market before the day properly begins** — Khanh wakes or lies awake to predawn/early market sounds he once knew by heart: trucks, shutters, chopping, motorbikes, vendors, dogs, household movement. Goal: re-enter home through perception rather than exposition. Turn: familiar noise makes clear how long he has been away.
2. **Downstairs family/shop routine** — breakfast, small jobs and practical talk with Lan and Hùng. Near-Rằm workload appears through action. Lan can tell one short, gentle uncanny customer anecdote while working, framed explicitly as something somebody told her rather than proven truth. Khanh naturally offers a mundane possibility without mocking her.
3. **Mai crosses the shop/market flow** — she appears because of real logistics/business movement, not to visit the protagonist on cue. A light `Việt kiều Nhật` tease is allowed. The scene should show her operational competence and their old familiarity without flirting or backstory dump.
4. **Quiet turn at the end** — after the busiest noise shifts away, an ordinary sound from upstairs/old-house structure briefly triggers Khanh's childhood ghost-fear reflex before he can reason about it. Give or imply a plausible mundane explanation; the important fact is his involuntary bodily response, not whether the house is haunted. End on resumed ordinary activity rather than a jump scare.

## Information movement

### Reader learns

- Khanh is 27 and recently returned from years of software work in Japan.
- He is home temporarily and expects to resume normal work in Hanoi.
- Lan runs the shop day-to-day; Hùng is quieter and practical.
- the market/shop environment is busy, commercial and ordinary rather than gothic.
- Mai belongs naturally to the local logistics/food-supply world.
- Khanh does not automatically dismiss spiritual stories, but he checks ordinary causes first.
- his childhood fear of ghosts still has a bodily afterimage.

### POV character learns

No major new factual revelation. The chapter is about re-entry and recalibration, not discovery.

### POV character must NOT learn yet

- anything about the future Case Zero purchaser;
- the dead third sibling or buried Case Zero history;
- grandfather's refusal notes/notebooks as a pattern;
- any confirmed supernatural rule;
- any reason to think he is spiritually sensitive.

## Promises / questions

- planted: soft thematic promise that ordinary life and fear coexist; grandfather's memory exists in the house/family without becoming a quest.
- advanced: none formally.
- paid off: none.
- opened: no formal case question yet.
- partially answered: none.
- resolved: none.

Do not create Story Skills promise/question entities merely for these soft tonal setups.

## Continuity risks

- opening too creepy and teaching the reader that the family house is a haunted-house setting;
- turning Lan's story into exposition or objective supernatural canon;
- explaining the whole family/grandfather history in Chapter 1;
- making Mai's appearance feel like romance-bait or a designated sidekick entrance;
- using `Cán bộ Nhật` instead of the established `Việt kiều Nhật` joke;
- inventing an exact hometown proper name;
- loading the chapter with ritual terminology that has not become necessary yet;
- making Khanh sound like a detective or programmer giving a root-cause lecture.

## Forbidden changes

- no Case Zero order/customer in Chapter 1;
- no full apparition;
- no Japan nightmare opening;
- no Thủy, Nhân, workplace cast or police contact;
- no chosen-heir implication;
- no confirmation of grandfather's supernatural abilities;
- no paranormal investigation decision;
- no cliffhanger that promises an immediate haunted-house plot.
