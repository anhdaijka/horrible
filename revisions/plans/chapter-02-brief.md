# Chapter 2 Brief

## Chapter

- Number: 2
- Working title: `Người đặt`
- POV: Nguyễn Minh Khanh
- Primary location: family votive-paper shop / immediate market frontage
- Arc(s): Volume 1 opening; Case Zero inciting order
- Status: **awaiting author approval**

## Purpose

Introduce the woman who places the order that will later become Case Zero, while making the transaction feel ordinary enough that neither Khanh nor the reader is instructed to fear it yet.

The chapter must establish fair-play clues without highlighting them as clues. The first explanation available to the family should remain mundane: somebody may be seriously ill and relatives may be preparing paper goods in advance.

The chapter should end with the order existing in the shop's normal workflow, not with a horror button.

## Starting state

- Khanh: still on break at home, helping intermittently at the shop; no active mystery.
- Lan/Hùng: normal shop/household work continues.
- Shop: ordinary customer traffic and custom-order logistics already established.
- Case Zero: not yet psychologically important to anyone.

## Ending state

- A custom order has been recorded for a named living man.
- The purchaser has supplied a separate `người đặt` identity and says she will return in three days.
- One unusually specific item — an old-style cassette radio — makes the order memorable as a work problem, not yet as a supernatural sign.
- The family still has a plausible ordinary model: the intended recipient may be seriously ill, and the purchaser/family may simply be preparing ahead.
- No one has yet established that the recipient is healthy.

## Scene sequence

### 1. Ordinary shop pressure

Goal: re-enter shop life without reintroducing everyone.

- A normal working period with interruptions, substitutions, packaging, stock questions or a small errand.
- Keep the scene useful and socially textured rather than atmospheric.
- Khanh is available to write/custom-order details because bà Lan is occupied with another customer/task.

Turn: a woman in the rough visual range of 50–60 arrives and asks for several ordinary male paper goods plus a custom object.

### 2. The order

Goal: record the transaction exactly enough for later fair play.

The woman should look and behave **ordinary**, not ghost-coded.

First-appearance rule:
- give 2–4 integrated physical anchors;
- observer age is only an estimate;
- establish what her hands are doing naturally enough that later CCTV handedness can be compared;
- do not use pallor, coldness, strange eyes, silence, archaic speech or any other horror shorthand.

Order choreography:
- ordinary male paper goods remain flexible and research-grounded;
- she asks for an old-style cassette radio and describes enough visible construction detail for the shop/maker to reproduce it;
- Khanh records `làm cho ai`: full name of the male recipient plus enough locality/address detail to distinguish him from a namesake;
- age/`tuổi âm` may appear only because the purchaser supplies it, not because the narration claims it is mandatory;
- Khanh separately asks/records `người đặt` for pickup/order tracking;
- she gives the name that will later belong to the long-dead third sibling;
- she gives no useful phone number or says she will return herself;
- she requests pickup in three days;
- bà Lan may need to check whether the radio can be made on that schedule.

Fair-play body detail:
- the purchaser naturally uses her **left hand** for one or more ordinary actions (writing, holding/returning the pen, counting/passing cash, opening a bag, moving the order slip).
- Do not narrate `she was left-handed` or make Khanh notice it consciously.

### 3. Ordinary explanation wins

Goal: prevent the chapter from announcing the mystery too soon.

After the purchaser leaves:
- any mild curiosity should be commercial/practical, especially the cassette radio or deadline;
- bà Lan/Khanh can infer that the named man may be gravely ill or that the family knows something outsiders do not;
- buying paper goods in advance is not treated as rare or supernatural;
- nobody needs to investigate the recipient now.

The shop immediately moves on to other work.

End on an ordinary action: checking with a maker/supplier, setting the order slip aside, packing another customer's goods, receiving a delivery, eating late breakfast/lunch, or another concrete task.

## Information movement

### Reader learns

- the purchaser's appearance and ordinary behavior;
- the separate purchaser/recipient names exist on the order record;
- the custom cassette radio is unusually specific;
- the purchaser plans to return in three days;
- the left-hand behavior is visible but unmarked;
- the family has a completely plausible reason not to find advance purchase alarming.

### Khanh learns

Only the commercial/order facts above. He does **not** yet know:
- the purchaser name belongs to a dead woman;
- the recipient is healthy;
- the recipient will die four days later;
- the radio has old family significance.

### POV character must NOT learn yet

- no CCTV comparison;
- no surviving-sister resemblance;
- no old third-sibling history;
- no grandfather connection;
- no evidence that the recipient is unexpectedly healthy unless the author decides that reveal belongs at the very end of Chapter 2 rather than Chapter 3. Default: save it for later.

## Prose / depth targets

- Apply `normal first, wrongness later` literally: this chapter may contain **zero felt horror**.
- Use free-indirect closeness rather than narrator explanation of Khanh's skepticism.
- Let shop dialogue carry subtext through practical questions rather than ritual exposition.
- Use scene for the actual order; summarize repetitive shop logistics around it.
- Do not highlight fair-play details with ominous phrasing.
- The woman should have enough physical specificity to be remembered later, but not so much that the text advertises her as important.
- Preserve social address: older adults in narration use appropriate kinship or `ông/bà + tên` forms when named.

Do not invent:
- a universal funeral-paper-goods package;
- a universal requirement for full name + lunar age + address;
- a standard three-day custom lead time;
- a supernatural meaning for the cassette radio itself.

## Continuity risks

- accidentally signaling the purchaser as supernatural through description;
- making bà Lan unrealistically shocked by advance purchase;
- revealing too early that the recipient is healthy;
- overemphasizing left-handedness;
- turning the order-taking scene into ethnographic exposition;
- letting the cassette radio prove supernatural knowledge rather than remain compatible with a living information route.

## Forbidden changes

- Do not identify the purchaser.
- Do not establish the recipient's coming death as known/foreseen by Khanh or family.
- Do not imply advance purchase is culturally abnormal by itself.
- Do not make the woman visibly spectral, temporally displaced or obviously impersonating someone.
- Do not introduce a new supernatural rule.
- Do not turn Chapter 2 into investigation.
