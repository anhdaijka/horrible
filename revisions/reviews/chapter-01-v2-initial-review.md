## What this version intentionally changes

The chapter no longer asks the reader to wait for the novel to become horror.

Opening image/fact:

> on the morning Vũ Văn Cường dies, a paper cassette radio already made for him is still waiting uncollected on bà Lan's shop cabinet.

The opening then discovers the order date predates his death and rewinds four days to the original sale.

The reader therefore carries dread into an otherwise ordinary transaction without requiring ghost-coded weather, body or dialogue.

## Character / physicality result

### Bà Lan

First appearance integrates build, hair and dry/quick shop hands. Her recognition is shown by interruption of practiced motion when Cường's name is mentioned.

### Khanh

Introduced while doing shop work, with small physical/homecoming anchors. His analytical attention does not switch on until the order-date mismatch actually exists.

### Purchaser

Fresh baseline established from scratch:

- mid/late-fifties impression;
- medium build / slightly rounded shoulders;
- short greying hair;
- practical market clothing and shopping bag;
- left-handed writing/sketching integrated into ordinary task behavior.

No spectral physical markers.

## Cultural/shop result

Scene avoids claiming a universal funeral package.

After-death family purchase is small/practical and compatible with current funeral logistics. Original custom order uses family-shop administration (`người đặt`, recipient, pickup date) rather than claiming ritual requirements.

The custom radio is coordinated with an outside maker and the three-day deadline is treated as potentially tight, not an industry norm.
