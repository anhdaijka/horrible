# Opening Genre Reset Audit

Status: author feedback received; material direction reset required before further drafting.

## Trigger

After rereading Chapters 1–2, the author identified a structural genre failure rather than a local prose problem:

- Chapter 2 leaks the Case Zero premise by having characters/narration immediately frame the order as advance preparation for a living, possibly dying person.
- Narration repeatedly explains or normalizes what the reader should infer, weakening suspense and surprise.
- Across the first two chapters, the opening does not yet provide a strong enough promise that this is a compelling Vietnamese ghost story.
- The accumulated rules around realism, ambiguity, ordinary-life-first, cultural respect and epistemic caution may be suppressing horror rather than grounding it.

Do not continue to Chapter 3 from the current trajectory.

## Core diagnosis

### 1. Realism was allowed to become anti-horror

The project correctly requires cultural research and rejects invented ritual claims. That does **not** require fictional supernatural events to be emotionally neutral, weak, or permanently undecidable.

Research accuracy belongs to real-world cultural facts. The fiction may still contain genuine supernatural encounters.

### 2. `Normal first` became `genre promise later`

The current opening spends too much narrative capital proving that life is ordinary before giving the reader a reason to believe something frightening is coming.

A ghost story may use ordinary detail heavily while still making an early promise of death, haunting, dread, taboo, strange testimony, or a supernatural disturbance.

### 3. Ambiguity became the default answer instead of one tool

The project should distinguish:

- uncertainty about **mechanism/cosmology**;
- uncertainty about **witness interpretation**;
- whether a particular encounter is allowed to feel genuinely supernatural.

Not every case or scene should retreat to equal-weight natural explanations. Some events may be human, some ambiguous, and some strongly or plainly supernatural without explaining the universe.

### 4. The narrator has been over-disciplined

The effort to avoid melodrama and over-explanation created a narrator that often explains normality instead. The narrator may create anticipation, dread, irony and tonal framing. What must be avoided is solving or neutralizing the story for the reader.

### 5. Case Zero information order is currently wrong

The shop should not begin by consciously framing the purchase as `buying for a living person`.

Stronger information order:

1. woman buys/orders male votive goods using ordinary shop behavior;
2. shop naturally assumes the named recipient is dead / the goods concern a death, because nothing tells them otherwise;
3. later they discover the order was placed while the recipient was still alive;
4. first mundane repair: perhaps family knew he was gravely ill and prepared early;
5. later evidence removes that repair: he was outwardly healthy, active, and his household did not expect his death;
6. he dies unexpectedly, turning the original ordinary transaction into foreknowledge.

The anomaly is therefore revealed **retroactively**, not narrated in advance.

## Required rethink before revision

Audit and likely relax or replace rules that currently over-constrain:

- `Normal first. Wrongness later.` as a chapter/novel-level law;
- `If the reader already feels something is wrong before the story has earned it, the scene is probably over-written.`;
- Chapter 2 target of `zero felt horror`;
- default equal-weight natural/supernatural ambiguity;
- reflexive suppression of atmospheric or storyteller framing;
- ending every uncanny beat with an immediate mundane explanation.

Preserve:

- cultural research gate;
- natural Vietnamese dialogue;
- character agency;
- physical/body-language baselines;
- social address;
- no invented religious doctrine;
- no chosen-one/power fantasy;
- Khanh checks natural causes when that is character-appropriate.

## Opening-level goal after reset

The first chapter(s) must deliver both:

1. **trustworthy lived Vietnamese reality**, and
2. **a clear pleasurable promise of ghost-story danger / dread / the unseen**.

The reader should not need to wait several chapters to learn what kind of emotional experience the novel intends to give them.

## Current manuscript disposition

- Chapter 1 remains formally canon for now but is under material opening-direction reconsideration.
- Chapter 2 is **not approved** and must not be canonized from the current draft.
- Do not plan Chapter 3 until the opening genre reset is author-approved.
