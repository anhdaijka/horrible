# CH-01 — a morning at the shop

Bà Lan and Khanh sit at the entrance of the family's votive-paper shop beside the market crossroads. Khanh has his laptop open, doing real work in fits and starts while helping his mother when she needs another hand. Customers have purchases to make and time to linger; conversation moves through village ghost stories.

Follow the author's sequence: shop/laptop → mother and customers tell ghost stories → the listeners respond and offer related accounts → Khanh unexpectedly encounters widely shared local hanging/suicide news on social media → he asks his mother → she and the customers are surprised and uninformed → another customer from xóm Trại arrives to buy ordinary goods and tells of Cường's death, the dreadful sight of his body and the distress of Lan Anh, Toàn and the household.

Give the embedded tales enough duration, ordinary circumstances, speech and concrete experience to be worth hearing in their own right. They should frighten or unsettle; listeners may disagree, misremember, attend selectively or prefer their own explanation. Purchases and family work continue around the speakers. The author explicitly wants sustained natural storytelling, not abbreviated ghost anecdotes or a row of information exchanges.

The death news interrupts people whose imagined terrors were at a comfortable distance. Give the transition and family grief time. Preserve the author’s focus on people experiencing the death rather than explaining a case. This is a whole opening chapter candidate; write the space it needs, without padding to a number or adding the next chapter's visit.

Fixed facts: Cường was found hanged inside his house by Lan Anh, who called help promptly. People currently believe suicide. His son Toàn, daughter-in-law Lan Anh and father cụ Cổn have different outward responses; the customer knows only what they saw/heard. The customer arrived after the discovery and did not observe the death itself. No exact death time, cause determination, perpetrator, historical family truth or objective supernatural law is available to the shop. Stories and online comments are attributed, not narrator-certified explanations.

Use the approved flexible narrator within these information limits. Keep author beat order; dialogue rhythm, scene/summary, paragraph size, local action, incidental names and clear retelling transitions are the writer's composition choices. End within the weight of the reported death and its reception, without a new scene route.
