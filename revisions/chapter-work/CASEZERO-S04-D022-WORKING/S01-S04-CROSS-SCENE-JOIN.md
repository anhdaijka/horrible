## One actual cross-scene continuity defect found and fixed

The ORIGINAL author-approved S01 and later S03 V3 both show bà Lan telling Khanh/Lan Anh that the young shop helper **thought the customer's requested RED stripe was a paint error and wanted to retouch it**. S04 V1 and S04 V2 had inadvertently placed the helper AWAY for the whole mùng10 afternoon. That would make the normal shopkeeper anecdote in the already approved opening false or an implausible fabricated recollection. **S04 V3 now shows the young helper working IN THE INNER ROOM during Thoa's sale, emerging after she leaves, noticing the red stripe and offering to “fix” it.** Bà Lan declines because the customer specifically asked for that mark. This restores every speaker's legitimate knowledge and preserves the original D-018 S01 without rewriting it.

## Style and repetition across seams

- S01's shop pressure is justified by its own paid paper-car deadline and Khanh's real remote work; S04 may briefly return to the same shop but must introduce **mùng10 custom artwork negotiation**, not replay S01's exact paper stock complaint or a second praise paragraph for a competent young customer.
- S02's Mến can be verbose and personally convinced, Hảo skeptical, Nhàn concerned by late delivery. S03 lets the same accounts grow without delivering a narrator moral (“chợ lắm lời” etc.). **S04 should NOT include its own recap of every rumor.**
- S03 itself earns the **FIRST identity confirmation**. S04 may let bà Lan double-check the paper DATE in private while doing her job; it cannot duplicate S03's full conversation with Lan Anh or pre-state the fact that Thoa was objectively dead.
- The stripe ON FINISHED radio is RED; the correction medium ON original paper drawing is WHITE and applied with customer's LEFT hand. Neither is magically appearing at a fresh crime scene.
- **S01 accepted-original vs D-021 optional edited-S01:** the original has a reasonably compact left-handed mùng10 memory which S04 V3 expands; the optional S01 cut removes the detail and is smoother with S04. Since the original was verbatim-author-approved, keep BOTH histories. A later coordinated batch chooses whether to promote optional S01 variant. No unilateral replacement now.
- The S04 V1 closing droplet of white fluid visible four days later was removed in V2 as an unearned repeated clue; no further returning-of-white-fluid coincidence was reinserted in V3.
- Shop helper mùng10 intervention now harmonizes S01, S03 V3 and S04 V3; **Khanh needn't be physically present mùng10** (he returned home less than a week before S01 and may not yet be in town).
