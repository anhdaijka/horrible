## Divergent scene engines

### Engine A — Shop mismatch → public market refusal → domestic coda [PROVISIONAL RECOMMENDATION]

**Life already moving**
- Khanh is home temporarily, helping bà Lan through a busy morning rather than volunteering to investigate.
- Bà Lan has orders, stock, customers and delivery problems.
- Lan Anh is moving through funeral errands under family pressure.
- Chợ Bến supplies witnesses, interruptions and gossip for reasons independent of the mystery.

**Entry**
At the shop, ordinary morning traffic brings/establishes news that Cường died overnight. A practical pass through the order book or finished custom orders exposes the mùng-10 Vũ Văn Cường radio order.

**Middle engine**
Bà Lan carries the finished radio toward Chợ Bến as a practical delivery/clarification task. She encounters Lan Anh buying funeral/offering goods. The public mismatch is now socially costly: name + death + pre-existing order + refusal are witnessed without anyone needing to investigate.

**Exit**

**Strength**
Best fit with D-004 + D-005; gives ordinary life, public consequences and a clean P1→P2 handoff while preserving CH-02.

### Engine B — Record-integrity mystery → market consequence → coda

Khanh's system-minded attention is more central. He checks whether the date/name/order could be clerical error before the market encounter. Stronger cognitive hook, weaker social/lived flow; risk of making Khanh behave like investigator too early.

### Engine C — Mother–son delivery movement → market compression → coda

Very short shop setup, then Khanh accompanies bà Lan through morning commerce toward Chợ Bến. More embodied village movement and social observation; less time with the order book. Strong atmosphere but can weaken the clean logical recognition of the paradox.

## Manifestation discovery candidates

### M1 — Solid-memory shadow [archive-derived transformation]
At bright Chợ Bến, the lightweight hollow paper radio briefly casts a shadow that reads as denser/more complete than its actual construction — closer to a solid old cassette radio than a lattice-and-paper object. The mismatch is brief enough to admit overlap/angle rationalization.

Mechanism inherited only at principle level from archive CH-02's daylight negative-space anomaly; not the old "woman has no shadow" beat.

Pros: daylight horror, object-specific, cinematic, does not identify Thoa as dead.  
Risk: direct supernatural evidence may make P1 too loud.

### M2 — Material tuning [ecology-led]
Ambient market sound makes only the paper radio's foil/paper components vibrate in an ordered sequence suggestive of scanning/tuning despite having no mechanism capable of doing so. No voice, message or exact frequency.

Pros: arises from material construction + market noise; ambiguous source.  
Risk: can become gadget-horror if overexplained.

### M3 — Selective acoustic occlusion [ecology-led coda, PROVISIONAL RECOMMENDATION]
At Cường's busy household, a narrow threshold/zone selectively suppresses human speech from elsewhere in the house while non-human mechanical/material sounds remain audible. Moving out of the zone restores voices immediately.

Pros: uses occupied domestic space; no old call-pattern; supports objective off-POV; horror comes from social separation inside a crowded house.  
Risk: must be staged simply so it reads as impossible pressure rather than abstract sound design.

### M4 — Exact archive rehabilitation
Reuse the old no-shadow or cold-stair mechanism substantially as-is.

Allowed for consideration only because of the author's explicit current request to mine archive material. Not recommended: strongest historical contamination and least discovery-led.
