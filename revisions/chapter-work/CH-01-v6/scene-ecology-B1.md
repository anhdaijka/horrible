# Scene ecology — CH-01-v6

Writer-facing affordance field. This is not a beat sheet.

## Life already in motion

### Family votive shop
- Morning business continues despite village news.
- Stock, custom orders, money, ledger checking, carrying/packing and customer interruptions can compete for attention.
- Khanh is helping because he is home, not because he has chosen an investigation.
- Bà Lan's practical memory of customers/orders can matter while she remains busy with unrelated shop demands.

### Chợ Bến
- People are buying, carrying, loading, talking, waiting, crossing paths and noticing one another.
- Lan Anh has real errands connected to the household's sudden death.
- Public space creates witnesses without anyone intentionally "collecting clues."

### Cường household
- Sudden death does not stop practical movement: people must carry objects, clear paths, move between rooms, coordinate errands and deal with relatives.
- Family pressure and lack of sleep can make ordinary logistics abrasive before the supernatural intrusion.

## Independent wants and pressures

- Khanh may want to finish a task, avoid being pulled into village business, or satisfy a limited practical doubt.
- Bà Lan wants an odd custom order accounted for and her shop responsibilities handled cleanly.
- Lan Anh wants to complete errands and get back without creating more family trouble.
- Toàn wants control over what leaves the household, especially the name Thoa, while maintaining outward functionality.
- Market people may want gossip, business, speed, amusement or simply to understand the commotion.

## Available collisions

The writer may select/transform/omit:
- a customer interrupts the death story for a purchase;
- two versions of the death report differ in small ways;
- somebody treats the order mismatch as a prank or revenge rather than ghostly;
- a practical shop question forces the full recipient/purchaser names into speech;
- Chợ Bến attention grows unevenly rather than all at once;
- Lan Anh is pressured by being watched more than by the object itself;
- a mundane market errand becomes harder because she wants to leave quickly;
- household people keep using a route until the physical obstruction makes avoidance practical.

## Horror intrusion

### Oral body horror
The death is first made concrete through spoken description. One strong grotesque image is enough. The teller's words can be vivid; narration does not certify them.

### Temporal horror
The ledger and finished radio do not need to behave supernaturally. Their dates/names become frightening because the death report came first.

### Social horror
The strange order becomes public property through witnesses and retelling. Gossip can distort while the ledger remains fixed.

### Domestic physical haunting
At the household, apparently empty space near Cường's death site exerts human-sized physical resistance on an object being moved through it. Shared practical consequence matters more than explanation.

## Reader-detective affordances

Available to notice:
- differences between rumor and fixed record;
- the exact temporal contradiction;
- Lan Anh's ignorance versus Toàn's recognition;
- public witnesses sufficient for later rumor spread;
- the coda's physical effect without visible body.

Do not turn every shop detail, market face or household object into evidence.

## Local invention license

Transient customers, market vendors, errands, minor price/stock problems, temporary moods, ordinary household objects and local misunderstandings may be invented when consistent with current people/place.

Do not invent:
- new family history;
- new knowledge about Thoa;
- ritual law;
- new forensic findings;
- new durable object provenance;
- new cross-chapter witness obligations without recording them.
