# Scene ecology — CH-01-v6

> Affordance field only. Do not treat these possibilities as required beats or restate them as dialogue.

## Life already in motion

### Family shop
Morning business is already underway: packing, custom orders, cash, ledger use, customers calling from the doorway, unfinished breakfast, carrying and small errands. Khanh is home and available to be absorbed into shop work without having chosen an investigation.

### Chợ Bến
People are buying, carrying, waiting, bargaining, crossing paths and only half-listening to one another. Lan Anh has urgent errands of her own. Public space creates witnesses without anyone intentionally collecting evidence.

### Cường household
Sudden death has not stopped logistics. People still need to move furniture/objects, clear routes, fetch things, receive relatives and keep the house functioning.

## Independent wants and pressures

- Khanh may want food, quiet, to finish a task, avoid village business, joke with his mother or accept a mundane explanation.
- Bà Lan may want to keep customers moving, settle an odd order, remember who owes what, finish packing or prevent shop confusion.
- Lan Anh may prioritize errands and reducing further family trouble.
- Toàn may prioritize keeping the household functional while containing what he fears may surface.
- Bystanders may be busy, nosy, helpful, dismissive, competitive storytellers or only partly interested.

None require a particular line, gesture or emotional display.

## Material / social affordances

Optional:
- customers continue buying while a death story is being told;
- one person tells a longer version while someone else corrects, interrupts or remembers a different detail;
- an ordinary shop task makes the recipient tag/order ledger visible without formal investigation;
- bà Lan and Khanh may talk around the order while continuing another task rather than stopping for a data-exchange scene;
- market people overhear fragments and later retell them inaccurately;
- household movement continues until the physical obstruction makes rerouting simpler than arguing.

Select, transform or omit.

## Narrative flow affordances

### Shop
The shop can support sustained storytelling rather than shot-by-shot blocking:
- a customer's gossip may run for several sentences with digressions, correction and practical interruption;
- bà Lan can work continuously through a conversation instead of receiving an action beat after each line;
- Khanh's attention can move associatively between what he hears, what he knows of Cường, hunger/sleepiness, the object in his hands and the ledger;
- routine morning activity may be summarized across minutes instead of dramatized customer-by-customer;
- one paragraph may carry several linked physical actions if they belong to one flow of work.

### Market
Crowd speech may be partially summarized. Not every bystander needs a line. The narration may stay with how a story mutates socially rather than transcribing every turn.

### Household
Objective coda may group activity and time without becoming camera directions. The narration can sustain the practical problem of moving through the house across a paragraph before isolating the impossible resistance.

## Horror field

- Community speech can give the death a grotesque physical image while remaining unreliable.
- The ledger/radio can become frightening without behaving supernaturally.
- The market can turn a private anomaly into public rumor through ordinary social traffic.
- Near Cường's death site, apparently empty human-sized space can exert repeatable physical resistance on something being moved through it.

No named craft technique or scare cadence is required.

## Reader-detective affordances

The ecology permits contrast between mutable speech and fixed record, between what Lan Anh does not know and what Toàn appears to know, and between visible emptiness and practical physical resistance.

The writer does not need to stage every contrast explicitly.

## Local invention license

Transient customers, vendors, errands, work problems, minor misunderstandings, temporary moods and ordinary household objects are free when consistent with the setting. Record only inventions that create a later durable obligation.
