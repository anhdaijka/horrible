## Core disturbance and reader experience

On the morning after Cường's sudden death, mutable village talk collides with a stable shop record: a custom paper radio for him was ordered on mùng 10 while he was still alive.

The chapter should leave the reader with dread around three facts: the death already has an ugly physical shape in community speech; the order predates it; the name **Vũ Thị Thoa** means more to Toàn than to the people around him.

## Hard story boundary

- Presentation remains on morning 14.
- The mùng-10 shop transaction belongs to CH-02 and is not replayed in full here.
- The order record carries: recipient Vũ Văn Cường / xóm Trại, custom paper radio, purchaser Vũ Thị Thoa, order date mùng 10.
- The public Chợ Bến encounter remains the source event from which later community gossip can plausibly spread.
- Lan Anh does not know Thoa's buried 1996 truth.
- By chapter end, the reader can observe that Toàn recognizes the name as dangerous without learning why.
- After Lan Anh refuses the radio, it returns to the family votive shop's custody.
- The chapter closes in Cường's household with the approved physical haunting: apparently empty human-sized space near the death site can obstruct/deflect a mundane object strongly enough to alter practical movement.
- Do not reveal that Thoa is dead, the supernatural killer, or the objective truth of the mùng-10 purchaser.

These are end-state/reveal boundaries. They do not prescribe exact dialogue, reactions or micro-order inside the approved shop → Chợ Bến → household presentation.

## Reader-detective field

Keep fairly available:
- oral accounts of the death are testimony and may vary;
- the written date/name/object record is stable;
- the public market event has witnesses;
- Lan Anh's knowledge and Toàn's knowledge are not the same;
- the final obstruction has a shared practical effect.

Do not explain the intended deduction.

## Dramatic space

The writer owns how people reach these outcomes: interruptions, indirect speech, misunderstanding, ordinary work, silence, humor, embarrassment, delays, local bystanders, practical decisions and the exact conversational route.

Khanh need not interrogate anyone or verbalize every discrepancy.

## Concrete reality

- Bà Lan operates the family votive-paper shop and keeps an order ledger.
- Khanh is temporarily home and may help casually.
- Lan Anh is under immediate household/funeral pressure.
- A vivid description of Cường's body/neck may appear only as attributed hearsay/testimony, not narrator-certified forensics.
- Do not imply that family funeral preparation overrides required professional handling of the body.

No concrete ritual taboo or universal hàng-mã rule is required.

## Execution freedom

Open: exact death-news teller, shop task, vernacular phrasing, dialogue length, who interrupts whom, minor market business, transient bystanders, incidental humor/irritation, mundane object used in the coda, silence and narrative distance.

Packet facts are not required spoken lines.

Record after drafting only new durable promises, established prior events, custody changes, future meetings, distinctive object facts or durable knowledge.

## Deliberately unanswered

- How accurate the body rumor is.
- Why Toàn knows the name Thoa matters.
- The objective identity/mechanism behind the mùng-10 order.
- The mechanism of the household obstruction.
- What theory Khanh settles on, if any.
