# Workflow audit — CH-01-v6 B10 reveal/consequence continuity

Date: 2026-09-22

## Trigger

The B9 bounded raw trial materially improved sustained narrative movement, conversational floor continuity, register accuracy and persistent horror residue. The author nevertheless remained dissatisfied and requested an independent audit before further prose.

The trial remains chat-only, non-candidate diagnostic prose. It is not imported here as a style source or reusable story example. This audit records only mechanism-level findings needed to repair the workflow.

## Result

**FOUR ROOT COMPOSITION DEFECTS CONFIRMED.**

B9 solved the earlier reset/transaction problem but still allowed the Writer to make the underlying plot architecture visible through smoother prose.

### 1. Reveal spotlighting

Hard facts could still become successive scene centers: an important fact arrives, ordinary motion yields, a question follows, the next fact appears, then the prose gives the reader a small interpretive landing. Sustained movement remains intact at macro scale, yet revelation can still form a hidden staircase underneath it.

Root implication: the compiler and Writer need **reveal distribution**. Fair facts must remain available without being guaranteed solitary dramatic emphasis. Availability, character attention and reader significance may occur at different times.

### 2. Interpretive mediation

Persistent-dread instructions can be satisfied by narrator or close-POV sentences explaining why an ordinary thing now feels wrong. That transfers packet-level reader-pressure reasoning directly into prose and reduces reader inference.

Root implication: close-third interiority must remain lived cognition rather than author commentary on intended effect. Concrete consequence should be trusted when it already carries the shift.

### 3. Consequence under-realization

B9 correctly named changed meaning, custody and practical consequence, but generation could still carry most dread as remembered unease while the material/social world remained largely unchanged.

Root implication: packet compilation must provide **consequence substrate**—ordinary handling, custody, placement, route, etiquette, money, work or spatial pressures through which wrongness can persist without another manifestation.

### 4. Conversational payoff smoothing

B8/B9 fixed short adjacency-pair dialogue, but a second-order artifact remained possible: familiar characters sustain longer talk by producing too many successful jokes, comebacks and mutually legible returns. The floor is continuous but suspiciously entertaining and complete.

Root implication: conversational-floor conditioning must include **roughness**. Jokes may miss; bids may die; complaints may receive useless answers; topics may dissolve without payoff.

## Non-regression firewall

The B10 patch must preserve:

1. sustained narrative movement as the primary generative unit;
2. conversational floor continuity without turn-length quotas;
3. human causality / anti-investigator counterfactual;
4. ecology de-beating;
5. ordinary-life richness and POV associativity;
6. kinship/address/register integrity;
7. B9 persistent dread residue without extra manifestations, scare cadence or fear quotas;
8. reader-detective architecture in the reader, not converted into protagonist investigation.

A repair that hides the reveal ladder by making facts obscure or unfair also fails. A repair that removes interiority, humor or long dialogue also fails.

## Root patch

### Preflight / packet compiler

- Add reveal-distribution integrity: compile fact availability without implicit `clue → pause → question → next clue` choreography.
- Add consequence substrate: horror carriers receive ordinary practical/social/material pressures capable of keeping wrongness active.
- Add conversational roughness alongside floor continuity: continuity is not polished repartee.
- READY fails when horror depends mainly on interpretive explanation or when clue order is silently encoded as dramatic staging.

### Writer

- Important facts may remain embedded in ongoing work, partially attended or understood later.
- Reader significance may precede POV interpretation.
- Close-third interiority may rationalize, dismiss, associate or stop unfinished; it should not paraphrase packet reader-pressure.
- Prefer consequence-borne dread through custody/handling/work/social/spatial effects before narrator explanation.
- Allow failed jokes, ignored bids, useless acknowledgments and conversational dead ends without breaking floor continuity.

## B10 acceptance test

A fresh bounded trial fails if it:

- reconstructs a clean death → radio → ledger → date → purchaser reveal staircase even with smooth prose;
- repeatedly tells the reader why normality is now frightening;
- keeps dread mostly inside Khanh's stated unease while the radio/order create no practical residue;
- makes mother-son familiarity read as continuous successful banter;
- repairs any of the above by adding investigation, extra anomalies, ominous diction, reaction beats or dead ordinary-life filler.

It demonstrates the intended repair when:

- ordinary shop processes have local purposes beyond clue delivery;
- important facts can be fairly present without monopolizing attention;
- practical handling/custody/social consequence carries some of the horror pressure on its own;
- the reader can connect more than Khanh explicitly explains;
- conversation remains sustained but socially imperfect;
- B7/B8/B9 gains remain intact.
