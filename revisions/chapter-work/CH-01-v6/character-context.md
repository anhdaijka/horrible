# Character context — CH-01-v6

> Writer-safe relationship/knowledge context. Knowledge is not a required line, question, reaction or visible proof.

## Relationships and address

### Khanh
27, son of bà Lan. Temporarily home after several years of Japan-based software work; ordinary mother-son shorthand with bà Lan. Lan Anh and Toàn are village acquaintances; Toàn is older.

### Bà Lan
55, Khanh's mother and primary operator of the family votive-paper shop. Practical, socially observant, strong memory for customers/orders. Not a supernatural authority.

### Lan Anh
27, Toàn's wife and Cường's daughter-in-law. Under immediate household/funeral pressure.

### Toàn
30, Cường's son and Lan Anh's husband. Dump-truck driver; under severe private pressure.

## People beyond the plot

Khanh has a life/work routine outside the village and does not automatically treat strange events as his case.

Bà Lan has a shop to run; business can continue while disturbing information enters.

Lan Anh's day is crowded by sudden death and errands. She is not present to serve as a mystery witness.

Toàn is grieving/managing household pressure as well as hiding dangerous knowledge. He need not behave suspiciously in every moment.

## Agency outside the case

Khanh may help, postpone curiosity, joke, get annoyed, accept an ordinary explanation temporarily or decide something is not his business.

Bà Lan may prioritize a customer, money, an order or a practical errand over discussing the mystery.

Lan Anh may leave, refuse an object, answer minimally, redirect attention or focus on getting home.

Toàn may evade, lie, shut down a topic, keep working, redirect his wife or avoid explanation.

These are affordances, not required behaviors.

## Knowledge and viewpoint

### Khanh — main POV at shop/market
He does not know the 1996 truth, that Thoa is dead, Toàn's night-13 scuffle, the clean-nails anomaly or the supernatural killer truth.

He may learn the death report and the order facts in CH-01. He does not need to articulate every inference.

### Bà Lan
She personally handled the mùng-10 transaction. CH-01 may use only what the present practical situation requires; CH-02 owns the full experiential replay.

### Lan Anh
She knows Cường is dead and knows the immediate household crisis available to her. She does **not** know Thoa's actual fate or the buried 1996 truth.

Not knowing Thoa does not require her to ask who Thoa is, and it does not require any particular visible reaction.

### Toàn
He knows the family secret that makes the name **Vũ Thị Thoa** dangerous to him. He does not know that Thoa supernaturally killed Cường.

Because the household coda is objective/off-POV, his underlying reason must remain unspoken/unknown to the reader. Recognition may emerge through whatever outward behavior feels natural; no fixed line or reaction is required.
