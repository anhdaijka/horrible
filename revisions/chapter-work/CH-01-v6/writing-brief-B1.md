## Core disturbance and reader experience

A sudden death enters an ordinary morning first as a disturbing spoken account, then becomes harder to dismiss when the family's shop ledger shows that a custom paper radio for Vũ Văn Cường was ordered on mùng 10 while he was still alive.

The chapter should move the reader through:
**physical dread → temporal impossibility → public unease → hidden-family pressure → direct domestic supernatural consequence.**

By the end, the question is not only who ordered the object, but why the name **Vũ Thị Thoa** makes Toàn try to suppress it and why Cường's empty death-site behaves as though a human body still occupies space there.

## Story truth and reveal edge

- Morning 14 only.
- Cường died during night 13.
- The shop order was placed on mùng 10 for recipient Vũ Văn Cường, xóm Trại, by purchaser name Vũ Thị Thoa.
- The finished custom paper radio exists by morning 14.
- Full mùng-10 transaction belongs to CH-02; do not replay it here.
- Bà Lan/Khanh take the radio into the Chợ Bến social field; Lan Anh refuses it publicly.
- Lan Anh is alarmed by the impossible pre-death order, not by hidden knowledge of Thoa.
- Later at home, Lan Anh reports the purchaser name. Toàn reacts disproportionately and forbids her to repeat it to outsiders. Do not explain why.
- After the public refusal, the radio returns to the family votive shop's custody.
- Final coda is objective/off-POV at Cường's household.
- Near the place Cường was found, a mundane object being moved through an apparently clear route encounters/deflects around an invisible human-sized resistance; more than one person experiences the physical obstruction; practical movement is rerouted.
- Do not identify Thoa as dead, the supernatural killer, or the objective purchaser truth.

## Reader-detective field

Keep fairly observable:
- the spoken death report is attributed to a person/community source;
- order date mùng 10;
- recipient full name / xóm Trại;
- purchaser name Vũ Thị Thoa;
- Cường was alive when the order was placed;
- Lan Anh's public refusal;
- witnesses around Chợ Bến;
- Toàn's specific reaction to the name;
- the final physical obstruction.

The reader may connect these more aggressively than Khanh does. Khanh does not need to state the correct theory.

## Dramatic space

The chapter may breathe through:
- real shop work and interruptions;
- local customers carrying news for reasons unrelated to investigation;
- bà Lan deciding what practical responsibility the shop has for an undelivered custom order;
- Khanh balancing curiosity with the fact that this is somebody else's family crisis;
- Chợ Bến traffic, witnesses and embarrassment;
- household tasks continuing despite death.

Do not convert these into mandatory beats beyond the locked outcome above.

## Concrete reality and safe sources

Safe practical ground:
- bà Lan runs a family votive-paper shop and keeps a shop ledger;
- Khanh is temporarily home after Japan-based software work and helps casually;
- Lan Anh is Cường's daughter-in-law and is handling funeral-related errands;
- funeral preparation may begin, but prose must not imply family preference overrides required professional handling of the body.

The oral description of Cường's corpse may be vivid, but it remains hearsay/testimony. Do not turn it into a medical finding.

No concrete ritual taboo, "điểm nhãn" rule, disposal rule or universal vàng-mã belief is required.

## Execution freedom

Open:
- exact local teller of the death news;
- exact shop task under way;
- exact vernacular phrasing of the body account;
- how Khanh/bà Lan discover the order in the course of work;
- route and incidental activity toward/at Chợ Bến;
- market bystanders and local interruptions;
- dialogue tactics, silence, humor, embarrassment, minor mistakes;
- mundane object involved in the coda obstruction;
- micro-order and sensory choices inside the approved progression.

Keep any new durable fact, relationship history, future promise, custody change or distinctive object fact out of silent invention; record it after drafting.

## Deliberately unanswered

- Whether the oral corpse description is perfectly accurate.
- Who/what the purchaser objectively was.
- Why Toàn knows the name Thoa matters.
- What mechanism causes the coda obstruction.
- What theory Khanh settles on, if any, before the chapter ends.
