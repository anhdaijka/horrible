# Workflow audit — CH-01-v6 B9 persistent dread pressure

Date: 2026-09-22

## Scope

Triggered by author rereading of the B7 bounded raw trial before running B8. The prose had become materially more lived and natural, but the opening still read too easily as an ordinary realist/mystery novel with a strange fact rather than as ghost-horror exerting sustained pressure.

This audit inspects only the route:

`Preflight horror discovery → Writer packet compiler → Writer horror prior`

It does not reopen Q1–Q8, redesign E1, add manifestations, alter chronology, change clue/reveal allocation, modify character knowledge or touch canon/continuity.

## Result

**HORROR-PRESSURE COMPRESSION DEFECT CONFIRMED.**

The workflow was asymmetric.

The ordinary-life path had high generative resolution:
- lived motion;
- human-causality counterfactual;
- anti-investigator audit;
- ecology de-beating;
- concrete material processes;
- sustained narrative movement;
- conversational floor continuity;
- relationship/register substrate.

The horror path had high design scrutiny during discovery, but after convergence it was compressed mostly into:
- a named manifestation/contact surface;
- a broad reader-pressure statement;
- the Writer instruction to let horror "deform" an established movement.

That is sufficient to prevent bad horror staging, but not sufficient to generate strong horror. The Writer could honor every rule, present the approved anomaly correctly, then allow ordinary activity to restore the previous neutral baseline. The result is mystery information embedded in life rather than dread contaminating life.

## Root distinction

The missing unit is **persistent dread residue**.

An approved horror fact or manifestation should be able to change the status of an ordinary carrier—object, obligation, route, social meaning, testimony, space, expectation—so that the wrongness remains reader-visible after immediate attention moves elsewhere.

This is not:
- another manifestation;
- a callback quota;
- ominous atmosphere;
- character fear;
- a sequence of escalating scares.

Character attention and reader pressure may diverge. A person can return to work while an object, obligation or route remains harder for the reader to place back into its former category.

## Non-regression firewall

Every future horror-conditioning patch must preserve these invariants:

1. **Sustained narrative movement remains the first-pass generative unit.** Horror may deform a movement; it may not replace movement with scare beats.
2. **Conversational floor continuity remains active.** Anomaly contact, reaction or topic change does not automatically reset social initiative.
3. **Human causality / anti-investigator remains a hard gate.** Horror intensity cannot justify unmotivated questions, lookups, visits or confrontations.
4. **Ecology remains de-beated.** Packet files may describe pressure carriers and consequences, not `sign 1 → reaction → sign 2`.
5. **Ordinary life remains positive generative material.** The fix is not to remove commerce, food, humor, errands or dead time.
6. **Kinship/address/register accuracy remains independent and binding.**
7. **No horror proxy metrics.** No anomaly count, scare cadence, reaction quota, sentence-shortening rule, ominous-adjective quota or mandatory bodily fear symptoms.
8. **No extra manifestations for tone.** First use changed meaning/consequence of approved material.

A proposed patch that strengthens genre tone by violating any invariant above is rejected.

## Root patch

### Preflight discovery

Manifestation candidates are now tested for **pressure propagation**: can their wrongness keep deforming existing work, obligation, space, social meaning or reader expectation after first contact, without another supernatural event?

### Packet compiler

The compiler now exports **dread continuity** as positive generative information:

- `writing-brief.md / Reader pressure` states the dread vector: what ordinary confidence becomes less trustworthy and what pressure should remain after attention moves on.
- `scene-ecology.md / Horror pressure field` states the approved contact surface plus the ordinary carriers and practical/social/material residue through which wrongness can persist.

This remains field-level. It must not encode scare order or character reactions.

### Writer

The Writer now treats dread as residue carried inside sustained movement. Ordinary activity may resume without resetting the reader's baseline. Changed meaning and practical consequence are preferred to additional effects.

## CH-01-v6 B9 recompile

B9 keeps the exact approved E1 design and B8 relationship/dialogue improvements.

No new supernatural event is introduced.

The existing opening pressure is recompiled so that:
- Cường's body report remains fallible hearsay;
- the finished radio + ledger belong to ordinary shop reality and therefore have stronger material persistence;
- the radio remains completely non-performing;
- shop work can continue without neutralizing the temporal/ownership mismatch;
- the unresolved object can be carried toward Chợ Bến by ordinary errands while retaining reader-facing pressure.

B8 remains frozen and was intentionally not trialed after this root defect was identified.

## Acceptance test for B9

The next raw trial should be rejected if horror is achieved by:
- extra anomaly events;
- conspicuous spooky diction;
- forced fear reactions;
- investigative questioning;
- short scare beats;
- erosion of shop life or dialogue continuity.

It should demonstrate instead that:
- ordinary work remains convincing;
- the death report, order and radio alter the reader's baseline rather than merely deliver information;
- pressure survives interruptions and topic drift;
- the radio remains mundane in behavior but increasingly difficult to place socially/temporally;
- no movement is reset merely because characters resume normal activity;
- B7 sustained-movement gains and B8 floor/register gains remain intact.

## Canon/design impact

**CLEAN.**

No E1 engine, chronology, POV, relationship, supernatural mechanism, manifestation, knowledge boundary, reveal, object custody or durable state changes.
