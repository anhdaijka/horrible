# Scene ecology — CH-01-v6

> Affordance field only. Do not treat these possibilities as required beats or restate them as dialogue.

## Life already in motion

### Family shop
Morning business is active: packing, ledger use, custom orders, money, customers, carrying and small interruptions. Khanh is home and available to be pulled into work without having chosen an investigation.

### Chợ Bến
People are buying, carrying, waiting, bargaining, crossing paths and listening selectively. Lan Anh has her own urgent errands. Public space creates witnesses without anyone needing to collect evidence.

### Cường household
Sudden death has not stopped logistics. People still need to move furniture/objects, clear routes, fetch things, deal with relatives and keep the house functioning.

## Independent wants and pressures

- Khanh may prefer to finish a task, avoid village business or accept a mundane explanation.
- Bà Lan may prioritize shop responsibility and practical resolution over speculation.
- Lan Anh may prioritize finishing errands and reducing further family trouble.
- Toàn may prioritize controlling household fallout while remaining functional.
- Bystanders may be busy, nosy, helpful, dismissive or only half-listening.

None of these require a particular line, gesture or emotional display.

## Material / social affordances

Optional possibilities:
- customers interrupt serious talk with ordinary purchases;
- versions of the death story differ;
- market people overhear fragments and complete the story wrongly;
- practical work forces names/dates into view without anyone formally interrogating;
- social attention rises and falls unevenly;
- household movement continues until the physical obstruction makes rerouting simpler than arguing about it.

Select, transform or omit.

## Horror field

- Community speech can give the death a grotesque physical image while remaining unreliable.
- The ledger/radio can become frightening without behaving supernaturally.
- The market can turn a private anomaly into public rumor through ordinary social traffic.
- Near Cường's death site, apparently empty human-sized space can exert repeatable physical resistance on something being moved through it.

No named craft technique or scare cadence is required.

## Reader-detective affordances

The ecology permits contrast between mutable speech and fixed record, between what Lan Anh does not know and what Toàn appears to know, and between visible emptiness and practical physical resistance.

The writer does not need to stage every contrast explicitly.

## Local invention license

Transient customers, vendors, errands, work problems, minor misunderstandings, temporary moods and ordinary household objects are free when consistent with the setting. Record only inventions that create a later durable obligation.
