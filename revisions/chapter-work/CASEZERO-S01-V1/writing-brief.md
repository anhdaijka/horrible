# Case Zero S01 — Writing brief (candidate raw exploratory pilot)

**Prepared under D-011 Treatment V1. Not an author-frozen A/B run, not canon prose.** The same bounded scene must be used in a future strict rich/fresh comparison. This brief is a safe writer projection; avoid opening full plot, approvals/audits or rejected prose during a later clean Writer run.

Write a single sustained Vietnamese prose scene, about 1,700–2,300 words where the scene naturally supports it, in close-third **bà Trần Thị Lan** POV. Time: mùng14 morning, after Cường has died night mùng13; DO NOT name a Gregorian month/date until author locks calendar. Place: her working votive-paper shop near fictional Chợ Bến in contemporary Kinh Bắc.

**Beginning:** bà Lan and son Khanh are working through ordinary customers' orders. She is trying to finish/pack real paid products while a routine customer/supplier interruption costs time. Khanh, 27, returned from Japan, is helping at home but an actual Japan-facing software-team handover competes for his attention. Family bickering and ordinary shop obligation should be rich and specific without explaining rural custom as an encyclopedia entry.

**Disturbance:** death news about ông Vũ Văn Cường of xóm Trại reaches the shop via an ordinary local customer/trader who plausibly heard the news that morning; speaker has NO official forensic access and no certain supernatural explanation. Bà Lan already has a **paid mùng10 order from a woman who called herself Thoa**, for a **paper replica** of a red Czech cassette radio destined for then-alive Cường. Woman exhibited easy **left-hand** action while handling the order/sketch/pen; bà Lan may naturally remember a detail of custom paint or repair she specified, but exact mark is not yet finalized. bà Lan had already identified the customer-specified recipient as ông Cường xóm Trại if the register says so; don't make her discover his identity in the morning as new information.

**Ordinary change of plan:** The radio is finished and physically stays at the shop until bà Lan/Khanh agree to attempt normal daylight delivery via Chợ Bến. The reader can notice the mismatch of paid order mùng10 and Cường death night mùng13. Bà Lan can consider a mundane spite prank or confusion without declaring a ghost. Khanh's interest can stay reluctant. Their mother–adult-son trust/irritation drives the decision, not a detective speech. Scene ends **before** the public Chợ Bến meeting/refusal by Lan Anh, before Long visits and before a ghost appears. This is only S01, NOT the full opening chapter.

**Do not reveal:** Thoa objectively died in 1996; Thoa is a spirit, murdered Cường, or exact medical description of Cường death; father/son 2026 fight; Thủy threats/alibi; Bình tống tiền. No mùng10 full flashback: that gets separate S04 in bà Lan POV. No foreshadowing by absurd "no shadow" or magical customer message. Preserve temporal order mùng10 order before death mùng13, radio ready mùng14. No 1996 cassette recording.

**Ending state:** mother and son have an actual work/business decision about the newly ominous paid custom order; shop custody preserved, both will approach Chợ Bến by ordinary route; no meeting yet. No cheap supernatural final sting.

**Craft focus:** one continuous shop morning with interruptions and residue, naturally varying sentence/dialogue length, imperfect family conversation, gossip arriving incidentally rather than narration pausing at each clue. Maintain close bà Lan interior; do not explain horror significance after objects/obligations have already made it felt.
