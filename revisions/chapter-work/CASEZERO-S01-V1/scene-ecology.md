# Case Zero S01 — Safe lived-world scene ecology (exploratory packet)

**Only ordinary supports, not a plotted beat-by-beat screenplay.** Late-morning working shop near Chợ Bến; space is cramped enough that large finished paper offerings cannot easily be shifted without disrupting nearby work. Bà Lan has competent, practiced shop movements with dry hands, short nails and bundled hair. Ordinary inventory might include dyed sheets, cardboard frames, paste/glue, scissors, binding and wrapping materials, paper offerings needing to dry before packing. Choose *only a few* processes with physical continuity; avoid improbable craft jargon or teaching readers a ritual dictionary. Radio paper replica has **already been assembled after the mùng10 order** and is physically in the shop, not yet delivered.

Two simultaneous mundane pressures: (i) a current paying customer's small deadline/measurement/packaging snag; (ii) Khanh's remote engineering handover/meeting deadline that mother does not necessarily value or understand. These pressures can continue during news and the radio decision; people may talk over unfinished work and fail to acknowledge every remark. A known local customer/trader bringing news plausibly overheard that ông Cường suddenly died and the household is preparing for funeral; they cannot know medical/official findings or confirm paranormal causes.

**Social routing:** The regular shop has a practical trade route through Chợ Bến. Bà Lan and Khanh have a reason to visit/cross it when attempting to contact the Cường family, not a contrived detour solely to produce public witnesses. Exact form of travel and physical size of radio should remain ordinary and consistent. The public refusal is a different subsequent scene; the object has to return to shop afterward.

**Mùng10 order record:** recipient already written as ông Cường xóm Trại; purchaser called herself Thoa, provided instructions for red Czech cassette-replica shape and a personally modified feature, and used left hand fluidly. Bà Lan remembers the transaction like a competent merchant with a face/order to recall, not a psychic. If customer likeness to family is invoked, keep it an impression/ordinary observation not proof. Do not invent payment fraud, a second Thoa or an object that magically changes shape.

**Bà Lan's line of sight:** what entered shop, what Khanh and visitor said/heard, what she recalled, where objects actually are. No access to Toàn or Lan Anh interior, Toàn's hidden fight, official examination or reader-author ghost truth. Feeling uneasy is not supernatural evidence. Night ghost-haunting of Cường belongs to the family's house later; no manifestation in S01.

**Physical and social continuity checks:** set down scissors before handling a large fragile object; if radio gets wrapped/shifted, track who holds it and whether wet glue would ruin packing; if Khanh steps away for a work call, he has to return/communicate for their final joint delivery decision. Do not let the finished radio vanish from the work counter between paragraphs, or give bà Lan enough free time for a historical exposition monologue while customers queue.
