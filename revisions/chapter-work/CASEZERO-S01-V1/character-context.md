# Case Zero S01 — Safe character and social relationships

**Only relations and human traits necessary for S01.** Never load author-level Thoa murder truth into the S01 Writer payload.

**Bà Trần Thị Lan:** 55; mother of 27-year-old Khanh; married to Nguyễn Văn Hùng, adult daughter Hạnh. Operates family votive shop and has a strong but fallible memory for orders, faces and village relationships. Quick, practical and sociable; possible belief in ghosts does not make her a psychic. She prioritizes packing/delivery/customer promises while privately registering oddness of the mùng10 commission. A competent customer-specific question at ordering time is ordinary: recipient, model, when needed; no need to demand ritual authorization. She may recognize Cường xóm Trại from the start, which must remain consistent on mùng14. Typical reference: bà Lan, mother when Khanh speaks. Tone contextual, not a dialect caricature.

**Nguyễn Minh Khanh:** 27; adult son of bà Lan, practical Japan-facing software engineer returned from work in Japan for a short break. He helps her at the shop when free and still has remote team tasks. Around mother he can be sleepy, joking, irritated or grudgingly helpful; should not be the smartest person in every line or switch instantly into homicide investigator over a weird order. His relationship with his mother is actual kinship: she calls him Khanh/con/mày when emotionally appropriate; he says mẹ/con in familiar ordinary register. Don't invent an unrelated age/gender kinship label or overuse dialect catchphrases.

**The local news-bearer:** a normal trader/customer who plausibly passed the household or heard news from a relative, while continuing their own errand. May be an unnamed established customer rather than a newly important witness. Their account is ordinary circulating information: Cường died suddenly overnight, funeral preparations are being arranged; they do NOT know the medical cause or see a ghost.

**The mùng10 purchaser remembered by bà Lan:** a woman who gives name Thoa and the correct ordinary order details, including left-hand fluency and a red Czech cassette-radio personal detail. Bà Lan has no grounds to know the visitor was the objectively real spirit Thoa. The purchaser need not act cryptic or speak in riddles. A complete mùng10 flashback is reserved for a later scene. Her birth, 1996 death and identity are NOT described in authorial voice in S01.

**Lan Anh:** eldest daughter-in-law in Cường household, married to Toàn, knows bà Lan through trade. She has genuine grief/funeral errands and will publicly refuse paper radio at Chợ Bến in a FOLLOWING scene; she does not appear in this bounded S01 or speak through a suspicious phone call that would short-circuit public refusal.

**Officer Long:** not present, not tipped directly by family; he later learns of public market event through independent gossip. **Mai:** childhood platonic friend of Khanh, not an implied romance in this scene.
