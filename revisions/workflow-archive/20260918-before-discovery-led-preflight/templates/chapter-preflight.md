## Consequence-bearing hinges and dependencies
Only where the chapter depends on them: perception → available knowledge/inference → choice → consequence. Include a credible counter-reading when it could change durable knowledge or later behavior. Identify causes presented in later chapters that must already be understood, without drafting those chapters early.

For each proposed hinge, state why competing readings would materially change knowledge, obligation or durable consequence. If an ordinary source-grounded baseline is enough and no consequence changes, remove it from this section and leave the implementation unlocked.

Classify gaps as SOURCE-RESOLVABLE, EXECUTION or AUTHOR-DECISION. A material unresolved hinge means AWAITING_AUTHOR, not READY.
