# Quality and readiness

1. Input sufficiency: approval scope is explicit; verified boundaries, relationships and practical context support consequence-bearing causal hinges; no fixed fact quota.
2. Draft handoff: generative pass completed before QA context; prose then self-edited against the separate qa-card; locator-only identity, separate handoff/deltas and prior versions preserved.
3. Literary review: locator + full prose first, then contextual diagnosis under docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md. No score or technique quota; COMPROMISED cannot issue independent PASS.
4. Safety: verified facts, relationships, knowledge/reveals, incompatible later state/obligations and cultural/professional claims; no required visible reaction; shared issues reach the editor.
5. Revision: execution may be rebuilt within approved design even when a requested review lane is pending; missing lanes are recorded, and renewed reviews remain required.
6. Acceptance: matching candidate bytes and consequential authority basis, independent literary PASS, safety PASS, explicit acceptance and approved durable-state diff.
7. Promotion: prose only, continuity/registry updates, relevant validation.

Structural checks do not certify literary improvement. `npm run workflow:check` verifies contract wiring and bootstrap preservation only. Paired manual/model cases live in evals/horrible-workflow-regressions.md. A scene trial is useful after repeated failure but is not a mandatory extra gate on all chapters.
