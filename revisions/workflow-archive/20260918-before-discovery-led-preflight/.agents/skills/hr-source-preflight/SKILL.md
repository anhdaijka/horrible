# Horrible preflight

Read .agents/rules/03-planning.md and the preparation section of docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md. Before producing the packet, separate decisions the author actually approved from planner readiness. Identify only the consequence-bearing hinges the chapter depends on and test perception → inference → choice → consequence, including a cause dramatized in a later chapter. Use a credible counter-reading where it could change durable knowledge or behavior. Do not turn routine occupational or family behavior into a hinge just because it is later recontextualized; direct author experience may establish a family-specific baseline, and immaterial implementation belongs to the writer. Resolve source-answerable gaps yourself; when a new material choice remains, offer pressure-tested options in design-discussion.md, mark AWAITING_AUTHOR and stop before the writer packet.

Once resolved, use the current sources and templates to produce preflight.md, generative writing-brief.md, character-context.md and a separate qa-card.md. Project exact spoiler-safe hard boundaries into the generative files, but keep causal tests, regression history, safety checks and authority verification in qa-card.md for the later self-edit phase. Character context should carry relationships, shared history, ordinary pressures and contradictions—not performance tics, body-language menus or sample lines. Do not point the writer at a section that also contains future solutions. Record the evidence for READY, the consequential authority basis and actual next action in session-state.
