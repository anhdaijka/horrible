# Horrible safety

Read candidate.md, candidate prose, qa-card.md, consequence-bearing deltas and relevant authority sections. Use templates/safety-review-report.md. Verify chronology, knowledge, reveal, kinship/address, durable state, clue limits and real-world claims. At a material dependency, flag only an observable contradiction: impossible knowledge, incompatible later action/state, or a required obligation that disappears. A character may notice without speaking, react privately, postpone, forget an immaterial detail or simply continue routine work. Lack of an on-page question, emotion or visible reaction is not itself a safety defect.

Cite source and prose location for conflicts. If a fact changes the literary exchange, cross-reference it for revision rather than prescribing sentence style. Bind the result to the exact candidate SHA-256 and consequential authority basis; no PASS on unchecked claims, changed prose or an affected changed basis.

Shared operating details: docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md. Load only the section relevant to this role.
