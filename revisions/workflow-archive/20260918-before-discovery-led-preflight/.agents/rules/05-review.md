# Review and repair

Use docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md as a reader-oriented standard, not a scorecard.

Literary review records a blind first reading from prose plus locator-only identity, then a contextual diagnosis using the generative packet and qa-card. Evaluate only dimensions material to the actual reading experience and work's scope. Prioritize root causes over an exhaustive inventory. Classify findings as BLOCKER, MATERIAL_DEFECT or OPTIONAL_EXPERIMENT; an optional preference cannot by itself produce REVISE_REQUIRED. Record concrete protected strengths so revision does not flatten what already works. At a consequential dependency, state a credible competing reading only when it breaks intended knowledge or later behavior. No requirement that every line or scene accomplish every function.

Safety review verifies hard facts and knowledge against authority sources, including kinship and address. It flags impossible knowledge, incompatible later action/state or a dropped material obligation—not the absence of a visible reaction. A fact with a literary consequence is shared between lanes: flag the contradiction in safety and repair its whole interaction in revision. Do not send it back and forth as someone else's responsibility.

A substantial scene rewrite is permitted within approved outcomes and durable boundaries. Local canon repair must still sound human. Correcting a pronoun alone does not repair a missing relationship.

Both reviews name the exact candidate path, SHA-256 and consequential authority basis used. Any prose change invalidates old PASS. A changed decision/source dependency invalidates the affected part of a review even when prose bytes are unchanged; recheck only the affected scope and its consequences. Independent full reading is required before acceptance; disclose contaminated context.

When a prior candidate exposed a recurring failure, review the mechanism-level regression test without treating the old wording or repair as a model. A changed workspace or restarted draft does not itself close the risk.
