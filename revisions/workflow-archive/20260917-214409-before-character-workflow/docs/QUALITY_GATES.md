# Quality Gates

Quality is not a single score. Literary success and boundary safety are independently necessary.

## Gate 1 — Preflight projection

- internal safety card verifies purpose, outcome, chronology, knowledge/reveal boundaries, durable state, factual dependencies, exclusions, and author approval;
- writer brief contains only five to eight hard facts plus human pressure and creative room;
- no blocking material choice remains.

## Gate 2 — Draft integrity

- prose comes from the approved brief;
- hard facts remain intact;
- no audit appendix is embedded in the manuscript;
- the scene has an intentional movement or after-effect.

## Gate 3 — Blind literary review

Review the page before the specification: desire, resistance, change, voice, dialogue tactics, subtext, narrative distance, rhythm, imagery, horror, and emotional afterlife. Findings require textual evidence, but no fixed quota. The recommended repair may be local or substantial.

## Gate 4 — Safety review

Separately verify canon, chronology, knowledge, reveal order, clue/custody limits, durable state, cultural/professional claims, and rejected-material exclusion. A safety PASS does not certify literary quality.

## Gate 5 — Revision and re-review

Repair safety issues conservatively and literary issues at their true scale. Keep `revised.md` free of audit material. Meaningful changes receive fresh literary and safety reviews.

## Gate 6 — Deterministic health

Run repository validation, links, and continuity checks. Errors block canonization. Structural checks certify their contracts, not literary quality or source truth.

## Gate 7 — Canon diff and author approval

List proposed durable changes by character, relationship, knowledge, object, promise, question, timeline, and world rule. Require fresh literary PASS, safety PASS, and explicit author acceptance for the same candidate.

## Gate 8 — Promotion

Promote manuscript prose only, apply accepted durable state, validate again, and create a semantic commit only when requested or part of the approved transition.
