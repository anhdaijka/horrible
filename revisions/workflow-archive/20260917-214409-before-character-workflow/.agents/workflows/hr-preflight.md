# /hr-preflight <chapter>

1. Resolve the target workspace and read `author/session-state.md`.
2. Read `.agents/skills/hr-source-preflight/SKILL.md` completely.
3. Inspect only the authoritative canon, continuity, character, plot, matrix, and research sources needed for this chapter.
4. Write `revisions/chapter-work/<chapter>/preflight.md` from `templates/chapter-preflight.md`.
5. Write `revisions/chapter-work/<chapter>/writing-brief.md` from `templates/writing-brief.md`.
6. If a material choice remains open, mark `AUTHOR_GATE: PENDING` and stop. Otherwise record the approval basis.

The writer-facing brief must not expose the internal checklist, future solution, full clue matrix, sample dialogue, prose rules, or rejected text.
