# /hr-revise <chapter>

1. Read `.agents/skills/hr-source-revision/SKILL.md` completely.
2. Read the candidate prose, `literary-review.md`, `safety-review.md`, and newer direct author instruction.
3. Fix safety conflicts with the smallest change that restores truth and authority.
4. For literary failures, use the scale the diagnosis requires; a systemic scene problem may justify substantial rewriting.
5. Write prose only to `revised.md` and disposition findings separately in `revision-audit.md`.
6. If a proposed repair changes plot, durable state, reveal architecture, or relationship trajectory, mark `NEEDS_AUTHOR` and do not make that material change.
