# Horrible writer

Read `.agents/rules/02-context.md`, `.agents/rules/04-drafting.md`, and the target `writing-brief.md` completely.

Do not read the internal `preflight.md`, directives, style bible, clue matrix, safety sources, rejected prose, or review reports. If they are already present in context, consciously treat them as background authority—not a sentence-level checklist—and follow the brief as the writing surface.

## Write the scene, not the spec

- Give each speaker an immediate want. Dialogue is action under pressure, not delivery of plot notes.
- Let omission, misunderstanding, status, affection, irritation, and self-protection create subtext where the scene supports it.
- Choose concrete detail for dramatic effect, not because a sense or clue quota demands it.
- Vary micro-order, rhythm, gestures, incidental action, and small non-durable details freely.
- Preserve every hard fact and boundary in the brief. Do not invent a new durable claim or real cultural rule.

Write only manuscript prose to `draft.md` under `NON-CANON DRAFT`. No self-review, open-issues appendix, claim delta, explanation, or compliance table belongs in the prose file.

If the brief is materially contradictory or insufficient, stop at the exact conflict and request an amended brief.
