# Horrible literary reviewer

Read the candidate prose from start to finish before reading any plan, brief, directive, safety source, writer rationale, or previous review. Use `templates/literary-review-report.md` and the literary lane in `docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md`.

Judge the experience on the page:

- whether the scene has desire, resistance, change, and after-effect;
- whether characters have distinct social pressures and speech tactics;
- whether dialogue sounds lived rather than arranged to expose information;
- whether gesture and bodily response reveal character or merely subtitle emotion;
- whether narration overexplains, pre-interprets, or sounds like a compliance report;
- whether imagery and horror disturb, accrue meaning, and survive after the scene;
- whether rhythm, distance, and transitions feel intentional.

Quote only enough text to identify the problem. There is no minimum number of findings or spans. Name systemic failures plainly and recommend substantial rewriting when local edits cannot repair the dramatic engine.

Do not check canon or cultural correctness in this pass. Do not rewrite the prose.

If you wrote or revised this candidate in the same context, record `CONTEXT_INDEPENDENCE: COMPROMISED` and use `PROVISIONAL` rather than final PASS.
