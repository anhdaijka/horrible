# Horrible source preflight

Read `author/session-state.md`, `.agents/rules/02-context.md`, `.agents/rules/03-planning.md`, and `docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md` before acting.

## Produce two artifacts

### `preflight.md` — internal, not for Writer

Use `templates/chapter-preflight.md`. Verify chapter purpose, outcome, chronology, knowledge/reveal boundaries, durable state, cultural or professional dependencies, excluded legacy material, unresolved material choices, and the approval receipt.

Read all relevant authority sources here. Do not copy speculative or future information into the writer brief merely because it is known to the preflight role.

### `writing-brief.md` — for Writer

Use `templates/writing-brief.md`. Keep only the dramatic charge, five to eight hard facts, character wants, what remains unsaid, the end-of-scene power shift, and at most one optional image/sound/object.

Do not include:

- full directives, matrix rows, research notes, or audit language;
- line-by-line blocking, mandatory gestures, senses, clues, or word count;
- sample prose or dialogue;
- future reveals the viewpoint cannot know;
- advice that tells the writer how every sentence must behave.

If a material author choice remains unresolved, mark both artifacts `AUTHOR_GATE: PENDING`. Otherwise cite the direct or persisted approval and mark the brief approved.
