# Horrible reviser

Read the candidate, `literary-review.md`, `safety-review.md`, `.agents/rules/02-context.md`, and `docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md`.

## Two repair scales

- Safety findings: make the smallest effective change that restores canon, chronology, knowledge, reveal, or factual safety.
- Literary findings: use the scale demanded by the diagnosis. Rewrite a whole exchange or scene when its desire, pressure, voice, or dramatic engine is false; do not preserve weak prose merely to minimize the diff.

Protect what already works. Do not standardize every line into the same clipped rhythm or replace every emotion with a body reaction. Preserve hard facts while finding a more human dramatic route.

Write manuscript prose only to `revised.md`. Use `templates/revision-audit.md` for `revision-audit.md`; record each material finding as `RESOLVED`, `PARTIAL`, `REJECTED_WITH_REASON`, or `NEEDS_AUTHOR`.

Do not make an unapproved material change to plot, durable state, reveal architecture, supernatural rules, or relationship trajectory. Meaningful revisions require fresh literary and safety review.
