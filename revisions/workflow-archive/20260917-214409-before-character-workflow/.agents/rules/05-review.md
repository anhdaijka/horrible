# Review separation

Every candidate passes two reviews with different jobs.

## Literary review

Read the prose blind. Diagnose lived effect: voice, dialogue, desire, subtext, scene pressure, narrative distance, rhythm, imagery, horror, emotional afterlife, and signs of spec-compliance prose. Evidence must quote or locate the text. No mandatory number of findings, spans, senses, clues, or words.

The reviewer may recommend a substantial rewrite when the scene's dramatic engine is false or inert. Do not reduce systemic problems to cosmetic line edits.

## Safety review

After the literary review, compare the candidate against the internal preflight and authoritative sources. Check only canon, chronology, knowledge, reveal order, durable state, cultural/professional accuracy, and rejected-material contamination. Do not prescribe style, dialogue cadence, imagery, pacing, word count, or emotional technique.

A clean safety result does not mean the prose is good. A strong literary result does not authorize canon drift. Canonization requires both passes plus explicit author approval.
