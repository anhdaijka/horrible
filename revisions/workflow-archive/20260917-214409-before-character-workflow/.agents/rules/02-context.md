# Context routing

Load context by role. More context is not automatically safer or more creative.

## Preflight / safety / canonization

Read the authoritative sources needed to verify hard facts: current author instruction, `author/session-state.md`, relevant directives, decisions, continuity, plot, character files, clue matrix, and verified research. Resolve conflicts before exposing a writing brief.

## Writer

Read only:

1. the approved `writing-brief.md` for the active chapter;
2. the immediately preceding accepted manuscript passage when continuity of voice or action requires it;
3. a narrowly requested source excerpt when the brief explicitly marks a blocking factual gap.

Do not preload the internal `preflight.md`, full directives, style bible, clue matrix, review reports, rejected drafts, or old chapter prose. The brief carries the hard boundaries. If it is insufficient, stop and ask for a brief amendment instead of researching the whole repository mid-draft.

## Literary reviewer

Read the candidate prose first and judge what is actually on the page. Do not read the preflight, writing brief, safety sources, writer rationale, or previous reviews until the blind literary verdict is written.

## Reviser

Read the candidate prose, `literary-review.md`, `safety-review.md`, and direct author instruction. Open an authority source only to resolve a cited safety finding.

Never use rejected prose as a model unless the author explicitly requests historical comparison.
