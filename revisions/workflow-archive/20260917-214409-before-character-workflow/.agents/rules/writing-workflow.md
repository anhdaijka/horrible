# Creative–safety chapter workflow

The active pipeline is:

1. `/hr-preflight <chapter>` → internal `preflight.md` plus writer-facing `writing-brief.md`.
2. `/hr-write <chapter>` → prose-only `draft.md`.
3. `/hr-review <chapter>` → blind `literary-review.md`.
4. `/hr-safety <chapter>` → factual/canon `safety-review.md`.
5. `/hr-revise <chapter>` → prose-only `revised.md` plus separate `revision-audit.md`.
6. Repeat literary and safety review on the revised candidate when changes are meaningful.
7. `/hr-canonize <chapter>` only after fresh literary PASS, safety PASS, and explicit author acceptance.

These are separate authority lanes, not a single checklist. The writer never receives the internal safety card. The literary reviewer never receives the specification before reaching a page-based verdict. The safety reviewer never acts as a stylist.

Legacy workspaces remain historical evidence but do not route the active workflow. A restarted chapter uses a new workspace suffix such as `CH-01-v2` rather than overwriting old artifacts.
