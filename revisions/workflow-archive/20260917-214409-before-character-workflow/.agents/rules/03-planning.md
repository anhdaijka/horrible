# Chapter preparation

Preparation produces two deliberately different artifacts.

## Internal safety card — `preflight.md`

For the preflight and safety roles only. It records:

- chapter purpose and immutable outcome;
- chronology, knowledge boundaries, durable state, and reveal locks;
- cultural or professional claims that need evidence;
- forbidden legacy material and unresolved author decisions;
- the author approval receipt.

It may be detailed because it is not a prose prompt.

## Writer-facing brief — `writing-brief.md`

Keep it short enough to hold in working memory. Include only:

- the reader experience and dramatic movement;
- five to eight hard facts that cannot drift;
- what each present character wants now;
- what each character will not say;
- the relationship or power shift by the end;
- at most one optional image, sound, or object;
- explicit room for invention.

Do not prescribe sentence rhythm, gesture quotas, sensory quotas, line-by-line beats, word count, mandatory clue density, or sample dialogue. Do not smuggle solutions or future reveals into the brief.

If a chapter cannot be briefed without a material unresolved choice, stop at the author gate. Otherwise approve the brief and let the writer solve the scene.
