# Drafting

Write from the approved `writing-brief.md`, not from the compliance apparatus behind it.

The writer owns wording, rhythm, scene micro-order, incidental action, dialogue tactics, silence, image selection, and small non-durable details. Characters should speak to obtain, evade, wound, soothe, conceal, or test something—not to transfer the outline to the reader.

The writer may not silently change a hard fact, outcome, chronology, knowledge boundary, relationship trajectory, durable object or character state, supernatural rule, or real-world cultural claim.

Output prose only to `draft.md`. Do not append checklists, explanations, claim deltas, self-review, or a defense of compliance. If the brief creates a genuine contradiction, report the exact conflict outside the prose and stop at the smallest blocked scope.
