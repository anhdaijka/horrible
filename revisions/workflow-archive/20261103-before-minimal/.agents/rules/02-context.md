# Context by role

Use the minimum context sufficient for the actual role. Operational brevity does not set manuscript length.

- **Director / Preflight:** session-state, current instruction, `author/case-zero-current.md` (CZ-20261007 / D-008 dated 2026-10-07), relevant decisions, supplied author beats, plot/continuity/character sources and relevant verified research. First give the lightweight plot-following preflight under 03-planning.md; wait for missing author beats before scene-route development. The October receipt retires conflicting September Case Zero plot packets; a higher decision number or later Git commit does not mean newer author intent. No archives or rejected prose for inspiration.
- **Writer, generation:** 04-drafting.md plus writing-brief.md, scene-ecology.md, character-context.md, relevant accepted preceding prose and explicitly named spoiler-safe lived-context excerpts. Concrete story life matters more than a small word count. Retrieve missing established facts through targeted safe excerpts.
- **Writer, self-edit:** after a complete draft, open qa-card.md and targeted hard-source references. Keep discovery alternatives, prior failures and rejected prose out of generation.
- **Literary reviewer:** given an exact workspace, read locator-only candidate.md and full prose before session-state, handoff, packet or prior findings. Record that reading, then diagnose with relevant context and 05-review.md. Prior exposure means COMPROMISED.
- **Safety reviewer:** candidate, packet, QA and authority needed for consequential facts/state and real-world claims.
- **Reviser:** candidate, author feedback and available reviews first, then targeted sources. Record missing review lanes; complete them before acceptance.

First-pass Writer does not preload discovery-slate, preflight, QA, reviews, failure-memory, whole profiles, style bibles or craft lexicons. General role instructions are not story sources; a file-reading restriction cannot hide instructions already in task history. Use an actually fresh context for strict trials.

Upstream skills are explicit-only. Do not implicitly combine generic writing skills with Horrible roles. Archives and summaries never replace current source.
