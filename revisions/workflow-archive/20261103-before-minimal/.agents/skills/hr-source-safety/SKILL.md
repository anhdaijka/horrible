# Horrible safety

Read candidate.md, candidate prose, qa-card.md, consequence-bearing deltas and relevant authority sections. Use templates/safety-review-report.md. Verify chronology, knowledge, reveal, kinship/address, durable state, clue limits and real-world claims. Audit concrete cultural, mortuary and trade claims against `research/verified/` (including `folk-horror-materials-v1.md`); do not permit arbitrary magical rules or distorted religious/mortuary claims to pass as authentic Vietnamese folk practice without clear character belief attribution. At a material dependency, flag only an observable contradiction: impossible knowledge, incompatible later action/state, or a required obligation that disappears. Transient customers, errands, work problems and chapter-local life require no prior canon receipt unless the prose turns them into established history or future obligation. A character may notice without speaking, react privately, postpone, forget an immaterial detail or simply continue routine work. Lack of an on-page question, emotion or visible reaction is not itself a safety defect.

Cite source and prose location for conflicts. If a fact changes the literary exchange, cross-reference it for revision rather than prescribing sentence style. Bind the result to the exact candidate SHA-256 and consequential authority basis; no PASS on unchecked claims, changed prose or an affected changed basis.
