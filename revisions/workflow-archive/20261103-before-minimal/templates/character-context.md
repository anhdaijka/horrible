# Character context — <chapter>

> Writer-safe people context. Give enough present life to generate behavior; keep future solutions out.

## POV life at entry
For the focal character, state the current bodily/mental residue, ordinary concerns, outside life, recent displacement or preoccupation that can shape attention before the plot demands anything.

This is not a list of required thoughts. It is the life from which selective attention emerges.

## Relationships with history
For important pairs, give the useful shared history, familiarity, status, address, affection, irritation, debt, embarrassment, habits of cooperation or old friction that already exists before this chapter.

Prefer relationship texture over trait labels.

## Kinship / address / register map
For every scene-relevant pair—or named third party likely to be referred to through family/social context—record only the facts that constrain natural Vietnamese reference: actual relationship, gender, relative generation/age/status where material, and any established address/reference forms supported by authority or lived context.

Keep factual relationship separate from optional surface wording. If an exact pronoun, kinship term, nickname, diminutive or classifier is not established, leave it open rather than inventing one for local color. Preserve relation direction: parent/child, in-law relation, senior/junior and gender must not drift when a person is identified through somebody else.

## Immediate ordinary concerns and agency
What can each person plausibly want to finish, avoid, protect, postpone, complain about or get back to today for reasons larger than the mystery?

These are present-life affordances, not prescribed mini-arcs.

## Conversational field
Describe situation-specific social dynamics as **floor continuity**, not dialogue quotas: who can hold social initiative across several thoughts or actions, what work/route/complaint/story can carry their speech, how another person may listen selectively or answer late, what interruptions merely suspend a topic rather than replacing it, and what shared context need not be explained.

Include where politeness, familiarity, status or pressure changes floor ownership and turn shape. A customer arrival, gesture, short acknowledgment or paragraph break does not automatically transfer the floor. Do not require long speeches either; uneven turn length should emerge from relationship and situation.

Do not script sample lines, reusable tics or target sentence/turn lengths.

## Knowledge / reveal edge
State only what each important person knows, believes, misreads or cannot know at this point when it materially constrains narration or action.

Knowledge is a boundary. It does not require a line, visible reaction or question.

## Targeted lived-context
Include an explicitly approved generative-safe excerpt only when one truly helps. If no safe excerpt exists, leave this section empty rather than importing rejected prose or inventing a style sample.
