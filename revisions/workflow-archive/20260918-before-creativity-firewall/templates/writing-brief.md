## Situation and reader experience
What is happening, why it matters to these people, what should remain with the reader?

## People in the situation
Who needs what from whom? What practical or relational difficulty makes it interesting? What choices can they make? Include unspoken concerns only when real, not as a mandatory column.

## Hard boundaries
Only consequential facts, outcome, knowledge/reveal limits, chronology and durable state. No fixed count. Keep author constraints separate from motives.

## Causal hinges and regression tests
Only the hinges whose failure would make behavior or later knowledge false. State the observable result, not required wording. Include relevant mechanism-level regression tests without old prose or a mandatory repair.

## Concrete life and sources
Useful verified details, allowed source sections, and any genuine gap. Do not replace all practical needs with vague "things" to avoid research.

## Execution freedom
Local decisions, mistakes, cooperation, timing, narrative summary/interiority and dialogue length are open within the boundaries. Options are not compulsory beats.

New texture is free. Record separately any new promise, prior event, custody change, future meeting, distinctive object fact or durable knowledge that creates a cross-scene obligation.
