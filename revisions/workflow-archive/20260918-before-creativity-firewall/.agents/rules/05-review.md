# Review and repair

Use docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md as a reader-oriented standard, not a scorecard.

Literary review records a blind first reading from prose plus locator-only identity, then a contextual diagnosis using the writer packet. Evaluate people, choices, dialogue, address, narration/interiority, practical life, causality, rhythm, horror, mystery, emotion and after-effect as relevant to the work's scope. At a consequential hinge, state any credible competing reading and whether it breaks intended knowledge or later behavior. No requirement that every line or scene accomplish every function.

Safety review verifies hard facts and knowledge against authority sources, including kinship and address. It also checks implications that follow directly from established perception and knowledge: correct facts do not excuse an impossible inference or missing proportionate response. A fact with a literary consequence is shared between lanes: flag the contradiction in safety and repair its whole interaction in revision. Do not send it back and forth as someone else's responsibility.

A substantial scene rewrite is permitted within approved outcomes and durable boundaries. Local canon repair must still sound human. Correcting a pronoun alone does not repair a missing relationship.

Both reviews name the exact candidate path, SHA-256 and consequential authority basis used. Any prose change invalidates old PASS. A changed decision/source dependency invalidates the affected part of a review even when prose bytes are unchanged; recheck only the affected scope and its consequences. Independent full reading is required before acceptance; disclose contaminated context.

When a prior candidate exposed a recurring failure, review the mechanism-level regression test without treating the old wording or repair as a model. A changed workspace or restarted draft does not itself close the risk.
