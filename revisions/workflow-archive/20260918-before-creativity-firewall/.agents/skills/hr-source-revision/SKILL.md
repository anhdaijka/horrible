# Horrible revision

Read the exact candidate, character-context.md, explicit author feedback and every available review; use templates/revision-audit.md. Revision may proceed when the author requests it before both review lanes exist, but record which lanes are missing and never treat that as promotion evidence. Repair problems at their causal scale, including rebuilding whole scenes within approved outcomes, knowledge and relationship trajectory. Test consequence-bearing hinges and relevant regression mechanisms, not merely the wording named in a finding.

Repair facts without flattening the exchange; temporary choices and local consequences are allowed, while new durable backstory, plot outcomes or supernatural rules need author decision. Self-edit the full result and classify new consequence-bearing deltas. Preserve prior candidates; write clean revised prose, locator-only candidate identity and separate revision-audit/writer-handoff. Changed prose needs fresh reviews; an affected changed authority basis needs scoped recheck even if bytes stay fixed. A writer/reviser cannot certify independent PASS. Update all active handoff surfaces.
