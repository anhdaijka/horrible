# Horrible safety

Read candidate.md, candidate prose, internal preflight, consequence-bearing deltas and relevant authority sections. Use templates/safety-review-report.md. Verify chronology, knowledge, reveal, kinship/address, durable state, clue limits and real-world claims. For each consequential hinge, check direct implications: what a person who perceives and knows those facts could plausibly infer or must at least react to. This is not a mandate that every person interrogate, panic or reach the correct conclusion.

Cite source and prose location for conflicts. If a fact changes the literary exchange, cross-reference it for revision rather than prescribing sentence style. Bind the result to the exact candidate SHA-256 and consequential authority basis; no PASS on unchecked claims, changed prose or an affected changed basis.
