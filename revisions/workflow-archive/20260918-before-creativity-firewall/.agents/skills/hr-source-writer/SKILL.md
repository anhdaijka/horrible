# Horrible writer

Read .agents/rules/04-drafting.md, writing-brief.md and character-context.md. Consult only named safe excerpts when needed; do not guess missing kinship or address. Read docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md for calibration when establishing or repairing voice. Write people making situational choices, with summary and interiority available inside the approved viewpoint. Dialogue length follows the exchange; no fragments, filler, verbal-tic, hidden-agenda or aggression quota.

Self-edit the entire candidate, then verify the packet's consequence-bearing hinges and relevant regression tests against what a skeptical reader can actually perceive. Record consequence-bearing deltas separately from local texture. Output clean prose, locator-only candidate.md and separate writer-handoff.md; do not put self-praise, findings or repair rationale where a blind reviewer must read it. Update session-state, preserve prior candidates and never self-certify independent PASS. For a scene trial, name the hypothesis and scope; do not claim a complete chapter or scale it without recording what the trial established.
