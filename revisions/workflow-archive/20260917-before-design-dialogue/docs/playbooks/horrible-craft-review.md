# Horrible chapter operating playbook

## Preparation
Read current author instruction and source state. Produce internal preflight.md plus writing-brief.md and character-context.md. Verify outcome, knowledge, chronology and facts; project kinship, social address, ordinary commitments and practical needs into the packet. Keep craft options distinct from durable facts. There is no limit of eight facts or mandatory hidden agenda.

Writer may read named source excerpts when the packet lacks context. Select sections rather than full spoiler-bearing dossiers. Restoring established relationships does not reopen approved plot design.

## Draft and self-edit
Writer uses the packet and relevant accepted preceding prose. Local choices, momentary attitudes, mistakes, cooperation, rhythm, summary and interiority are available within approved boundaries. Rewriting execution is different from changing plot or relationship trajectory.

Read and self-edit the complete result before delivery: intelligible relationships, proportionate behavior, actual choices, selective dialogue, object continuity and emotional effect. This is not independent review. Prose stays clean; candidate.md records path, scope, SHA-256 and superseded candidate. Preserve existing work before replacement; update session-state.

When repairing a demonstrated recurring failure, a scene trial may test the solution before a full rewrite. It is not a new compulsory stage for every chapter.

## Literary review
Use docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md. Read candidate.md and the entire named prose first. Record the first-read experience without brief/rationale. Then inspect character context and relevant brief; add contextual diagnosis without retroactively erasing that experience.

FRESH requires actual absence of prior exposure to this candidate's planning/rationale and writing. Same task history or changing models cannot erase context. A compromised reviewer can provide useful provisional findings but cannot issue final independent PASS.

Review the appropriate scale: a scene trial is not a whole chapter, and an opening need not resolve an arc. Quote enough evidence to locate causes, not to meet a span quota.

## Safety review and overlap
Verify canon, chronology, knowledge/reveal order, relationships/address, physical/custody state and real cultural/professional claims against sources. Real-world claims need evidence or explicit belief/local framing.

Safety may identify a mother–son relationship error; literary revision handles how restoring it changes the conversation. Neither lane may dismiss a shared issue as exclusively the other's job. Safety does not prescribe preferred cadence or aesthetic devices.

## Revision and authorization
Rebuild an exchange or whole scene when the literary diagnosis warrants it, preserving approved outcomes, knowledge, chronology and durable relationships/rules. No additional approval for execution-only rewriting under a revision request. Ask for changes to plot purpose/outcome, reveal architecture, new durable history, relationship trajectory or supernatural rule.

Resolve findings with evidence; rejecting an optional suggestion with a reason is allowed. Output revised prose separately from revision-audit.md. Self-edit after repair.

## Candidate identity and acceptance
candidate.md and both reviews carry exact path and SHA-256. Compute from file bytes; do not invent a hash. Any content change invalidates earlier PASS for promotion. Re-review the changed scope and its effects, issuing a new verdict against current bytes. The initial independent literary pass reads the full candidate; do not substitute searching for reading.

Canonization requires matching current candidate identities, independent literary PASS, safety PASS, explicit author acceptance of that candidate and an approved durable-state diff. Transfer manuscript prose only, update registries and validate. Approval of a scene trial is not approval of a complete chapter.

## Handoff and preservation
Record actual non-canon progress, active workspace, candidate scope and next command in session-state after each operation. Do not overwrite an existing draft just because the router says write. Before a broad workflow redesign, copy the actual working-tree instruction set and verify a manifest. Mark archives historical; they do not supply active instructions.

## Evidence limits
Skill/schema/link checks prove structure only. Report separately whether prose was tried, self-edited, independently reviewed or accepted. See the literary standard for craft research provenance. Do not promise an author's level of quality on the strength of validators.
