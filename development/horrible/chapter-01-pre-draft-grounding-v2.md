# Chapter 1 v2 — Just-in-time pre-draft grounding

Status: writer preparation under the author-approved Chapter 1 brief. Not manuscript canon.

## Opening transaction mechanics

Use only modern/family-shop-safe mechanics already supported by verified research and the author's domain correction.

### After-death hook

- The victim dies during the night.
- By the next morning, a relative from his household comes to bà Lan's shop for a small set of ordinary funeral/memorial paper goods. Do not write a universal `bộ tang lễ chuẩn`.
- The relative naturally mentions the dead man's name and local identifier while explaining whose funeral the goods are for.
- Bà Lan recognizes the name/locality because a still-uncollected custom order for that same recipient is tagged/stored in the shop.
- She checks the shop ledger/order note.
- The order date predates the death by four days.
- The relative is not aware of the earlier custom order.
- Do not introduce the `perhaps he was gravely ill / family prepared early` hypothesis in Chapter 1. That explanation belongs only after the chronology has been discovered and the story moves forward.

This mechanism avoids unsupported ritual claims: the name is recognized through shop administration and social memory, not because every funeral purchase requires a metaphysical name/address procedure.

## Original order mechanics — four days earlier

- The woman asks for several ordinary male paper goods plus one custom old cassette radio.
- The shop naturally assumes these goods are intended for someone already dead; nobody asks whether the recipient is alive.
- The custom radio requires coordination with an outside maker/supplier; bà Lan may say three days is tight and that she must check.
- Khanh writes a simple custom-order note because the shop is busy and the item must be made/picked up later.
- Recipient identity and purchaser/pickup identity are recorded separately as a family-shop administrative habit, not a ritual rule.
- The purchaser declines/leaves no useful phone number and says she will return herself in three days.
- The order is not collected before the later death, leaving the tagged order/record available for the opening-hook recognition.

Prototype appearance is rejected and must not be reused.

- medium build, shoulders slightly rounded in the ordinary way of someone used to carrying shopping/household loads;

- practical dark quilted jacket, black trousers and ordinary market shoes with damp dirt at the soles;
- carries a woven/plastic shopping bag looped over the right forearm, leaving the left hand freer;

Nothing about her appearance is ghost-coded. The later horror comes from identity and chronology, not spectral styling.
