# Case Zero: Character Profiles

Status: accepted development material for Case Zero (D-050 Master Plan).

This document outlines the specific identities, contradictions, and roles for the characters involved in **Case Zero: Đơn hàng cho người chưa chết**. 

> **Note on Naming:** The victim's son-in-law is named **Đinh Công Toàn** (previously drafted as Tuấn) to avoid a name collision with Khanh's brother-in-law (Lê Đức Tuấn).

---

## 1. Vũ Thị Thủy (The Surviving Sister)
- **Role:** The victim's younger sister. An unmarried garment factory worker in Yên Phong.
- **The 1996 Sin:** Was physically present and complicit when Thoa was drowned, driven by jealousy and family pressure.
- **The 2026 Motive:** Exploited for 30 years, she was enraged when Cường tried to cut her out of the multi-billion dong road-frontage land.
- **Action in Case Zero:** Conducted a severe psychological intimidation campaign against Cường (playing ghost tapes, slashing photos). Created a complex, fake alibi at her factory to avoid murder charges.
- **Contradiction:** A ruthless schemer for land, yet genuinely terrified of the supernatural. Her fake alibi backfires when the real ghost appears.
- **Physical Clue:** Right-handed with a deformed/stiff left wrist (proving she could not be the left-handed purchaser at the shop).

## 2. Đinh Công Toàn (The Son-in-Law / The Loyalty Trap)
- **Role:** Vũ Văn Cường's son-in-law, married to Lan Anh. 
- **The 2026 Motive:** Drowning in massive, dangerous debt from gambling/gangsters. He desperately needs the land deed.
- **Action in Case Zero:** Slipped a heavy dose of Diazepam (sedatives) into Cường's medicinal wine to knock him out and steal the deed. When Cường died (snapped neck), Toàn panicked and used the "burglary" theory to cover his tracks.
- **Contradiction:** A coward who resorts to poisoning, but truly loves his wife and child. He is not a cold-blooded killer, just a desperate man who made a fatal mistake.

## 3. Vũ Thị Lan Anh (The Daughter)
- **Role:** Cường's daughter, Toàn's wife.
- **Action in Case Zero:** Discovers her husband's muddy shoes and the half-empty blister pack of sleeping pills. Realizes her husband likely caused her father's death.
- **The Loyalty Trap:** Caught in an impossible moral dilemma. She chooses her living husband/child over her dead father and actively burns the evidence (the pills) in the votive fire (*hóa vàng*) during the funeral. 
- **Interaction with Khanh:** Khanh catches her destroying evidence, creating an intense, unspoken moral standoff.

## 4. Bà Mùi (The Widow / The Confirmation Bias)
- **Role:** Cường's wife.
- **Function:** She acts as the ultimate "noise generator" for the investigation. She knows Cường was terrified of "nó về đòi nợ" (Thoa's ghost), but interprets everything through pure superstition.
- **Storytelling Style:** Hysterical, weeping, constantly offering superstitious explanations that blur the line between Cường's real paranoia and her own grief.

## 5. Trưởng họ Vũ Cổn (The Patriarch / The Fourth Accomplice)
- **Role:** The eldest and most respected man in the Vũ clan (xóm Trại).
- **The 1996 Sin:** The true architect of the tragedy. He witnessed the drowning and gave the patriarchal order: *"Gia môn bất hạnh, dìm nó cho khuất mắt"* (Drown her to hide the shame). He orchestrated the cover-up and erased Thoa from the genealogy.
- **The 2026 Cover-up:** When Cường dies, Ông Cổn uses his immense village authority to force the police and family to accept the "accidental fall" ruling. He demands an immediate burial without an autopsy to ensure the 1996 secret stays buried.
- **Contradiction:** Projects perfect Confucian morality and clan honor, but is functionally a murderer who values reputation over human life.

## 6. Đặng Văn Bình (Thầy Cúng / The Secret Keeper)
- **Role:** Local ritual specialist. Apprentice to the heterodox master who planted the 30-year bamboo talisman in 1996.
- **Action in Case Zero:** Had a tense, secret meeting with Cường the night before the death to discuss the rotting talisman. 
- **The Cover-up:** When questioned by police, he provides an ironclad (but fake) alibi about performing a ritual elsewhere. He rushes the encoffining process, trying to hammer iron nails into the coffin to suppress the spirit.
- **Epistemic Style:** Operates purely on occult logic and self-preservation.

## 7. Đào Văn Sang (Cò đất / The Red Herring)
- **Role:** A sleazy, opportunistic real estate broker.
- **Action in Case Zero:** Intentionally stokes the hatred between Thủy and Toàn, feeding them paranoia to force a quick, cheap sale of the disputed land.
- **Function:** He knows nothing about the ghost or the murder. However, his heavy-handed manipulation provides Khanh with a massive, perfectly logical "human motive" that acts as a smokescreen masking the supernatural truth.

## 8. Bà Lụa (The Neighbor / The Embedded Storyteller)
- **Role:** An ordinary village neighbor. 
- **Action in Case Zero:** Comes to Bà Lan's shop to buy votive paper for her own family's death anniversary (*giỗ*). 
- **Storytelling Style:** Casually gossips while picking out paper clothes. She recounts a distorted, 30-year-old memory of Thoa's disappearance, mentioning "nghe tiếng khóc nhưng nhà bảo theo trai" and "ông thầy pháp lạ mặt đóng cọc tre".
- **Function:** Delivers the critical "cọc tre" clue organically through village gossip, showing how the community network remembers what the family tried to erase.
