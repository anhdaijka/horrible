## 2. Thủy's new hybrid: “the evidence exchange” (candidate T-H1)

**One immediate cause, two naturally conflicting purposes**, not unrelated A1+A2+A3 tasks packed into the same scene.

Proposed after noon on mùng 13 (precise time/location/shift OPEN):

It must have a concrete plausible placement/power/ownership trail, and not be recycled from old rejected supernatural scenes. He now has a way to publicly discredit her.
- He contacts or summons Thủy **before her scheduled factory shift** and threatens to show it to Toàn, Cổn and others if she repeats her property claims or the family secret. Crucially, Thủy is afraid her *genuine* limited 1996 testimony may be dismissed if her separate staged scares are exposed as a deliberate lie. The conflict is about **credibility**, not just a theft or fake murder alibi.
- Thủy attends intending to retrieve/disable her prop **and obtain recognition of a concrete contribution to the family's pond-side assets**; those two stakes are causally linked because Cường uses the fake scare as leverage against her demand. She can carry **her own long-kept records** of repayments and challenge a specific discrepancy in Cường's ledger. Optional new culpable action: she copies, pockets or tears one limited household page despite refusal. Choose at most ONE trace/action after an audit of access, custody and whether it actually matters; do not assume a conveniently preserved single document proves title or the 1996 murder.
- They fail to agree. Thủy leaves **well before** the fatal window and reaches the factory with independent continuing activity evidence through ~22:54. Cường is primed to misattribute later **real** supernatural disturbances to a newly discovered human hoax; this can justify the canon local recording at ~22:54 without a character knowing the supernatural truth.

Discovery of an ordinary prop or the missing/copying of a ledger item (if approved) could fuel a *misdated* suspicion about a later visit, but actual time evidence ultimately differentiates earlier misconduct from the killing. Avoid building an omniscient CCTV timestamp, confession or magically surviving hardcopy as an all-purpose proof.

**Thematic pay-off:** Thủy chose a lie as a weapon against people who had erased her sister with a lie. It may now destroy the credibility of her *true* discovery of Thoa in 1996. When she later reports truth, she must first own the false scares rather than earning reader trust for free. Cường's exploitation of her lie is a fresh human cruelty without requiring an additional crime.

**Epistemic/reveal design:** If the visit scene contains explicit knowledge of Thoa already dead, do not show it from a Thủy limited POV before G6a and coyly redact what she naturally thinks. ## 3. Bình: why the earlier “Cường recontacts him and reveals everything” pitch was insufficient

**Critique:** A 2026 Cường already afraid of historic exposure should not willingly divulge the crime or precise grave to a partly informed former assistant merely to manufacture a blackmail suspect. If that is the only source, a late neat confession makes Bình an exposition courier and devalues the thirty-year silence. It also overstates the proof a ritual assistant could supply.

Three genuinely distinguishable source models:

**B-I: opportunistic probe from multiple *limited* sources (preferred to test, NOT approved).** In 1996 Bình had a real limited job for the outside ritual specialist, recognized the client family and a **broad** part of the pond; he did not see remains/the fatal blow. In 2026 the road/pond works are openly known; he may observe Cường's guarded response or unusual urgency when the disturbed shore is discussed. He makes a carefully vague insinuation during an ordinary social encounter. Cường reacts as though he has something serious to lose. The blackmail evolves from **Bình's credible ability to name an unusual secret commission/client/date and attract scrutiny**, *not proof of murder*. He may deliberately bluff that he knows what is buried; author and reader must distinguish fact, inference and bluff. The source of the mùng-13 confrontation is an escalation after earlier contact, not an impossible all-knowing accusation. His specific old material-memory can later narrow a search *only together with independent construction/topographic sources*.

**B-II: Cổn renews pressure on Bình for a limited purpose.** Cổn need not believe in ghosts: he once used a ritual specialist as a **tool of controlling others' fear**. In 2026 he has a mundane concern about scrutiny and meets Bình; inadvertently confirms an old secret, which Bình uses against Cường. This gives Cổn strong psychology but prematurely chooses an explicit 2026 act, which author **deliberately deferred**. Therefore **hold, not active proposal**, until Cổn is better developed.

**B-III: Cường independently makes *limited* modern contact, not a confession.** Cường notices pond-edge work, receives Thủy's fake threats and sends a narrow instruction/warning about the old job to Bình; Bình realizes he has leverage but does not gain the cause of death. More externally corroborable if a plausible surviving 2026 contact exists; weaker if the only purpose is to deliver a conveniently saved clue. Don't assert such messages happened until approved.

**Required 4-column epistemic audit for any chosen B model:** 1996 facts Bình directly witnessed; 2026 observations from independently plausible sources; guesses or deliberate bluffs; objectively corroborable records/testimony. His night-13 ash/contact can place him at a relevant location if independently verified, but ritual ash by itself is not a unique personal identifier, murder time or proof he knew how Thoa died. Lan Anh can overhear a threat and report **the words she heard**, not infer all 1996 truth. A blackmail threat without evidence may still work against a guilty, frightened man, but dramatize the initial test and reaction.

**Connectedness without conspiracy:** If Thủy's fake scares amplify Cường's fear, and land works are public, **Bình does not need to collude with Thủy** to notice the frightened Cường. Keep their information silos intact unless a later incident earns new knowledge.

## 4. Cổn — develop the person, DO NOT yet lock the 2026 “choice”

Write a **contradiction dossier**, three short intimate situations, and a dependency map before creating his next consequential wrong:
- To himself Cổn is a man who saved the family from collapse in 1996; to Thủy he is the one who allocated his failure to his daughters. His love for an unborn grandson need not be feigned, and **need not justify** sacrificing Thoa. A surviving father can truly love and materially harm his children.
- His claimed rationalist contempt for ghost stories is compatible with the 1996 outsourced ritual: he may have paid to **manage what frightened people will say/do**, not because he possessed occult knowledge. Whether he secretly feared Thoa even then is a nuanced character question and should not be declared prematurely.
- In 2026 he relies on other people's labor and filial deference to keep authority even as he ages. Explore the humiliating gap between private reliance on Thủy and public language of entitlement, without inventing illness or a new dependent wife.
- Map what he independently knows about Cường's last weeks, Toàn's debts and Bình's old part, and what he **does not** know about the night-13 scuffle, Thủy's fake operation or ghost Thoa's killing. No patriarchal omniscience.
- For his arc test a scene of ordinary family administration, a distinct one of a quiet/private relationship with Thủy and one social situation where he can either humiliate or protect a vulnerable relative. **These are test situations, not committed events.** Only after evidence of his contradiction on the page should a specific 2026 preventable choice be proposed for approval.
