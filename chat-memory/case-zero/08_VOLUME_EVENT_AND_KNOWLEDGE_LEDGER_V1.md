## E. Fresh original horror ecology — candidates ONLY

**Source boundary:** early major haunting by recently dead Cường, attached to **his house and familiar people/authority**, not omniscient global physics. Late real Thoa presence/order/killing is a separate causal source. **No return** to archived call/answer, steps, wet-footsteps, delayed mirror, or retired experimental scenes. The following are new *proposed situations*, each with an active ordinary job, witness access and a durable cost. No quota of one manifestation per movement and no mandatory re-use of motifs.

### H1 — "The unusable bed": mourning labor becomes physical danger

**Normal activity:** relatives must reorganize Cường's room after authorized handling; guests need sleeping space. Several people carry his old bed/frame while one volunteer directs and Cổn is occupied outside. **Phenomenon proposal:** when the movers shift the frame, a sudden concentrated human-like weight on an empty part forces them to drop it, trapping an arm/leg and injuring one real helper. No self-proving camera angle; witnesses feel/see different finite things. **Consequence:** guest space shrinks, volunteer unable to finish funeral labor, family pays costs, disagreements over whether furniture was already unstable. The bed is later treated as structurally suspect but never reveals a "ghost physics" law. **Narrative pressure:** new dead Cường still seems to assert ownership of what he occupied; Toàn's relationship with the genuine affectionate father complicates his fear. **Risk:** if this reads as generic haunted furniture, move the dangerous load from furniture to a genuinely current mourning task and keep the after-effect.

### H2 — "The kitchen route": believing fake scares are solved creates a fresh injury

**Normal activity:** after some fake audio/photo trickery of Thủy is exposed, Lan Anh and a relative clean up and carry scalding water/food along a familiar, lit route within home. **Phenomenon proposal:** Lan Anh experiences a momentarily impossible return to the same newly cleaned interior when she believed she had already stepped into the courtyard. A witness outside only sees her hesitate then fall; there is no narrator-established repeatable house-teleportation technology. **Consequence:** scalded skin, divided witness testimony, an altered cooking/care routine at a time the household must feed people. The family cannot dismiss everything as Thủy's remote audio; rational doubt about accident still remains plausible to outsiders. **Risk:** if the physical staging is too vague/cinematic, replace the event rather than assert an unlimited spatial-bending power.

### H3 — "The father sees his son": Cổn's private break, without a universal rite

**Normal activity:** after some of the historical truth comes into focus, Cổn performs one of the old household jobs he insists only he can do (perhaps sorting funeral/ancestral household goods; **exact job must be scene-designed**). He has genuinely solved other people's practical problems but refuses the idea that the house is occupied by the dead. **Phenomenon proposal:** he encounters an unmistakably Cường-shaped presence in a painful/familiar familial relation, not via words calling from stairs or a mirror. Strong candidate sensory design: the son performs a plain domestic action silently, while his damaged neck/head physically cannot maintain the orientation a living person would need; father is forced to remain present long enough to recognize both the man he raised and an impossibility of that body. **Consequence:** Cổn can no longer honestly deny his own experience yet might publicly cling to face; a practical action such as sponsoring Bình's bounded ritual follows the **already approved** late tendency without asserting ritual automatically works. **Risk:** grotesquerie alone does not define Cổn's change; build the human moment before overt sight and follow with daily behavioral evidence rather than shouting.

### H4 — "Returning for the living": independent after-truth household climax

**Normal activity:** AFTER G6a and substantial G6b, Lan Anh must recover her personal belongings or move out while the older family still uses the house; Toàn returns because he cannot accept losing the marriage/old paternal home; Thủy must assist a real family obligation, Cổn resists others acting without his permission. The exact practical trigger and Cổn's final new act remain **OPEN** until his character dossier is author-approved.

**Phenomenon proposal:** the ghost's force escalates from oppressive presence and physical interference into an overt event that imperils **at least one already deeply established recurring person** in a constrained part of Cường's familiar home. Candidate, not frozen: someone becomes trapped inside an old household storage/work room while belongings/family records are being moved; the dead Cường is seen at human distance by different participants with mutually limited perceptions, forcing them to choose between abandoning a person and returning to a danger they cannot explain. A documented physical cost and post-event need for help remain; no obligatory additional death is created without explicit author approval.

**Human crisis:** Cổn faces a son whose controlling habits partly replicate his own; Toàn chooses whether to protect Lan Anh at the expense of his advantageous version of his fight; Thủy refuses another command to remain silent, but her courage is not a magical spell. They make interdependent actions to physically rescue someone. **Ghost endpoint:** an observed withdrawal happens, but narrator does not assert that testimony, confession, funeral rite or forgiveness is a general off-switch. Cường has an autonomous late crisis despite already substantial human truth; Thoa's vengeance is a separate objective event.

**Intensity criterion:** the last sequence should be viscerally more frightening, socially harder and more physically dangerous than H1, not merely louder or longer. If its staging cannot meet that standard, rebuild from a concrete family task rather than import rejected test effects.

### H5 — "Thoa as a person, not an answer machine": G6c reveal form

**Normal narrative task:** after G6a/G6b have earned the buried personhood, reveal the supernatural author-only truth without making police wrong for lacking it. **Candidate form:** very short objective/spectral perspective revisits mùng-10 radio commission and the ~22:54 moment, showing why the personal repaired/drawn radio detail mattered to Thoa and confirming that she herself killed Cường. No monologue explaining netherworld law; no automatic redemption or enforced forgiveness. **Consequence:** the reader's understanding of the opening and death changes; the living characters' legal evidence and independent Cường haunting continue on their own terms. **Placement recommendation:** near the beginning of the final movement BEFORE the last Cường haunting crisis if it strengthens autonomous supernatural stakes; if it deflates uncertainty, split into two concise authorial confirmations on either side. Author must select the precise reveal and POV allocation before prose.
