# Case Zero / Volume — Causal Engine Proposal v1

**Status:** NEW DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL / **AWAITING AUTHOR**. Written 2026-09-25 after D-008. This file does **not** modify approved canon, fix word/chapter count, or make its invented incidents historical facts. Do not treat as approved author decisions or as a writer's source.

## Editorial engine

The central causal idea: **a family survives an old crime not by one continuous lie but by repeatedly turning that lie into new duties for others.** The 2026 suspects hide separate real actions that aggravate each other's conflicts. Long pursues finite evidence, Khanh participates only through his family's shop and lawful/social context. Ghost Cường has a distinct domestic-horror plot that survives exposure of Thủy's fake hauntings; ghost Thoa's order/killing is later author-confirmed, never derived from elimination of living suspects.

Keep three self-sustaining plots (2026 death, Cường haunting, 1996–2026 family costs); keep G6a credible identification, G6b independently supported historical responsibility and G6c separate narrative supernatural truth. Give readers many more chapters with lived-in people, natural unrelated events and authentic gaps between clue access and recognition, **without** resolving the volume with an exposition dump.

## Proposed dated human causality — not yet fact

A friend/employer may retain a seemingly unimportant artifact, not crime evidence. Show Thoa affectionate and imperfect toward Thủy. Wedding funding/forced marriage remains the canon cause.
- **Chronology gate:** Toàn is approximately 30 in 2026 while Cường marries in summer 1996. To make exact ages cohere, decide story's 2026 lunar month, Toàn's birthday and whether Toàn's mother was already pregnant at Cường's wedding (a high-value OPTIONAL candidate, not a fact). If pregnant, its social pressure reinforces—but must not justify—the 1996 abuse.
- **1996–early 2000s:** Cổn sells/pawns resources and borrows for Cường's wedding after losing hoped-for marriage proceeds. Map original creditor, amounts and land-use documentation *before* writing contracts. Thủy contributes through several forms of labor; a lender's independent books might later confirm transactions but not the murder.
- **2000s–2010s:** Some original debt is repaid. Family continually rebrands the extraction as rent, medical/help obligations, unpaid care and filial duty, while Thủy makes real attempts at moving away, earning, asking advice or protecting relationships. Choose 2–3 distinct milestones; avoid 30 years of one impossible unchanged loan. Never imply the present factory existed in 1996.
- **Late 2010s–2026:** Toàn learns *some* older concealed-death information through a specific, credibly sourced episode involving Cường or Cổn, but not the fatal shove or every grave detail. Define his knowledge precisely enough to explain why he later bans Thoa's name but Lan Anh does not know. Preserve Cường's genuine affection and Toàn's dependency/resentment.
- **Before mùng 10, 2026:** Pond-edge survey/work physically compromises old burial structure; property stakes renew extraction. Proposed 2026 Cổn choice: when a worker mentions unexpectedly disturbed bank fill and asks for a pause/check, Cổn suppresses scrutiny or pressures Cường to proceed, foregoing a real chance to stop renewed concealment. Exact report, competence and legal obligations need validation.
- **After the death:** A legal investigation checks the modern death, while independent economic/personal/documentary sources eventually motivate competent inquiry into old remains. Skull trauma (if scientifically identifiable), witness memories, dated social/work traces, and separately verified statements support only their own propositions, not certainty that a particular person struck a fatal blow.

## Thủy option A — proposed earlier-trip variants

Thủy asks him to recognize her contributions to the family's asset or show an old household account; argument intensifies, she takes/photographs a limited item or retains a torn account fragment in anger. Another fragment/witness may establish a daylight visit without proving murder-time presence. She fears exposing her threats or possible unauthorized taking. Source and custody need exact design. This turns *her legitimate claim and culpable intimidation* into a conflict, not a murderer cosplay.

**A2: an earlier clandestine document inspection** in a genuinely plausible unattended room; an innocuous physical rearrangement is later misdated by someone. Strong mystery potential, weaker interpersonal character scene and needs a real access route (no magically working thirty-year-old key).

**A3: a final failed effort to stop her own fabricated scares / remove props**, in which Cường catches her and interprets it as murder preparation. Strong fake-vs-real haunting dialectic, but weaker direct connection to her economic story unless combined with a human bargain. Avoid mechanical proof via an implausible timestamp.

For A1 illustrative timing ONLY: afternoon mùng 13 visit well before 19:00 candidate shift; enough realistic geographic travel/buffer to have coworker-observed ongoing labor at 22:54; leave work after notification, never arrive before police simply to recover a seized phone. **Do not lock exact hours or travel distance until actual fictional xóm/market-to-factory map and employer shift regime are selected.** D-008 factual earlier visit and true death-window alibi remain fixed whatever variant.

## Other logic gates

**Bình:** choose and stage his new 2026 contact with Cường; separate memory of partial ritual work, contemporary fear leverage, what he suspects, and what he can prove. If he knows about a body, require a new finite source and timing.

**Toàn:** a staged two-step learning path may work: an overheard evasive comment in youth, then a specific later slip from Cường when land is discussed. He can know Thoa died and was concealed *without* witnessing or knowing who caused the head injury. Avoid inherited omniscience.

**Nails:** Toàn's sight and subjective horror are fixed. Whether any laboratory trace is found, or why the nails look abnormally clean, are separate propositions. No DNA result establishes ghosts, excludes every human act or precisely dates skin transfer; do not dictate a supernatural mechanism prematurely.

**Grandfather:** his 1996 ethical refusal of an opaque coercive job is fact. A current shop accepting a paid paper-radio order is an ordinary commercial transaction; any alleged prohibition is attributed character belief and should not be narrator-certified doctrine.

**Calendar/land/location:** determine the lunar month and summer/weather continuity, Toàn's exact age, fictional Chợ Bến/xóm Trại placement and travel radius before minute-level story timing. Avoid stating that a legally unproven 1996 pledge of family pond land proves today's ownership. The 1993 Land Law had a dated land-use-rights regime; details of title/creditor/mortgage require specialist checking. The 2025 local/provincial restructuring affects 2026 official administrative titles and locality wording.

## Side-story and structural candidate network

**Market testimony:** an independently resolved Chợ Bến commercial conflict shows how witness accounts honestly diverge according to prior context; later Lan Anh's public refusal is reinterpreted without a character delivering a lecture. Give that subplot its own cost to a merchant/customer.

**Factory's shared shift:** a coworker's own wages/rota dispute shows Thủy's real relationships and work competence and produces naturally available *multiple* alibi sources, never one magic swipe. Her work story must matter absent the killing.

**Another votive-shop commission:** living relatives disagree about who has authority to decide for a dead loved one's possessions, with its own emotional or supernatural outcome; thematic echo to Thoa without an extra evidence cache.

**Khanh's ongoing engineering life:** external deadline and a separate data/motive misattribution cost Khanh something, but confer no police/telecom access; he stays anchored in real mother–son shop obligations and platonic Mai friendship.

**Interlocks:** exposure of Thủy's false sounds makes Toàn falsely assume he has explained all haunting, risking Lan Anh; disclosure of Toàn's torn shirt makes him divert suspicion onto Bình; exposing Bình's blackmail pushes independent inquiry toward the disturbed pond without granting him an explanatory monologue; earlier-visit records eventually clear Thủy only for murder-time presence, *not* intimidation or concealed acts. Not all subplots need to tie into this graph; preserve truly unrelated life.

## Horror plot — candidate dramatic functions, no retired concrete scene recycling

Early Cường manifestations should alter real household logistics and relationships, then move into bodily risk/social division, then worsen after human fake scares have been exposed. Initial ghost-denying Cổn has a later personal encounter; he responds in a way that renews social control and worsens family trust. Give the final Cường confrontation its own peak *after* credible 1996 human truth, with observable withdrawal but no universal exorcism recipe, no coercive forgiveness and no paranormal courtroom proof. Invent specific manifestation events at a later dedicated **fresh ecology ideation** pass: ordinary task, precise space, bodies at risk, what each witness can actually perceive, immediate practical damage, ongoing consequence. No scare quota; do not lift or remodel old D-002/audit/test examples.
