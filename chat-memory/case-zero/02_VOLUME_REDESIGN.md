# Approved editorial architecture — not a finalized scene list

**Approval:** author accepted the complete *recommendations* on 2026-09-25. Concrete new episodes, cast, horror manifestations, outcomes, chapter counts and length require specific causal design before being treated as story facts.

## Why the old Case Zero skeleton felt shallow

- The three suspect tracks were too neat and symmetrical: Toàn's ripped shirt and fight, Thủy's threatening campaign, Bình's ash and blackmail. Instead, let their **genuine distinct wrongs interact**: exposing one worsens another's position and motivates further dangerous actions; a real alibi is not a magic acquittal for other wrongdoing.
- The 1996 crime was powerful but the **thirty intervening years** insufficiently lived. Develop Thoa's specific independent future, peers, warmth, faults, music and aspirations, the real borrowing/repayment succession, Thủy's attempted autonomy and reasons for renewed silence, generations that benefited and chose to re-conceal.
- Former P1–P6 knowledge gates were too easy to turn into a chapter-by-chapter clue ladder. Keep epistemic fairness, free the volume to follow autonomous stories and return later to newly reinterpreted details.
- Ghost **Cường** must have a proper primary domestic-horror plot rather than fill time before discovery of Thoa's body. Manifestations arise freshly from household ecology and damage sleep, care, livelihood, social trust and physical safety, with independent consequences.
- Ending cannot rely on remains/1996 reconstruction/ghost confirmation delivered as one end-of-book explanation. Give substantial human truth space **before** the ultimate moral and supernatural crisis.

## Three interleaving, individually compelling tracks

1. **2026 mystery:** lawful Long investigation and distinct, limited Khanh shop/community observations; witnesses and evidence are partial, sources/custody realistic, multiple false explanations have temporary causal strength.
2. **Cường's escalating haunting:** a deep supernatural story about a dead patriarch's familiar home and relational control, with several qualitatively different modes of terror and its own climax. Unmasking Thủy's human stagecraft does NOT exorcise actual Cường. Do not leak Thoa's objective spirit agency into early authorized narration.
3. **1996–2026 family/social tragedy:** survivor labor, property, obligations, denied alternatives, who knew what when, and the erasure of an actual person with a future, not merely a corpse/twist.

All three need their own decisions, irreversible turns and dramatic stakes.

## Five large *flexible* dramatic movements (not chapters)

- **Nhà có tang:** morning-14 shop news and mùng-10 paper order; public Chợ Bến refusal spreads social consequences; family horror has an early practical intrusion.
- **Mỗi nhà một lời khai:** ordinary household/work/funeral life; Toàn/Thủy/Bình lie about separate concrete conduct, and different reasonable interpretations cause one another harm; domestic haunting develops an autonomous pressure sequence.
- **Những món nợ không có tên:** independent alibi checks reveal Thủy's true death-window location *without* erasing earlier threat/visit; expand intergenerational obligations and survival; fear shreds cooperation.
- **Đầm sen:** credible multi-source path to search and properly paced remains identification; Cổn gets a personal paranormal encounter AFTER his earlier aggressive denial; investigation opens G6a and separately G6b on earned evidence.
- **Không ai nhận lễ:** after major human truths are already known, Cường-haunting's independent terminal crisis and hard human choices occur; G6c confirms for reader rather than producing admissible supernatural forensic proof; aftermath gives Thủy agency over paper radio and restores Thoa's memory without enforced forgiveness.

Approval of a five-movement **macro map** does not freeze chapter ordering, G6 subgate spacing, CH-01/CH-02 or any precise incident.

## Side-story candidates — assess for independent value, don't insert all by quota

A Chợ Bến social/commerce incident in which accounts diverge with each teller's partial history; a Yên Phong coworker shift/pay conflict through which Thủy emerges as a capable worker/person, not only suspect; a separate paper-offering job about living relatives' disagreement over the dead person's things; Khanh's real software-project obligations involving a human mistake about records, identity and motive. A side story must *work as its own small story* even if nobody later mentions Cường. A later pattern echo may enrich reader/character reasoning without becoming an artificial lesson, uncanny clue or Khanh's superpower. Include uninstrumental ordinary meals, banter, errands and time between plot turns.

## Five functions for escalating horror (not approved concrete manifestations)

1. Early event interrupts a legitimate task and leaves **an unavoidable practical consequence**.
2. Familiar care, funeral, work or night routines become genuinely unsafe/strained.
3. Explaining some fake scares makes new real paranormal events *less*, not more, manageable; frightened decisions worsen human danger.
4. Cổn experiences a late direct event that transforms his earlier categorical ghost-denial; his next bid to control the family deepens the crisis.
5. Even after buried human history substantially emerges, the Cường haunting reaches a separate confrontation with observed withdrawal and **no automatically validated religious or magical law**.

Derive novel scare ideas independently from each scene's task, space, bodily risk, group dynamics, active spiritual source and timing. Do NOT recycle sounds/calling/staircase test scenes or treat audit examples as required forms; avoid scare quotas, empty gloomy adjectives, expository narrator and ghosts that exist only to deliver information. Strong, natural scenes should carry sustained movement and imperfect northern-family speech.

## Deepen moral causality without bigger random atrocities

Make Thoa's lost future credible through living witnesses and tangible decisions; plot the actual original loan and successive shifts from debt to obligation rather than claiming an unchanged 30-year loan; precisely limit Toàn's inherited knowledge; give Cổn a 2026 opportunity to prevent a new repetition whose chosen failure could have its own irreversible cost. Preserve Thủy's guilt from true actions alongside victimhood and her real alibi. Final moral action is not a mastermind confession, one magic tape, or a one-night all-party solve. New details in this paragraph are development **assignments**, not already occurring events.
