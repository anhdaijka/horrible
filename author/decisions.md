# Author Decision Log — Horrible

Decisions below were explicitly accepted during discovery. Future agents must not casually reopen them. Story Skills canon has not yet been initialized.

### D-001 — Project identity

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** `Horrible` is a long-running Northern Vietnam horror / spiritual / ghost novel with a strong mystery component.
- **Reason:** The project is inspired by the author's lived familiarity with spiritual stories and belief, but is fiction rather than memoir.
- **Implications:** Personal experience may inform worldview, emotional truth and texture without requiring literal biographical reproduction.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-002 — Horror-first genre hierarchy

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Horror / spiritual atmosphere is primary; mystery is structural support and must not dominate the novel's overall language.
- **Reason:** The author does not want detective procedural logic to displace ghost, horror and spiritual experience.
- **Implications:** Mystery must sharpen the horror, never sterilize it. Solving mechanism does not necessarily solve haunting.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-003 — Protagonist Khanh

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** The protagonist is Khanh, male, 27, a software engineer who has just returned from several years of onsite work in Japan and will resume work at the Hanoi branch after a few weeks at home.
- **Reason:** Engineering logic creates productive tension with a family environment long exposed to spiritual belief and stories.
- **Implications:** Khanh remains a real working engineer and skeptical believer; no confirmed supernatural ability early.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-004 — Khanh's Japan background

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh experienced several strange or possibly spiritual episodes in Japan. He was never sure whether they were stress, nightmares, projection, coincidence or something local and supernatural. When he called home, his parents tended to reassure or deny rather than deepen his fear.
- **Implications:** Japan is background pressure and may become a later arc, but the novel does not open by making Japan the main supernatural premise.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-005 — Khanh's epistemic flaw and moral arc

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh strongly wants coherent causal explanations. Over time he learns that knowing the truth creates moral responsibility but does not always create a clean obligation to disclose it.
- **Implications:** A future major arc will involve Khanh withholding a terrible truth from an innocent family, then later being forced into sustained contact with one of their descendants or relatives. The secrecy gradually eats at him.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-006 — Khanh's long ethical direction

- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Khanh should ultimately continue doing his ordinary work well while becoming more willing to help others and “tích đức” through action rather than cynical dismissal.
- **Implications:** Virtue is not an RPG reward system; good actions do not guarantee supernatural protection or luck.
- **Supersedes:** none

- **Implications:** The shop is a strong recurring home base and information node, but not the exclusive source of cases.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-047 — Case Zero 30-Year Dual-Layer Catalyst

- **Date:** 2026-09-10
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Establish the dual-layer mechanism explaining why Case Zero erupts after exactly thirty years (1996 $\rightarrow$ 2026):
  1. *Physical / Environmental layer:* In early 2026, the district initiates road-widening and canal box-culverting that begins draining and filling the old lotus pond in xóm Trại right beside ông Cường's rear bedroom, threatening to unearth the buried Czech cassette radio and prompting a living third party to launch an urgent pressure campaign against Cường.
  2. *Folk spiritual expiration layer:* Following Thoa's 1996 drowning, Khanh's grandfather refused the family's request to bind the spirit (`Không nhận lễ`). The family hired a heterodox specialist to bury the cassette radio alongside a cinnabar-inscribed green bamboo water-binding talisman (*cọc tre khắc bùa trấn thủy*). In folk tradition, bamboo buried in anaerobic mud rots across roughly three decades (30 years). In winter 2026, the rotting talisman expires just as the excavator strikes the pond edge, releasing the displaced spirit to reclaim her radio and seek retribution before the pond is paved over.
- **Implications:** Synergizes detective reality (infrastructure works, unearthing physical relic) with Northern folk dread (30-year talisman decay, grandfather's moral integrity). Preserves anti-jumpscare discipline and re-read value.
- **Supersedes:** none

### D-048 — Case Zero Deep Master Architecture: Tri-Layer Sin, False Victimization, and Supernatural Retribution

- **Date:** 2026-09-10
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Establish the deep multi-layered narrative architecture of Case Zero combining historical human crime, living greed/revenge, and authentic supernatural vengeance ("Direction D"):
  1. *Historical Crime & Murder Tape (1996):* Vũ Thị Thoa's drowning was not an accidental tragedy, but a collective family crime. During a violent quarrel over forced marriage and money, Thoa was pushed and held down in the lotus pond mud. Her Czech cassette radio, running on record mode (`REC`) on the bank, captured the confrontation, family curses, and Thoa's dying cries. Cường hid the radio rather than burning it, using it as leverage against the family while sealing it beneath the pond with a 30-year bamboo talisman.
  2. *Living Conspirator & Inheritance Greed (2026):* Surviving sister Vũ Thị Thủy was an accomplice who spent 30 years exploited as an unmarried garment worker while Cường enjoyed the estate. In early 2026, when the road project turns the family land into multi-billion-dong real estate and Cường attempts to cut Thủy out of the inheritance, Thủy unleashes a revenge campaign. Weaponizing Cường's guilt and fear of Thoa's ghost, Thủy stalks Cường, plays tape recordings by the pond at night, and sends the slashed photo threat to drive Cường into a stroke/breakdown and claim the land.
  3. *Supernatural Retribution ("Kẻ mượn ma bị ma nuốt chửng"):* Thủy's scheme unintentionally triggers real cosmic and spiritual retribution. Coinciding with the rotting 30-year talisman and the excavator disturbing the pond bed, Thoa's genuine ghost awakens with terrifying wrath. On the stairs on the night of the 13th, while Thủy planned to merely scare Cường, Thoa's spirit actually manifests and snaps Cường's neck 180 degrees (*RẮC!*). In Chapter 5-6, Thủy stages the phone call and seizure to manufacture a spiritual alibi, but is horrified to realize Thoa's ghost has physically followed the paper radio into the room and now targets Thủy as the surviving murderer.
  4. *Fair-Play Detective Clues for Khanh:* Khanh detects subtle human inconsistencies in Thủy's behavior and timeline (bus ticket times, dried pond mud on her sandals, typewriter/handwriting style), while being confronted by undeniable supernatural facts that shatter mundane reductionism (the left-handed Dahua CCTV entity, the dry paper radio's mechanical tape hiss, the high-window pond mud handprint, the mid-air broken neck on the recording).
- **Implications:** Elevates Case Zero into a profound tragedy of Northern rural moral decay and greed colliding with relentless spiritual judgment. Solves the risk of simple ghost proceduralism by making the human conspirator's own trap turn against her.
- **Supersedes:** Any prior assumption that cô Thủy was an entirely passive, innocent victim or that Cường's death was a pure accidental trip without human provocation.

### D-049 — Case Zero 12-Chapter Slow-Burn Architecture & Multi-Suspect Web

- **Date:** 2026-09-10
- **Status:** accepted development decision
- **Decision:** Expand Case Zero (`Đơn hàng cho người chưa chết`) from an 8-chapter brief arc into a comprehensive 12-chapter slow-burn spiritual mystery novella (Chapters 1–12, ~48,000–50,000 words total):
  1. *The 3-Stage Pendulum Swings (Cơ chế con lắc nhận thức):*
     - Chapters 1–6 (Established): Supernatural anomaly & escalating dread (order for living man, CCTV left hand, tape hiss, broken vertebrae, 38-second Zalo recording).
