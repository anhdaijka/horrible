# Creative Boundaries — Horrible v2

These are project-specific constraints for `anhdaijka/horrible@main` after the opening reset.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not turn the novel into detective procedural fiction with ghosts as decoration.
- Do not turn Khanh into a chosen one, hereditary sorcerer, omnipotent medium, demon hunter or escalating supernatural fighter without explicit future author approval.
- Do not let technical skill become magical access to police, telecom, banking or private databases.
- Do not let spiritual practitioners function as infallible exposition machines.
- Do not equate religion or folk practice with evil cultism by default.
- Real Vietnamese beliefs and living traditions must be researched respectfully; plausible-sounding invention is not acceptable as cultural fact.
- Do not confuse cultural caution with metaphysical timidity: fictional ghosts and supernatural events may genuinely exist.
- Do not convert personal family inspiration into literal biography unless the author explicitly chooses to do so.

## Genre-promise boundary

The opening and each major arc must remember what the reader bought: **a ghost / spiritual horror novel**.

Do not:

- spend multiple early chapters proving ordinary life is ordinary before delivering horror;
- resolve the first meaningful scare mundanely merely to demonstrate skepticism;
- let every supernatural beat become only a clue-delivery device;
- repeatedly neutralize tension with narrator reassurance;
- hide the genre so completely that the opening reads as slice-of-life.

A chapter may be quiet, funny or domestic, but the early novel must maintain a disturbing question, supernatural promise or emotional/horror pressure.

## Mystery boundaries

- A case may be solved, mostly solved, partially solved, unresolved, dormant, intentionally ambiguous or wrongly closed.
- Some cases may also be **author-confirmed supernatural**.
- Unresolved cases still require designed causal possibilities and evidence discipline.
- Coincidence may trigger attention but cannot become a lazy solution.
- Everyday-life insight may reframe evidence; it must not conjure missing evidence.
- Reader-facing clues should remain fair when a later reveal depends on them.
- Police and professionals retain plausible competence. Khanh should not routinely outperform trained investigators at their own jobs.
- **Do not give Khanh unearned forensic knowledge or an answer-dumping monologue.** Local inference from available facts is allowed in any phase.
- **Do not place Khanh unnaturally at private scenes between suspects.** If a scene is private to Vũ family members or suspects with no plausible reason for Khanh's presence → choose objective or an appropriate limited POV (see DIR-03).

## POV boundary — D-008

Khanh anchors recurring experience; narrator may widen, summarize, retrospect, enter the chosen limited viewpoint or transition to another. Objective camera is optional, not the mode of all scenes without Khanh. No numeric allocations or prohibition of all exposition/interiority.

Observed facts stay true; POV interpretation can mislead fairly. No objective false innocence, false innocent interiority or deletion of knowledge being directly thought about. Select scope/distance/time fairly. A late narrative return may confirm planted cause; no unseeded major motive. Do not force Khanh to witness private scenes.

## Supernatural boundaries

- Do not over-define cosmology early.
- Do not create rigid RPG-like rules, levels, species charts or power scaling by default.
- **Ambiguity is not mandatory.**
- An apparition, haunting, dream, possession-like event or impossible encounter may be genuinely supernatural.
- A confirmed supernatural event may still have an unknown mechanism.
- The dead are not automatically truthful, benevolent, evil or omniscient.
- A human mechanism may coexist with supernatural residue.
- Some apparent hauntings should be human.
- Some encounters should remain unknowable.
- The series should vary these outcomes so the reader cannot predict the metaphysical answer from house style.

## Tone boundaries

- Dread and atmosphere are important, but explicit horror is allowed when earned.
- Classic horror tools are not banned merely because they are familiar. Avoid generic execution, not the tools themselves.
- Gore is low-to-moderate by default but may become stronger when consequence requires it.
- Preserve humor, work, food, errands, family calls and social friction as human counterpoint.
- Do not use ordinary humor to puncture every frightening beat.
- Do not keep every scene dark, solemn or mystical.
- Do not keep every scene neutral, tasteful or emotionally flattened either.

## Information-order boundary

Characters may only reason from information they plausibly have at that moment unless the narrator deliberately gives the reader a wider frame.

Do not leak future reveals by:

- explaining the natural explanation before the anomaly is known;
- making a character ask the exact question that only becomes meaningful later;
- reassuring the reader that a suspicious detail is harmless;
- narrating why an event is not strange before anyone has reason to consider it strange.

Foreshadowing should create a question. It should not answer tomorrow's question today.

## Character outcomes requiring explicit author approval

- death of core recurring cast
- resurrection
- irreversible betrayal
- major romance commitment or breakup
- identity reveal that changes a core relationship
- confirmed permanent supernatural ability
- permanent power or ability change
- Khanh abandoning software engineering to become a spiritual professional
- grandfather being revealed as a villain or master architect of the series

## Plot mechanisms to avoid by default

- secret agency that conveniently handles the supernatural
- inherited grimoire / ultimate ritual manual
- `all cases were connected to one ancient demon`
- prophecy that removes character agency
- dream as a universally reliable oracle or cheat code
- every ghost scene delivering clues
- every apparent accident becoming murder
- CCTV / AI enhancement producing impossible forensic certainty
- convenient police-friend database lookups
- escalating magical combat as the main resolution engine
- **Khanh delivering an unearned monologue that exceeds his information or spoon-feeds the complete case solution** (Anti-Detective rule, DIR-05)
- **Forced purple-prose comparisons that import crime/forensic language as metaphor** (e.g., hoa gạo như máu, tiếng gió như cuống họng bị bóp) (DIR-05)

## Scope boundary

The series may expand geographically and thematically, including Japan, but should remain grounded in human-scale horror, spirituality, memory, wrongdoing, grief, social life and mystery.

Expansion should deepen fear, culture and consequence rather than simply increase supernatural power levels.

## Case Zero — current boundaries (D-008)

- People believe Cường's hanging is suicide; narrator does not certify the mistaken conclusion. Stress is real, not a medical diagnosis or proof of intent.
- Thắng deliberately kills; reader-facing companion/friend first, late reveal. Motive is work/financial pressure, separate from Thoa/pond land. Help and fear can be real.
- Thoa1996 backbone remains, independent of murder2026. No mandatory living-recipient ghost order, Thoa execution, clean-nail erasure or G6a–G6c certification route.
- Real Cường haunting persists, memory fragmented across everyday matters and reveal. No ghost reporter, selective last-minute lucidity or perfect supernatural justice.
- Lan Anh discovers hanging and calls help promptly; Toàn has prior scuffle; Thủy has real work shift. No fake-alibi burglary/oil/handprint branch. Character concealment grows from blame/relations/fear, not forensic suspect lineup.
- Discovery uses experience, relations, memory/work and fair narration. Long/police/autopsy/DNA/camera are not engine, prerequisites or pending readiness gates. Retired technical proposals do not resurrect themselves as research work.
- Exact times/routes/room, sums/supporting-character names, manifestations and end-state not chosen remain open. No automatic extra death, sentence, land allocation or permanent exorcism.
- Past candidates/approved packets stay preserved but stale for the new design. Source sync does not canonize prose.
