## Intent and proposal

The author sets intent: what they want this part to explore or make the reader experience. The agent reads relevant established sources and develops a concrete proposed sequence: who wants what, how relationships and circumstances lead to choices, what changes, and how the telling unfolds. Explain consequential choices in ordinary language. Offer alternatives when there is a real tradeoff; no mandatory number of options, engines or questions.

The author selects the direction before drafting. Status AWAITING_AUTHOR_CHOICE means that this proposal awaits a choice, not that the author must write a beat sheet. Approval persists. A supplied sequence is binding within its scope; a newly approved proposal may replace it explicitly. The current CH-01 outer sequence remains approved while its embedded ghost story awaits a new proposal.

Resolve established facts from sources. Ask only when a new choice changes approved purpose/outcome, chronology/location, reveal order, durable relationships/state or supernatural truth. Small actions, dialogue, incidental details, narrative distance and transitions inside the chosen direction are writer decisions.

## Working material

A new chapter workspace normally needs `brief.md` and versioned `draft-vN.md` files. The brief records intent, approved direction/approval basis, enough people/place/relationship context, necessary information limits, and source links. Include consequential dependencies; avoid repeating each constraint in multiple sections. Older packet formats remain readable but are no longer required.

Research, alternatives and review notes are created only when they carry useful work. No obligatory manifests, discovery slates, ecology files, QA cards or handoff reports. Existing artifacts are preserved. Draft identity is its exact path/version and, for acceptance, a computed hash; a separate locator file is optional.

## Chapter scale and reader experience

Author-approved default, 2026-11-04. Aim for the scale of CH-01, around 6,900 Story Skills word-count units, with a working range of 6,500–7,500. A substantially shorter chapter is a deliberate exception for author choice.

Before drafting, the brief states what the reader should know, feel and still wonder by the end. It establishes who wants what, what the viewpoint character actually knows, the main movement and the causal or horror stories that run alongside it. The usual shape is one main thread and about two side threads with their own aims, friction and consequences. A side story may pay off in the chapter, deepen a relationship or leave its own unease. It need not become a plot clue.

Slow burn builds through changes in meaning and behavior. Give ordinary activity time to matter, then show how the disturbance changes decisions after it occurs. The delivered draft is a complete chapter accompanied by a compact list of new durable facts and open questions, in its brief.

## Writing and self-edit

Write from the selected direction and sufficient story context. Complete a coherent draft, then read the whole and revise at the scale needed. Narration may summarize, explain, move through time, sustain speech and enter the approved viewpoint. Sentence length, gesture frequency, ambiguity and horror technique have no quotas. Do not turn every detail into evidence or every person into a plot messenger.

The author's priorities are deep psychology/relationships, story organization, natural Vietnamese narration and dialogue. Scope-specific author feedback lives in session-state/brief and is read by an independent reviewer only after the initial prose reading. Details may create voice, pleasure, attachment or atmosphere without advancing plot; evaluate their cumulative effect.

Do not copy wording, signature voice or story incidents into Horrible. Reference findings are observations to test, not compulsory rules.

## Reading and repair

Before chapter acceptance, a reader independent of generation reads the complete exact draft without the brief, author diagnosis or writer rationale. Record a short first reading, then consult sources for context and factual checks. A previously exposed reader must disclose that limit and cannot claim a fresh reading. One independent reader can perform both literary and factual phases; separate agents/reports are not mandatory.

Feedback identifies consequential reader-facing problems, cites passages and explains effects. Preserve strengths. Distinguish factual contradictions from literary judgment and optional experiments. No automatic defect from matching a historic phrase, technique or signature; no total-score substitute for judgment.

Logic checking covers motive, physical/temporal causality, relationships, knowledge/reveal boundaries, and real-world claims actually used. The writer repairs the diagnosed situation while preserving effective voice, rhythm and relationships. Re-read changed passages in context; consequential rewrites need renewed independent reading of their effects.

## Acceptance and continuity

Show the exact complete chapter and a concise account of new durable facts needing acceptance. Author acceptance must apply to that version and those facts. Independent literary and factual checks must be complete with no unresolved material issue; they may share one note. Keep the accepted hash and approval/review basis in the chapter record or brief.

Then promote prose and update only approved continuity/knowledge/registry changes. Changed prose or consequential source dependencies invalidate affected reviews. A local trial cannot be promoted as a chapter. Candidate creation is never automatic acceptance.
