# Review and repair

Use docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md as a reader-oriented standard, not a scorecard.

Literary review records a blind first reading from prose plus locator-only identity, then a contextual diagnosis using the generative packet and qa-card. Evaluate only dimensions material to the actual reading experience and work's scope. Prioritize root causes over an exhaustive inventory. Mandatory audit against the Negative Craft Matrix in `docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md`: any presence of full-name narrative spam, POV head-hopping, pseudo-intellectual tell, purple prose, or AI cinematic endings MUST be classified as MATERIAL_DEFECT (requiring REVISE_REQUIRED) and cannot be given a PASS. Classify findings as BLOCKER, MATERIAL_DEFECT or OPTIONAL_EXPERIMENT; an optional preference cannot by itself produce REVISE_REQUIRED. Record concrete protected strengths so revision does not flatten what already works. Diagnose when every person/action exists only to carry plot, when ordinary life is merely decorative, when mystery data displaces the ghost-horror experience, or when a character explains deductions the reader should make. At a consequential dependency, state a credible competing reading only when it breaks intended knowledge or later behavior. No requirement that every line or scene accomplish every function; no mandatory subplot or clue count.

Anti-regression mandate: before issuing any PASS or completing a review, cross-reference the candidate prose against `docs/failure-memory.md`. This is not a checkbox pass — read the actual quoted rejected text in failure-memory and verify that no passage in the candidate shares its structural signature. A pattern that matches a failure-memory anti-pattern signature (same mechanism, different words) is a regression and must be logged as MATERIAL_DEFECT regardless of how the wording differs from the rejected original. Patterns documented in failure-memory represent author-validated evidence of recurring craft failure on this specific project; they carry higher diagnostic weight than generic craft principles.

Safety review verifies hard facts and knowledge against authority sources, including kinship and address. It flags impossible knowledge, incompatible later action/state or a dropped material obligation—not the absence of a visible reaction. Transient interactions and chapter-local life are not canon defects merely because no source prescribed them; evaluate whether they create a contradiction or durable obligation. A fact with a literary consequence is shared between lanes: flag the contradiction in safety and repair its whole interaction in revision. Do not send it back and forth as someone else's responsibility.

A substantial scene rewrite is permitted within approved outcomes and durable boundaries. Local canon repair must still sound human. Correcting a pronoun alone does not repair a missing relationship.

Both reviews name the exact candidate path, SHA-256 and consequential authority basis used. Any prose change invalidates old PASS. A changed decision/source dependency invalidates the affected part of a review even when prose bytes are unchanged; recheck only the affected scope and its consequences. Independent full reading is required before acceptance; disclose contaminated context.

When a prior candidate exposed a recurring failure, review the mechanism-level regression test without treating the old wording or repair as a model. A changed workspace or restarted draft does not itself close the risk.
