# Context routing

Use sufficient context for the people and situation, not a fixed token or fact quota.

Operational role contracts are instructions, not story-source payload. They remain active even when the author restricts a Writer trial to particular packet files. A request to read only writing-brief.md, scene-ecology.md and character-context.md means do not load additional story, audit, review or historical sources; it does not disable the positive composition prior in the active Writer contract.

Preflight/safety read the applicable author sources, character relationships, continuity, scene design, verified research, and plot/di-tuong-registry.md where required. Separate durable facts, approved design and optional craft advice.

Writer context has two phases. During the generative pass, story sources are limited to writing-brief.md, scene-ecology.md, character-context.md, relevant accepted preceding prose and explicitly named safe lived-context excerpts. Discovery, preflight reasoning, QA, Failure Memory, rejected engines/prose, reviews, regression history, full directives, style bibles and generic craft lexicons stay out until the appropriate later phase.

The purpose of the bounded source set is not minimalism. It is to let the Writer inhabit a complete living situation without audit contamination. If the packet lacks a relationship, practical fact, material condition or POV-life detail necessary to imagine the scene, that is a packet/compiler defect; Preflight should project the missing safe context instead of making the Writer guess from a generic model prior.

After a complete generative draft exists, open qa-card.md and only the named hard-source excerpts needed for self-edit. Failure Memory is opened only after an actual recurring defect has been diagnosed.

Literary review has two phases. For the first phase, read only a locator-only candidate record and the complete prose. Do not read session-state, writer handoff, prior findings, packet, rationale or repair proposal before recording the reading experience. Then consult session-state, character context, relevant brief and authority basis to diagnose intention versus execution. Preserve the first-read findings. Prior exposure to prose, brief, rationale, diagnosis or drafting must be disclosed; a new message or model is not a fresh context.

Reviser reads the candidate, character context, explicit author feedback and every available review; consult exact authority sources when needed. A requested revision may proceed with a missing review lane if that absence is recorded, but promotion remains blocked.

Generic craft skills are diagnostic aids. Upstream writing, dialogue, prose-style or revision skills are explicit-only and may not replace the Horrible role workflow or mutate canon directly.
