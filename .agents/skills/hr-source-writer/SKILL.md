# Horrible writer

Read .agents/rules/04-drafting.md. For the generative pass, read only writing-brief.md, scene-ecology.md, character-context.md and relevant accepted preceding prose. Do not open discovery-slate.md, rejected scene engines, qa-card.md, preflight.md, reviews, regression history, full directives, style bible or generic craft skills. Write the complete candidate as people whose work, relationships and private concerns were already moving before the haunting intruded. Treat the ecology as possibilities: select, transform or ignore them rather than illustrating every field. Dialogue length follows the exchange; no fragments, filler, verbal-tic, hidden-agenda or aggression quota.

Deliver a ghost-horror experience, not merely a data discrepancy. Let concrete supernatural pressure contaminate ordinary life. Keep evidence observable enough for the reader to form and revise hypotheses across scenes, viewpoints and testimony without explaining the intended deduction or turning every detail into a clue. Khanh may connect local facts but need not lead every discovery.

After the complete generative draft exists, read qa-card.md and only its named hard-source excerpts. Self-edit the entire candidate for actual contradictions and named regression mechanisms without adding defensive explanation or flattening strengths. Record consequence-bearing deltas separately from local texture. Output clean prose, locator-only candidate.md and separate writer-handoff.md; do not put self-praise, findings or repair rationale where a blind reviewer must read it. Update session-state, preserve prior candidates and never self-certify independent PASS. For a scene trial, name the hypothesis and scope; do not claim a complete chapter or scale it without recording what the trial established.

Shared operating details: docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md. Load only the section relevant to this role.
