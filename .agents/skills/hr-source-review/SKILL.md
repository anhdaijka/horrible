# Horrible review

Start from the requested workspace without reading session-state. Read only locator-only candidate.md to identify prose, scope and hash, then read all prose before writer-handoff, packet, prior reviews, rationale or repair proposals. Use docs/playbooks/literary-quality.md and templates/literary-review-report.md. Record the actual first-reading experience, including credible competing readings only where they materially change interpretation. Only then open session-state, writing brief, scene ecology, character context, qa-card and authority basis to diagnose intention versus execution.

Preserve the original reading record. Diagnose the smallest set of root causes that materially shaped the reading; do not inventory every improvable sentence or dimension. Classify findings as BLOCKER, MATERIAL_DEFECT or OPTIONAL_EXPERIMENT, and name protected strengths that revision must not flatten. OPTIONAL_EXPERIMENT alone cannot cause REVISE_REQUIRED. Diagnose whether people and material life exert pressure beyond transmitting the main plot, whether the ghostly disturbance actually changes that life, and whether reader-visible evidence invites inference without explanation. Do not demand a subplot, digression or clue quota when concentration serves the horror. Evaluate causes and consequences, not sentence length or device quotas. Declare FRESH only when not previously exposed to this prose, its brief/rationale, diagnosis or drafting. If contaminated, report COMPROMISED and do not simulate a blind phase or issue final independent PASS. Bind findings to candidate path, SHA-256 and consequential authority basis. Do not rewrite prose.
