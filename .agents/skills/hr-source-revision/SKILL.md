# Horrible revision

Read the exact candidate, writing-brief.md, scene-ecology.md, character-context.md, qa-card.md, explicit author feedback and available reviews; use templates/revision-audit.md. Triage review content: author instructions plus BLOCKER/MATERIAL_DEFECT findings are repair inputs; OPTIONAL_EXPERIMENT remains optional unless the author selects it. Preserve named strengths and reject incompatible suggestions rather than averaging them into the prose. Revision may proceed when the author requests it before both review lanes exist, but record which lanes are missing and never treat that as promotion evidence. Repair problems at their causal scale, including rebuilding whole scenes within approved outcomes, knowledge and relationship trajectory. When plot delivery has swallowed the scene or horror feels flat/cliché, recover independent life and consult `docs/playbooks/advanced-horror-craft-lexicon.md` as a repair playbook to replace crude exposition or jumpscares with subtle psychological/cinematic devices (such as Epistemic Dissociation, Acoustic Evaporation, Negative Space, or Decentered Observation); do not add decorative errands that leave causality unchanged.

Repair facts without flattening the exchange; temporary choices and local consequences are allowed, while new durable backstory, plot outcomes or supernatural rules need author decision. Self-edit the full result and classify new consequence-bearing deltas. Preserve prior candidates; write clean revised prose, locator-only candidate identity and separate revision-audit/writer-handoff. Changed prose needs fresh reviews; an affected changed authority basis needs scoped recheck even if bytes stay fixed. A writer/reviser cannot certify independent PASS. Update all active handoff surfaces.

Shared operating details: docs/playbooks/horrible-craft-review.md. Load only the section relevant to this role.
