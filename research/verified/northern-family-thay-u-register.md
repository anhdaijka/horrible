# Northern family register: thầy / u (S05 D-023)

**Scope:** lexical/cultural usage note only, not a rule for all northern Vietnamese households and not evidence that every family in Bắc Ninh uses the same terms. Source check performed 2026-09-28 after explicit author direction.

**Attested distinction:** `thầy` can be a Northern family term for a father; `u` for a mother. The pair `thầy u` refers to *parents*, not reciprocal address between a father and a daughter. Therefore a D-023 scene between Vũ Cổn and his own daughter Vũ Thị Thủy can naturally use Thủy→father `thầy`, Thủy self-reference `con`, Cổn→Thủy `mày` or `con` depending on agency, and Cổn's self-reference `thầy` when earned. `U` is appropriate only when the mother's relationship is actually involved and her status has been checked from current canon; no need to introduce a new mother episode to demonstrate the pair.

**Sources (basic attestation, not ethnography of this fictional household):**
- Educational explainer of grade-8 regional vocabulary, “Chương trình địa phương (phần tiếng Việt),” 2022-11-23, https://daykemtainha.com/chuong-trinh-dia-phuong-phan-tieng-viet-2/ : gives Đồng bằng Bắc Bộ thầy/u = bố/mẹ.
- Trần Văn Giang, “Đôi điều về cách xưng hô của người Việt,” https://www.namkyluctinh.org/tac-gia-tac-pham/s-t-u-v/tran-van-giang/dhoi-dieu-ve-cach-xung-ho-cua-nguoi-viet.html : lists Bắc father thầy, mother u among several variants.

**Authorial fictional choice:** D-023 selects this register specifically for the Cổn household. It supersedes prior `bố/con` wording in active future Cổn–Thủy composition, without changing the age, blood relation, illness, history, canon reveal or historical verbatim D-017 voice experiment. Other household kinship terms follow actual role and established speech, never mechanically inherit this pair.
