# Lê Đức Tuấn

## Role

The 31-year-old son-in-law of the deceased ông Vũ Văn Cường. He is married to Vũ Thị Mai (Cường's eldest daughter, currently recovering after childbirth at her maternal family home on the district town). Tuấn works as a dump-truck driver transporting gravel and sand for infrastructure projects around Chợ Bến and the highway.

In Case Zero (**D-049**), Tuấn serves as a major living suspect and red herring within the 12-chapter slow-burn architecture:
- **Financial desperation:** Behind his weary, dutiful son-in-law facade, Tuấn is drowning in over 1.2 billion VND of bad debts from truck installment loans and speculative land staking.
- **The forged land deal:** Knowing that the district's road-widening project would push the lotus pond frontage into multi-billion value, Tuấn forged Cường's signature on a land acquisition compensation consent form. Cường discovered the scheme days before his death, leading to a violent row where Cường struck Tuấn across the face and threatened to disown his daughter.
- **Sedative tampering:** On the night of the 13th, Tuấn slipped high-dose sedatives (Diazepam / Stilnox) into Cường's nighttime herbal medicinal wine (*rượu đinh lăng*) hoping Cường would sleep heavily while survey markers were driven into the pond bank.
- **The timing alibi:** Tuấn claims he was in the district center until late, returning at 23:30 on the 13th only to find Cường already dead at the foot of the stairs. Terrified of being charged with murder, Tuấn hid the medicine blister pack and feigned grief, steering suspicion toward rural superstition and "trùng tang".

## Core traits

Pragmatic, nervous, easily startled, driven by financial panic, outwardly deferential to elders but secretly cunning and desperate.

## Physical presence

Around 31 years old, stocky, sunburnt from driving trucks, short crew-cut hair, bloodshot eyes with dark bags from lack of sleep.

Key physical anchors:
- Calloused, oil-stained hands with bitten fingernails.
- Worn canvas work jacket over a dusty wool sweater, rubber boots or cheap plastic sandals.
- Constant nervous tick: tapping his thumb against his lighter or repeatedly checking his phone screen.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration as `Tuấn` or `anh Tuấn`. Addressed by bà Lan as `cháu Tuấn` or `anh Tuấn`.

## Voice

Raspy, hurried, dropping into nervous stammers when cornered about his whereabouts or finances.
