# Vũ Thị Thoa

## Role

The forgotten second child / middle daughter of the Vũ family in xóm Trại, who drowned in the summer of 1996 at age 20. She was the younger sister of Vũ Văn Cường and the older sister of Vũ Thị Thủy.

Her identity is the core historical key to Case Zero:
- On mùng 10 tháng Giêng, the uncanny woman who placed the advance order for ông Cường gave the name **Vũ Thị Thoa**.
- Unlike cô Thủy who was severely beaten by their Confucian father to use her right hand, Thoa was **naturally left-handed** (`thuận tay trái từ bé`) and drew skillfully with her left hand.
- Following her drowning under mysterious, contentious circumstances in 1996, her father (an austere, patriarchal retired Confucian scholar) declared her an unfilial curse, ordered all her photographs, drawings, and personal belongings burned after the 49-day rites, erased her from the formal family genealogy, and forbade anyone in the family or village from ever speaking her name again.
- Her most treasured possession was an old **black-and-silver cassette radio** sent from abroad by an uncle; upon her death, the cassette radio vanished without a trace.

## Core traits

Quiet, introverted, artistic, gentle, and stubborn in preserving her left-handedness despite severe family discipline. 

## Physical presence (In memory & footage)

In 1996, at age 20:
- Slender, pale skin, delicate bone structure.
- Shared the prominent family bone structure with her siblings (high cheekbones, slanted almond eyes, delicate jawline).
- Her left hand was notably long-fingered, pale, and remarkably agile when sketching or handling objects.
- In the mùng 10 CCTV footage, the entity appearing with cô Thủy's face exhibits Thoa's distinct, effortless left-handed dexterity.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in dialogue and historical recollection as `cô Thoa` or `Thoa` (by elders like bà Lan or family members).

## Thematic resonance

Represents the silenced, erased victims of patriarchal rural authority, whose unfinished grievances and unacknowledged deaths return through spiritual memory when family guilt is cracked open.
