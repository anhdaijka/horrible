# Vũ Thị Thoa

## Role

The forgotten second child / middle daughter of the Vũ family in xóm Trại, who died tragically in the summer of 1996 at age 22 (born 1974). She was the younger sister of Vũ Văn Cường and the older sister of Vũ Thị Thủy.

Under master canon (**D-056** & **DIR-02**), Thoa's death and memory constitute the dark supernatural heart of Case Zero:
- **Forced marriage dispute & fatal manslaughter (1996):** In summer 1996, the Vũ family urgently needed wedding funds for eldest son Cường (to marry the mother of Toàn). A wealthy local family's son was madly infatuated with Thoa's delicate beauty and offered a lavish bride price (refusing Thủy due to her clubbed left hand). When Thoa fiercely refused to be sold off as a sacrificial pawn for Cường's wedding, an explosive quarrel erupted by the lotus pond. Cường struck and shoved Thoa, causing her to slip and smash the back of her head against a submerged boulder on the pond bank, knocking her senseless.
- **The "faking" misjudgment & late discovery:** Cường and Ông Cổn assumed Thoa was throwing a tantrum and faking unconsciousness ("ngất ăn vạ"), so they angrily cursed her and went inside to eat. Late that night in a downpour, Thủy searched by the pond and discovered Thoa dead from fatal head trauma and blood loss.
- **Patriarchal concealment & heterodox binding:** To save Cường from prison/execution and preserve clan honor, Ông Cổn ruthlessly vetoed a funeral, forcing Cường and Thủy to conceal Thoa's body in the lotus pond mud. He erased her name from the genealogy and fabricated a rumor that she had fled with a lover. On the pond bank, Thoa's prized foreign Czech cassette radio was on `REC`, recording the quarrel, Cường's strike, the fatal fall, Thủy's weeping discovery, and Cổn's cover-up order. Cường buried the radio with a cinnabar bamboo talisman (*cọc tre khắc bùa trấn thủy*).
- **30-year expiration & retribution (2026):** Exactly thirty years later, the bamboo talisman decomposes and road excavators disturb the pond bed. Reclaiming her identity through the advance paper radio order at bà Lan's shop, Thoa's genuine spirit rises with terrifying wrath.
- **Supernatural execution:** On the night of the 13th, Thoa's spirit manifests on the staircase, breaking Cường's neck 180 degrees backwards (*RẮC!*), and subsequently pursues the remaining living conspirators.

## Core traits

In life: Quiet, introverted, artistic, gentle, and stubborn in preserving her left-handedness despite severe family discipline. 
In death: A relentless, terrifying spiritual force demanding moral and cosmic reckoning for thirty years of erasure and betrayal.

## Physical presence (In memory & footage)

In 1996, at age 22 (born 1974):
- Slender, pale skin, delicate bone structure.
- Shared the prominent family bone structure with her siblings (high cheekbones, slanted almond eyes, delicate jawline).
- Her left hand was notably long-fingered, pale, and remarkably agile when sketching or handling objects.
- In the mùng 10 CCTV footage, the entity appearing with cô Thủy's face exhibits Thoa's distinct, effortless left-handed dexterity.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in dialogue and historical recollection as `cô Thoa` or `Thoa` (by elders like bà Lan or family members).

## Thematic resonance

Represents the silenced, erased victims of patriarchal rural authority and sibling greed, whose suppressed blood-guilt cannot be permanently paved over by concrete or time.
