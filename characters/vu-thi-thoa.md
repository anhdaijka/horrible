# Vũ Thị Thoa

## Role

The forgotten second child / middle daughter of the Vũ family in xóm Trại, who drowned in the summer of 1996 at age 20. She was the younger sister of Vũ Văn Cường and the older sister of Vũ Thị Thủy.

Under master canon (**D-048**), Thoa's death and memory constitute the dark supernatural heart of Case Zero:
- **Collective family crime & the murder tape (1996):** In summer 1996, Thoa was killed during a violent confrontation with her father, Cường, and Thủy over money and forced marriage to finance Cường's wedding. Pushed and held down in the pond mud, she drowned. On the pond bank, her prized foreign Czech cassette radio was on record mode (`REC`), recording the entire quarrel, family curses, and her dying gasps.
- **Occlusion & heterodox binding:** To conceal the crime, her father erased her from the genealogy, burned her drawings and photos, and banned her name. Cường stole the cassette radio for leverage and had a shadowy specialist bury it by the pond with a cinnabar green bamboo water-talisman (*cọc tre khắc bùa trấn thủy*) after Khanh's grandfather refused to bind an unjustly dead soul (`Không nhận lễ`).
- **30-year expiration & retribution (2026):** Exactly thirty years later, the bamboo talisman decomposes and road-widening excavators disturb the pond bed. Stirred by Thủy's nocturnal tape playback and reclaiming her identity through the advance paper radio order at bà Lan's shop, Thoa's genuine spirit rises with terrifying wrath.
- **The advance order:** On mùng 10 tháng Giêng, an uncanny entity bearing Thủy's face but Thoa's left-handed dexterity placed an advance funeral order for Vũ Văn Cường at bà Lan's shop.
- **Supernatural execution:** On the night of the 13th, Thoa's spirit manifests on the staircase, breaking Cường's neck 180 degrees backwards (*RẮC!*), and subsequently pursues Thủy as the remaining surviving murderer.

## Core traits

In life: Quiet, introverted, artistic, gentle, and stubborn in preserving her left-handedness despite severe family discipline. 
In death: A relentless, terrifying spiritual force demanding moral and cosmic reckoning for thirty years of erasure and betrayal.

## Physical presence (In memory & footage)

In 1996, at age 20:
- Slender, pale skin, delicate bone structure.
- Shared the prominent family bone structure with her siblings (high cheekbones, slanted almond eyes, delicate jawline).
- Her left hand was notably long-fingered, pale, and remarkably agile when sketching or handling objects.
- In the mùng 10 CCTV footage, the entity appearing with cô Thủy's face exhibits Thoa's distinct, effortless left-handed dexterity.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in dialogue and historical recollection as `cô Thoa` or `Thoa` (by elders like bà Lan or family members).

## Thematic resonance

Represents the silenced, erased victims of patriarchal rural authority and sibling greed, whose suppressed blood-guilt cannot be permanently paved over by concrete or time.
