# Hoàng Ngọc Mai

## Role

Khanh's friend from childhood. Her family operates a major local vegetable/food-supply business, giving her practical knowledge of drivers, routes, market timing, warehouses and night logistics.

Mai and Khanh grew up around the same market/home environment and have known each other since they were children. Their familiarity should read as old friendship rather than romantic chemistry.

## Core traits

Fast, practical, confident and used to making small operational decisions while several things happen at once. She can be impatient with unnecessary explanation, but she is not a quip machine and does not exist to make Khanh look clever or grounded.

She is a practical half-believer: strange stories do not automatically impress her because night drivers, warehouses and markets generate enough mundane weirdness already. That does not mean she always dismisses them.

## Agency

Mai is not waiting for Khanh's plot. Her family business has drivers to coordinate, deliveries to correct, suppliers to chase, damaged stock, route problems and people calling her at inconvenient times.

She may enter a scene to borrow something, settle a delivery, ask Lan a business question or cut through the shop, then leave before Khanh's conversation reaches a satisfying end.

She knows a lot about local movement because of work, not because the story needs a local-information oracle. She can refuse to help, not know something, or decide a matter is none of her business.

## Relationship with Khanh

The relationship is strictly platonic. Neither Mai nor Khanh has had romantic feelings for the other, and their relationship must never develop into romance. Do not use jealousy, will-they-won't-they framing, parental matchmaking jokes, romantic subtext or romance bait between them.

Their closeness comes from shared childhood history, familiarity with each other's families and the ease of people who do not need to perform friendship for each other.

They can disagree over old memories. They can borrow things without asking ceremonially, leave sentences unfinished, insult each other's childhood habits, and resume a conversation days later without explanation.

## Voice

Mai's speech is context-heavy and task-shaped. When busy, she may talk while looking elsewhere, answer a phone midway through Khanh's sentence, use local shorthand and return to an earlier topic without transition.

Her teasing should be specific to shared history rather than generic banter. `Việt kiều Nhật` is available as a local joke, used sparingly.

When work pressure rises, her speech becomes more directive and economical because she is managing real people and time, not because she has a permanently clipped personality.
