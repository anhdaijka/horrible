# Vũ Cổn (Trưởng tộc Vũ Cổn)

## Role

The 76-year-old Patriarch (*Trưởng tộc*) of the Vũ lineage in xóm Trại. He is the father of Vũ Văn Cường (deceased), Vũ Thị Thoa (deceased 1996), and Vũ Thị Thủy; grandfather of Vũ Văn Toàn. He embodies the ruthless, feudal patriarchal authority of Northern rural lineages.

In Case Zero (**D-050**, **D-054**, **D-056**, **DIR-01**, **DIR-02**):
- **1996 Cover-Up Mastermind:** In summer 1996, obsessed with clan pride and terrified that his eldest son Cường (sole male lineage heir) would face criminal consequences for accidental manslaughter after striking Thoa, Ông Cổn ordered the cover-up. He made Cường help conceal Thoa's corpse in the lotus pond mud, coerced Thủy into silence after she pleaded for a proper funeral, erased Thoa's name from the genealogy, and fabricated the slander that Thoa "fled with a lover".
- **Rejected binding request:** Grandfather hears an opaque request to constrain an unwanted presence and refuses before learning about a death/body. The family then uses an outside specialist. Bình, a younger assistant, knows only the portion of binding work he was given. Neither is automatically omniscient about the crime.
- **1996 wedding cost and evolving extraction (D-011):** After the expected forced-marriage proceeds from Thoa did not materialize, Cổn and Cường sold or pledged actual household assets and borrowed to keep Cường's wedding plan. The initial wedding-era borrowing did NOT remain one unchanged enforceable 30-year debt: later Thủy's distinct earnings, care work and family support were repeatedly reframed as obligations to the household. Exact transaction forms, original loan payoff year and any land rights remain unapproved scene/economic detail, not proven clan-land security.
- **Funeral pressure (2026):** Cổn presses for speed and clan privacy, but his wishes cannot authorize bypassing required scene/body handling. Funeral preparation precedes release; encoffining follows appropriate professional steps. No autopsy-waiver mechanism.
- **Cognitive Stance (DIR-01):** In the early stages, Ông Cổn is rationalist and autocratic, violently shutting down talk of `trùng tang` or ghosts. Only later, after confronting Cường's vengeful spirit directly, does his denial collapse and he secretly sponsor Thầy Bình's ritual under the public pretext of `giải trùng tang`.

## Core traits

Autocratic, obsessed with lineage face (*thể diện dòng họ*), intimidating, superstitious beneath a ferocious veneer of rural materialism.

## Physical presence

Around 76 years old, tall but slightly stooped, dressed in traditional raw silk pajamas (*áo bà ba lụa màu mỡ gà*), wool beret (*mũ nồi len*), with a sparse grey goatee (*chòm râu dê hoa râm*).

Key physical anchors:
- Heavy silver-tipped ebony cane (*chiếc gậy gỗ mun bịt bạc*), constantly slammed down onto tile floors (*cộp... cộp...*) to assert authority.
- Sunken, cloudy eyes that glare with fierce dominance.
- Veiny, arthritic hands with protruding knuckles.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `ông Cổn`, `ông Vũ Cổn`, `ông Trưởng tộc`, or `bác Cổn`.

## Voice

Rasping, sharp and commanding; prone to explosive curses and barking orders to silence dissent.

## Approved ending direction — D-002

Cổn remains alive. He loses sole control over the family’s account when Thủy and Lan Anh make costly truth-telling choices. Resistance may persist; survival does not promise repentance, forgiveness or a particular criminal sentence. No all-explaining confession or single-night completion of recovery, identification and legal process.

The approved full-volume story develops the grandfather's **true capacity to care and run a household alongside his need to control daughters and descendants**. Earlier family wedding urgency includes the developmental direction of an already pregnant future daughter-in-law; no exact birthday/medical gestation or mother's informed complicity is inferred. His private dependence on Thủy's skill/help vs public characterization of it as duty must appear in lived ordinary scenes. **His specific new 2026 choice was subsequently APPROVED by D-014.** Focused candidate C-2026-1: after G6a he could weaponize proof of her fake scares to discredit what she actually witnessed about the 1996 discovery in a small family meeting, then suffer a loss of cooperation before direct Cường ghost encounter. This is a suggestion for future author approval, not existing canon. He survives the finale but loses exclusive narrative control; no compulsory confession, penitence or universally effective rite.

## D-014 — Approved new 2026 wrongdoing and cat omen boundary

After G6a has credibly identified Thoa's bones but before full G6b, Cổn deliberately cites Thủy's proved fake 2026 scares to discredit her *true narrowly witnessed* 1996 discovery of Thoa already dead and her request for a funeral. This is a small, consequential family encounter, not a declaration that Thoa's identified remains are fake; a true practical and occasionally tender father also again seizes control of who gets heard. Thủy can withdraw nonessential labor while arranging necessary elder care, and Cổn's subsequent sight of independently haunting embodied Cường in the late house climax is no magic absolution. D-014 confirms the black neighbor cat **actually jumps over Cường's home coffin after lawful body release**, leaving everyone terrified of supposed quỷ nhập tràng/trùng tang, but **no quỷ nhập tràng occurs and the cat does not cause Cường's ghost**. Cổn's potential omission of a thỉnh-hương-linh rite as a NEW occult cause is NOT author-approved.

## D-016 — Patriarchal speech distinct from ordinary familial mày

Cổn may say **mày** to Thủy, but unlike bà Lan's familiar mày with Khanh, the word can demand lifelong obedience and bring social/financial cost for refusing. His tactics include setting a task as already decided, reframing current service as family duty, recalling history selectively, and occasionally solving a genuine problem or showing bounded concern, which complicates rather than excuses coercion. He needn't shout or give thematic speeches. With paid funeral helpers he may speak practically and respectfully because he needs their work. Thủy must retain room for a concrete independent answer/action. Cổn's D-014 post-G6a cynical attack on Thủy's truthful **limited** 1996 discovery must not leak into an earlier standalone current-money voice test. See [Voice Bible](../plot/case-zero-voice-and-dialogue-bible-v1.md).
