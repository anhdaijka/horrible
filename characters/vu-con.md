# Vũ Cổn (Trưởng tộc Vũ Cổn)

## Role

The 76-year-old Patriarch (*Trưởng tộc*) of the Vũ lineage in xóm Trại. He is the father of Vũ Văn Cường (deceased), Vũ Thị Thoa (deceased 1996), and Vũ Thị Thủy; grandfather of Vũ Văn Toàn. He embodies the ruthless, feudal patriarchal authority of Northern rural lineages.

In Case Zero (**D-050**, **D-054**, **D-056**, **DIR-01**, **DIR-02**):
- **The Fourth Accomplice & 1996 Cover-Up Mastermind:** In summer 1996, obsessed with clan pride and terrified that his eldest son Cường (sole male lineage heir) would face criminal execution for accidental manslaughter after striking Thoa, Ông Cổn ruthlessly ordered the cover-up. He commanded Cường to conceal Thoa's corpse in the lotus pond mud, erased Thoa's name from the genealogy, fabricated the slander that Thoa "fled with a lover", and forced Khanh's grandfather to bury a heterodox talisman (which was rejected: `Không nhận lễ`).
- **30-Year Debt Exploitation:** When the suitor canceled the betrothal and left zero dowry, Ông Cổn fire-sold oxen, pawned clan land to fund Cường's lavish wedding, and dumped 30 years of grueling debt repayment onto 19-year-old youngest daughter Thủy, robbing her of her youth.
- **Autopsy Suppression (2026):** When Cường died violently with his neck snapped backwards on the night of the 13th, Ông Cổn immediately forced grand-daughter-in-law Lan Anh to sign an administrative waiver refusing a forensic autopsy, claiming the imperative of the "auspicious encoffining hour" (*giờ hoàng đạo nhập quan*) to suppress police inquiry into the house and protect clan reputation.
- **Cognitive Stance (DIR-01):** In the early stages (Chapters 1–5), Ông Cổn is strictly rationalist and autocratic, violently shutting down any talk of "trùng tang" or "ghosts" with his silver-tipped ebony walking stick (*"Trùng cái mả mẹ mày!"*). Only in later chapters, when confronted directly by Cường's vengeful spirit, does he suffer a psychological breakdown and secretly sponsor Thầy Bình's talisman ritual under the pretense of "giải trùng tang".

## Core traits

Autocratic, obsessed with lineage face (*thể diện dòng họ*), intimidating, superstitious beneath a ferocious veneer of rural materialism.

## Physical presence

Around 76 years old, tall but slightly stooped, dressed in traditional raw silk pajamas (*áo bà ba lụa màu mỡ gà*), wool beret (*mũ nồi len*), with a sparse grey goatee (*chòm râu dê hoa râm*).

Key physical anchors:
- Heavy silver-tipped ebony cane (*chiếc gậy gỗ mun bịt bạc*), constantly slammed down onto tile floors (*cộp... cộp...*) to assert authority.
- Sunken, cloudy eyes that glare with fierce dominance.
- Veiny, arthritic hands with protruding knuckles.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `ông Cổn`, `ông Vũ Cổn`, `ông Trưởng tộc`, or `bác Cổn`.

## Voice

Rasping, shrill, sharp like a military command; prone to explosive curses and barking orders to silence dissent.
