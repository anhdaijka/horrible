# Đặng Văn Bình (Thầy cúng Bình)

## Role

A 58-year-old local ritual specialist (*thầy cúng*) residing in xóm Trại, frequently hired by villagers around Chợ Bến for funeral rites, death anniversaries, and spirit-pacification work. Exact ritual vocabulary and practice must remain research-gated; this character file does not certify any invented rite as a real regional tradition.

Under current Case Zero canon (**D-054–D-059, DIR-01, DIR-02, DIR-04**), Bình is a human link to the heterodox concealment history rather than an omniscient supernatural explainer:
- **Partial binding knowledge (D-001 post-reset):** As a younger assistant to the outside specialist, Bình performed a limited task around the pond and remembers material/location clues. The employer concealed the killing and body from him. He can help narrow a search area, not reveal exact remains by supernatural expertise.
- **Knowledge boundary:** Bình suspects that old work has been disturbed, but does not know the 1996 fatal blow or that a body was hidden there until evidence reaches him. What he was told, what he actually did and what he inferred must remain separate. His claims about spirits are not narrator-certified fact.
- **2026 pressure:** When Cường dies violently, Bình has strong reasons to keep attention away from the old pond/binding history and to cooperate with Cổn once Cổn's public denial of `trùng tang` collapses. The public framing of later ritual intervention may be `giải trùng tang`, while Cổn's private motive is to stop Cường's haunting and contain scandal.
- **Planned ritual beats:** No old chapter or coffin-sealing scene survives as approval. Close ritual practice needs current research; end-state responsibility depends on his actual actions, not a blanket label of cultist or accomplice.

## Core traits

Superstitious, calculating, defensive about the history of his ritual lineage, outwardly authoritative in matters villagers bring to him but capable of being genuinely frightened when events exceed his explanations.

## Physical presence

Around 58 years old, lean, sharp-chinned, with thinning grey hair.

Physical/costume specifics tied to real ritual roles should be verified before close-up prose rather than assumed from generic occult imagery.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `thầy Bình` or `ông Bình` when socially appropriate.

## Voice

Grave and authoritative when performing his social role; sharp or evasive when questioned about the 1996 binding. He must not become a lore-dump machine.

## Ritual effect — D-002

A ritual may temporarily restore solidarity, company and the ability to rest without confirming its supernatural explanation or ending the haunting. Bình is neither an infallible exorcist nor automatically a fraudulent villain. Concrete practice remains research-gated. He cannot reveal the full old crime from his limited past role.
