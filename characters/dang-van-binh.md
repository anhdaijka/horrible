# Đặng Văn Bình (Thầy cúng Bình)

## Role

A 58-year-old local ritual specialist (*thầy cúng*) residing in xóm Trại, frequently hired by villagers around Chợ Bến for funeral rites, death anniversaries, and spirit-pacification work. Exact ritual vocabulary and practice must remain research-gated; this character file does not certify any invented rite as a real regional tradition.

Under current Case Zero canon (**D-054–D-059, DIR-01, DIR-02, DIR-04**), Bình is a human link to the heterodox concealment history rather than an omniscient supernatural explainer:
- **Lineage of the heterodox binding:** As a younger apprentice/associate of the outside specialist hired after Khanh's grandfather refused the matter (`Không nhận lễ`), Bình has historical knowledge of the Vũ family's attempt to bind/suppress the aftermath of Thoa's 1996 death. The buried Czech cassette radio and green-bamboo water-binding talisman belong to that concealed history.
- **Knowledge boundary:** Bình may understand that the old binding is failing or that the pond disturbance is dangerous, but he does not automatically know the full metaphysical truth of Thoa, Cường or the haunting. His ritual interpretation is a character claim unless independently confirmed by author-level canon.
- **2026 pressure:** When Cường dies violently, Bình has strong reasons to keep attention away from the old pond/binding history and to cooperate with Cổn once Cổn's public denial of `trùng tang` collapses. The public framing of later ritual intervention may be `giải trùng tang`, while Cổn's private motive is to stop Cường's haunting and contain scandal.
- **Planned ritual beats:** Coffin-sealing/talisman/nail imagery from older plans is not automatically locked by this profile. Any close-up ritual scene must follow the newest approved chapter plan plus verified cultural research rather than resurrecting deprecated D-049 mechanics by default.

## Core traits

Superstitious, calculating, defensive about the history of his ritual lineage, outwardly authoritative in matters villagers bring to him but capable of being genuinely frightened when events exceed his explanations.

## Physical presence

Around 58 years old, lean, sharp-chinned, with thinning grey hair.
