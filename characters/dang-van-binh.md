# Đặng Văn Bình (Thầy cúng Bình)

## Role

A 58-year-old local ritual specialist (*thầy cúng*) residing in xóm Trại, frequently hired by villagers around Chợ Bến for funeral rites, death anniversaries, and spirit pacification.

In Case Zero (**D-049**), Thầy Bình acts as a crucial link between the living conspiracy and the occult history of 1996:
- **Lineage of heterodox binding:** Thầy Bình was the junior apprentice of the shadowy outside ritual specialist hired by the Vũ family in 1996 (after Khanh's grandfather refused: `Không nhận lễ`). Bình witnessed the burial of Thoa's Czech cassette radio and the driving of the 30-year cinnabar green bamboo talisman (*cọc tre trấn thủy*) into the pond mud.
- **Occlusion of homicide:** When Cường died with his neck broken backwards, Thầy Bình immediately recognized the signs of spiritual retribution (*oán linh đoạt mạng*) from the expired 30-year talisman. Fearing that an official police autopsy or an extended wake would reveal the 1996 drowning and expose his master's illegal binding ritual, Bình used "trùng tang" panic to rush the family:
  - Demanding immediate encoffining before the midnight hour (giờ Tý);
  - Covering Cường's face with a heavy red sealing talisman;
  - Attempting to drive four ten-centimeter iron nails into the coffin corners to seal the corpse tight.
- **The supernatural resistance:** During the midnight sealing in Chapter 8, the fourth nail snaps clean in two (*CHÁT!*), and the oil lamp extinguishes with a stench of pond rot, shattering Bình's composure.

## Core traits

Superstitious, calculating, easily rattled when his occult lineage is questioned, outwardly authoritative in ritual matters but terrified of genuine demonic wrath.

## Physical presence

Around 58 years old, lean, sharp-chinned, with thinning grey hair tied back or covered by a traditional black headscarf (*khăn xếp*).

Key physical anchors:
- Nicotine-stained fingers and yellowed teeth from continuous rustic pipe smoking (*thuốc lào*).
- Dressed in a dark blue ritual robe (*áo the thâm*) with faded brass buttons, wearing black cloth shoes.
- Shifty eyes that frequently dart toward the dark corners or water bodies.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `thầy Bình` or `ông Bình`.

## Voice

Chanting, nasal, adopting a grave and solemn cadence when reciting funeral prayers, but turning sharp, defensive, and hurried when questioned about talismans.
