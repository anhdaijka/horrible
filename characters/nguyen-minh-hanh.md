# Nguyễn Minh Hạnh

## Role

Khanh's older sister, a primary-school teacher who is married and lives nearby.

## Core traits

Warm but practical. She often sees the social and ethical consequences that Khanh's root-cause focus misses. She is willing to question whether uncovering a truth automatically gives someone the right to expose it.

Her affection is ordinary sibling affection: when she wants, buys or gets something, she naturally thinks of Khanh and may ask whether he wants one too. Do not idealize her into a caretaker archetype.

## Voice

Familiar, direct and grounded. She can acknowledge spiritual possibilities without feeding fear or treating every anomaly as meaningful.
