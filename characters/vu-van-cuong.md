# Vũ Văn Cường

## Role

The deceased victim whose sudden death initiates **Case Zero — Đơn hàng cho người chưa chết**. A 59-year-old resident of xóm Trại, he appeared outwardly robust, healthy, and sociable right up until the night of his death.

Under master canon (**D-048**), Cường's outward affability hid decades of predatory guilt and complicity in family crime:
- **Historical crime & hidden leverage (1996):** In summer 1996, Cường and his father clashed violently with his middle sister Vũ Thị Thoa over money and forced marriage to fund Cường's wedding and new brick house. During the fight by the lotus pond, Thoa was pushed down and drowned in the mud. Thoa's prized Czech cassette radio—running on record mode (`REC`) on the bank—recorded the entire fatal confrontation and her dying cries. Instead of burning it with her belongings, Cường secretly hid the radio as lifelong blackmail against his father and younger sister Thủy, sealing it in the mud beside the pond under a heterodox 30-year bamboo water-binding talisman rejected by Khanh's grandfather (`Không nhận lễ`).
- **Inheritance greed (2026):** For thirty years, Cường enjoyed the family estate while Thủy worked as an exploited factory laborer. When the 2026 road-widening project made the pond frontage worth billions, Cường refused to give Thủy her share, triggering her intimidation campaign.
- **Advance order & death:** Four days before his death (mùng 10 tháng Giêng), a custom votive-paper order bearing his exact name, age, and address was placed at bà Lan's shop by a woman calling herself Vũ Thị Thoa.

## Core traits

Outwardly jovial, loud, fond of riding his red Honda Wave to buy tea, bantering at alley chess boards, and watching the evening news. Inwardly superstitious, paranoid, and terrified of retribution.

## Death circumstances

Died around 11:00 p.m. on the night of 13th Giêng at the foot of his staircase. 
- While officially reported to authorities as an accidental fall, his death was horrific supernatural retribution: Thoa's genuine ghost materialized on the stairs (as captured acoustically on his unsent 38-second Zalo voice message) and snapped his neck 180 degrees backwards (*RẮC!*).
- Physical trauma: 3rd cervical vertebra ruptured the skin at the nape with clotted black blood; eyes wide and inverted staring at the landing; mouth frozen in a grotesque sneer; fingernails torn off clawing the wall; body turning icy cold within seconds.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `ông Cường` or `bác Cường`.
