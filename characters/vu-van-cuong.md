# Vũ Văn Cường

## Role

The deceased victim whose sudden death initiates **Case Zero — Đơn hàng cho người chưa chết**. A 59-year-old resident of xóm Trại (born 1967), he appeared outwardly robust, healthy, and sociable right up until the night of his death.

Under master canon (**D-056**, **DIR-02**), Cường's outward affability hid decades of guilt and exploitation:
- **Historical manslaughter (1996):** Cường and his father pressured middle sister Vũ Thị Thoa into an unwanted marriage intended to help finance Cường's wedding. During the violent quarrel by the lotus pond, Cường struck/shoved Thoa; she fell backward, hit her head on a stone and became unconscious. After a cursory check and a missed opportunity for help, Cường and Cổn clung to the convenient `ngất ăn vạ` explanation and abandoned her. Thủy later found Thoa dead.
- **Cover-up:** Cường obeys Cổn’s order to hide the body. The radio is a personal-memory object, not a recording of the quarrel. Historical allegations require limited testimony and independent corroboration. Grandfather refused an opaque coercive request without knowing the killing; an outside specialist performed the binding.
- **Inheritance greed (2026):** For decades Cường benefited from family property whose debt burden had been pushed onto Thủy. When the road project made the pond-side land extremely valuable, he attempted to cut Thủy out, triggering her ghost-themed intimidation/extortion campaign.
- **Advance order:** The mùng 10 order precedes his death on night 13 by three calendar dates. The purchaser uses Thoa’s name; the order’s full meaning is revealed only at G6c.

## Core traits

Outwardly jovial, loud and socially ordinary; inwardly controlling, superstitious about the buried past and increasingly paranoid under Thủy's 2026 pressure campaign.

## Death circumstances

Objective author truth is locked by **D-058 / DIR-04**:
- **22:35–22:45:** Toàn confronts Cường over the land deed. Cường scratches Toàn's neck and tears his work shirt; Toàn shoves him and leaves for the sand truck.
- **22:45–22:53:** Cường is alone. Tape-like mechanical hiss and wet footsteps upstairs make him assume Thủy is continuing her intimidation campaign.
- **Around 22:54:** Cường starts a local recording to retain evidence of what he assumes is further intimidation.
- **22:54:32 (author-time only):** Thoa inflicts fatal neck injury before impact. No fixed angle or early medical time-of-death precision.
- **23:05–23:10:** Toàn returns, finds him dead and incorrectly believes his own earlier shove caused the death.

### Phone and physical evidence — D-002

The phone falls near the landing and is collected during the first professional examination. Thủy asks about the already-collected phone and fears recovery of her threats. Her separate haunting is not used to guard it. Stored threats and a local recording are assessed later by appropriate staff.

Neck trauma is severe and requires examination. Sound sequence, injury, timestamp and identity of the attacker are different propositions; no single noise proves all of them. Clean nails are uncanny author truth, not proof that Toàn never assaulted him. No Zalo/offline queue, no fixed 38-second file, no 180-degree requirement.

Funeral preparations may begin promptly, but body release and necessary professional handling precede encoffining.

## Post-death supernatural role

Under **D-054 / DIR-01**, Cường's newly dead spirit becomes the central direct domestic haunting force in the early Case Zero arc. Family and villagers interpret his return through `Trùng tang / bắt người đi theo`. That interpretation is reader-facing belief/red herring, not a universal supernatural law.

## Information-order firewall

This internal file may state objective truth. Reader-facing material before the later reveal must not identify Thoa as dead or as Cường's supernatural executioner.

## Family complexity and haunting signature — D-002

Cường genuinely cared for Toàn as well as controlling him; warm memories must coexist with wrongdoing, not erase it. His wife (Toàn’s mother) died years before 2026, after their son reached adulthood. No further murder, name, year or cause is implied.

His haunting works through familiar calls, pauses and repeated demands: family may obey by habit before recognizing the impossibility. It is tied to the household and familiar relationships, not omniscient control at any distance. Daytime partial witnesses and a later Toàn–Lan Anh stairway encounter are approved directions; the full latter beat does not belong in CH-01’s brief coda.

An observable final confrontation/withdrawal is required, but its exact cause and manifestation remain to be designed before P6. A confession or ritual is not automatically a magic switch.
