# Vũ Văn Cường

## Role

The deceased victim whose sudden death initiates **Case Zero — Đơn hàng cho người chưa chết**. A 59-year-old resident of xóm Trại, he appeared outwardly robust and healthy right up until the night of his death.

Four days before his death (mùng 10 tháng Giêng), a custom votive-paper order bearing his exact name, age, and address was placed at bà Lan's shop by a woman calling herself Vũ Thị Thoa.

## Core traits

In life, Cường was an ordinary, sociable older man in the village: fond of riding his red Honda Wave to buy tea, sitting at alley chess boards, watching the evening television news, and bantering with neighbors. He had no known terminal illness or impending death indicators.

## Death circumstances

Died around 11:00 p.m. on the night of 13th Giêng at the foot of his family home staircase. The death exhibited horrific, uncanny trauma:
- Cervical spine snapped and neck twisted near 180 degrees backwards, with the 3rd vertebra puncturing the skin at the nape;
- Eyes wide and bulging, mouth frozen in a grotesque sneer;
- Fingernails torn and broken to the bone from violently clawing into the plastered wall at the base of the stairs;
- Body turning icy cold almost instantly.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `ông Cường` or `bác Cường`.
