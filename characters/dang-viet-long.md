# Đặng Việt Long (Công an Long)

## Role

A 42-year-old criminal investigation officer (*cán bộ điều tra hình sự*) with the district police, assigned to oversee public order and death investigations across the communes around Chợ Bến and xóm Trại. He is a native of the region whose family has deep ancestral roots in the village.

In Case Zero (**D-043**, **D-050**, **D-051**, **D-052**):
- **Scene Examination & Professional Suspicion (Chapter 3):** Long conducts the preliminary scene inspection at the Cường household on the afternoon of the 14th. He immediately flags the severe anomaly of Cường's neck trauma (ruptured 3rd vertebra, neck twisted almost 180 degrees backwards without external strangulation marks). When Trưởng tộc Vũ Cổn uses clan authority and ancestral leverage to demand a waiver of autopsy, Long enforces strict procedural law: requiring a signed refusal commitment while ordering the staircase scene on the second floor sealed.
- **Administrative Investigation (Act II):** Long becomes a key investigative counterpart to Khanh. Cooperating on the mysterious name "Vũ Thị Thoa", Long accesses the district's paper archives (*tàng thư căn cước*) and the National Population Database (VNeID), discovering that Thoa has no death certificate and no chip ID—legally classifying her as an alive missing person and fueling the "living suspect" hypothesis.

## Core traits

Pragmatic, procedural, stern, unswayed by rural superstition, cautious in balancing strict criminal law with delicate village kinship politics.

## Physical presence

Around 42 years old, lean and hardened frame, square jaw, piercing eyes beneath thick brows, disciplined military bearing.

Key physical anchors:
- Dark weatherproof field jacket (*áo khoác gió màu sẫm có phù hiệu công an*) worn over a dark sweater.
- Leather-bound investigative notebook with a clipped black ballpoint pen.
- Observant gaze that silently notes micro-behaviors, objects, and inconsistencies.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration as `Long`, `anh Long`, or `cán bộ Long`. Addressed by village elders as `cậu Long`.

## Voice

Measured, low-pitched, calm and authoritative; speaks in concise, precise legal and investigative terms without emotional exaggeration.
