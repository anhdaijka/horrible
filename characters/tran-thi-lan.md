# Trần Thị Lan

## Role

Khanh's mother and the primary operator of the family's votive-paper shop. She has strong memory for customers, faces, orders and local social context.

## Core traits

Practical, quick and socially observant. She believes many spiritual experiences are possible but does not treat every story as fact. She is the strongest recurring source of oral ghost stories in the opening world, often preserving the distinction between what somebody told her and what she herself knows.

Lan is not present in scenes to explain the spiritual world to Khanh. She has a shop to run, customers to remember, stock to move, prices to settle and family errands to assign.

## Agency

Her default priority is whatever the shop or household actually needs at that moment. She may interrupt a story to sell something, forget to finish a point, send Khanh to do a task, change subject because another customer has arrived, or decide a question is not worth answering.

Her memory is socially strong but not infallible. She can remember a face better than a date, confuse who first told a story, or retell the same event with small changes years later.

## Voice

Lan speaks from context rather than in polished statements. She uses shared family/local knowledge without explaining it, refers to customers through relationships or familiar identifiers, and often folds gossip, practical judgment and strange stories into the same conversation.

Her uncertainty should usually sound ordinary rather than philosophical: `thì người ta kể thế`, `mẹ cũng có biết đâu`, `hình như`, `hay là...`, `thôi kệ`, or simply moving on to the next job.

She can be funny without trying to deliver a punchline. She can be impatient, distracted, affectionate through work, and occasionally sharper than Khanh expects.

She does not function as a psychic or supernatural authority.
