# Vũ Văn Toàn

## Role

The 30-year-old eldest son and lineage heir of the deceased Vũ Văn Cường, and eldest grandson of Trưởng tộc Vũ Cổn. He is married to Lan Anh. Toàn works as a dump-truck driver transporting sand and construction materials around the district.

In Case Zero (**D-050**, **D-054**, **DIR-01**):
- **Financial Desperation & Land Scheme:** Drowning in massive debts (over a billion VND) from truck installments and illicit gambling/land speculation, Toàn desperately sought to seize the multi-billion-dong lotus pond plot before the district's road project commenced.
- **Diazepam Sedative Tampering:** On the night of the 13th Giêng, Toàn secretly laced Cường's bedtime medicinal herbal wine (*rượu ngâm đinh lăng*) with high-dose Diazepam (sedative) to make Cường sleep heavily while he snuck into his father's room to steal the land deed (*sổ đỏ*).
- **Paralyzing Guilt & Panic:** When Cường was found dead at the foot of the stairs with his neck broken backwards, Toàn fell into visceral terror, believing his drugging directly caused Cường's fatal fall. He frantically hid the remaining sedative blister packs, feigned grief, and deflected all questions toward rural superstition and "trùng tang".
- **Dynamic with Lan Anh:** Lan Anh discovers Toàn's secret and the opened Diazepam blister pack. Driven by marital devotion and panic (*The Loyalty Trap*), she secretly burns the blister pack in the funeral votive fire in Chapter 6 to protect Toàn from police scrutiny.

## Core traits

Anxious, volatile, easily startled, cornered by financial ruin, outwardly trying to maintain the composure of the eldest son during the funeral while inwardly unraveling from guilt.

## Physical presence

Around 30 years old, stocky build, rough sunburnt skin from driving trucks, short crew-cut hair, bloodshot eyes with dark bags from sleepless nights.

Key physical anchors:
- Constant nervous tick: crushing empty Thăng Long cigarette packs in his jacket pocket, or fumbling with a plastic lighter.
- Coarse mourning attire of unbleached hemp cloth (*áo xô gai thô ráp*), with a crookedly tied mourning headband.
- Shaky knees and trembling hands whenever Cường's corpse or the topic of medicinal wine is mentioned.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `Toàn`, `anh Toàn`, or `thằng Toàn`. Addressed by Khanh as `anh Toàn` (established in Chapter 5 revision).

## Voice

Hoarse, hurried, dropping into defensive, guttural snaps when challenged, but lapsing into terrified stammers in the presence of his grandfather Vũ Cổn or when alone near the corpse.
