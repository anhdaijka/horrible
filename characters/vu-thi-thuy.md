# Vũ Thị Thủy

## Role

The surviving younger sister of the deceased ông Vũ Văn Cường and younger sister of Vũ Thị Thoa. She is a 49-year-old unmarried garment factory worker (born 1977) living/working in Yên Phong, Bắc Ninh.

Under the approved master canon (**D-056**, **D-058**, **D-059**, **DIR-02**, **DIR-04**), cô Thủy embodies the tragic intersection between family exploitation, human wrongdoing and supernatural retribution:

- **1996 discovery, not eyewitness to the fatal shove:** Thủy did **not** witness the fatal Cường–Thoa struggle itself. She knew the family had been pressuring Thoa over the forced-marriage/wedding-money dispute, but late that rainy night she went looking for her sister and discovered Thoa already dead by the lotus pond from the head injury. She ran back for Cổn and Cường and pleaded for a proper funeral.
- **Coerced silence & debt burden:** Cổn imposed wedding debts on Thủy after rejecting her plea for a funeral. She worked through different jobs, including garment work, over the following decades; she works at Yên Phong in 2026. Do not date the present factory to 1996.
- **Living conspirator / Burglary motive (2026):** When the 2026 road project made the redeemed pond-side land extremely valuable and Cường attempted to cut her out, Thủy launched an intimidation/extortion campaign from mùng 8 through noon 13. On Night 13, desperate for money to clear her debts, she sneaked out of her factory shift to burglarize Cường's house for the land deed (Sổ đỏ).
- **The Forged Alibi & Physical Evidence:** Thủy was *at the scene* when Cường died. She entered with her old 1996 keys, touched the body (getting blood on her hands), and fled in terror from the supernatural presence. She left behind a drop of sewing machine oil on the banister and a deformed left handprint on the glass cabinet. She rushed back to the factory and forged her alibi (having a coworker clock her out or avoiding cameras).
- **Information silo:** Thủy knows nothing about Toàn's 22:35–22:45 scuffle with Cường, nor Thầy Bình's blackmail visit. Toàn knows nothing about her intimidation campaign. Her panic after the death comes from her direct presence at the scene and fear of being framed for a murder she didn't commit, plus the sheer terror of what she witnessed.
- **Phone anxiety / Fatal Threat:** She urgently asks about the phone not just because of minor threats, but because her final message to Cường was a direct death threat ("mạng đổi mạng"). If the police find the phone and break her alibi, she is the perfect premeditated murder suspect.
- **Cường-haunting / Trùng tang framing:** In the early arc Thủy interprets the domestic haunting as Cường's newly dead spirit returning to `bắt người đi theo`. This is her character belief/fear and part of the D-054/D-055 smoke screen; it does not reveal the objective Thoa truth to the reader.
- **Biometric & physical impossibility:** Despite her human intimidation campaign, Thủy could not have been the fluid left-handed mùng 10 purchaser. Her left wrist/hand is severely deformed and she uses her right hand for ordinary tasks. This very deformity makes her handprint on the cabinet uniquely identifiable to the police.

## Core traits

Guarded, bitter, deeply anxious, worn down by decades of factory labor, resentful toward Cường and Cổn, and spiritually terrified when her own fake-haunting tactics collide with phenomena she cannot explain.

## Physical presence

Around 49 years old (born 1977), thin, gaunt, with pale, sallow skin from years under fluorescent factory lights.

Key physical anchors:
- Facial features: slightly flattened nose bridge from an old childhood fall, high cheekbones, slightly upturned/slanted eyes, and a small dark mole to the left of her nostril.
- The left wrist: thickened and deformed with a prominent bony protrusion on the outer wrist; the fingers are contracted inward and cannot perform fluid writing/sketching.
- Right-handedness: strictly right-handed in ordinary actions such as holding cups, lighting incense and writing.
- Clothing: modest, worn working-class garments suited to her life and shift work; avoid turning wardrobe into a fixed costume in every scene.

## Voice

Hoarse and worn, capable of defensive calculation under practical threat and sudden panic under supernatural pressure. Do not reduce every appearance to trembling or exposition.

## Approved trajectory — D-002

Thủy initially wants recognition of her work, material independence and a share in the life she helped sustain, not an abstract mission to vindicate Thoa. She has attempted to seek help or build an independent life before; particular episodes and obstacles need scene-level design, not invented total helplessness due to disability.

As truth becomes unavoidable she chooses to speak despite the cost to her own reputation and responsibility. She recalls Thoa through everyday warmth, friction and unfinished hopes, not only victimhood. The handmade radio detail belongs to this recognition, not forensic proof. At the ending she chooses what to keep or send of the paper radio; handling/custody must be settled before that scene. She is not obliged to forgive.
