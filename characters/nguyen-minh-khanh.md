# Nguyễn Minh Khanh

## Role

Khanh is the central POV character. He has recently returned from several years of onsite software work in Japan and is home for a short break before returning to his Vietnam–Japan technology job in Hanoi.

## Core traits

Practical, technically minded and inclined toward root-cause thinking when something genuinely fails to fit. He checks natural explanations first but does not dismiss spiritual possibilities out of hand. His weakness is an excessive need for causal closure: once an anomaly becomes real to him, he can keep digging beyond what the situation practically requires.

That analytical mode is not permanently switched on. Around family and old friends he can be sleepy, lazy, petty, amused, distracted, embarrassed, nostalgic or simply uninterested in analyzing anything.

Khanh has no confirmed supernatural ability. His body still remembers the intense fear of ghosts he had as a child even when his adult reasoning stays controlled.

## Narrative reference

In close-third narration, `Khanh` / `anh` are natural. His age and POV role do not require an honorific.

## Physical presence

Khanh is 27, medium-tall with a lean-to-average desk-worker build rather than an athletic one. His black hair is cut short but falls untidily when he has just woken or stopped caring about presentation. At home he defaults to plain, comfortable clothes and often looks less composed than his work persona suggests.

His hands are long-fingered and kept practical rather than styled; when relaxed at home he tends to sit or lean without much concern for posture.

## Body-language baseline

At home Khanh is physically loose, sometimes slouched or lazy. When genuine technical attention switches on, he tends to become more still: eyes stay on the source of a mismatch and his movements become deliberate.

Fear or uncertainty should not always become visible panic. Useful deviations include delaying one step longer than necessary, turning on a light he does not need, gripping an object too hard, choosing not to put his back to a space, moving through a task faster than usual, or becoming irritated because he notices himself hesitating.

Do not reuse one of these as a signature tic in every scene.

## Agency

Khanh has an ordinary life he wants to return to: work, independence, Hanoi routine and a sense that the Japan chapter of his life can settle into something manageable.

He should not accept every strange story as an invitation to investigate. Curiosity must compete with inconvenience, family boundaries, embarrassment, work obligations and the possibility that something is simply none of his business.

He can make wrong assumptions, lose interest, postpone checking something, or decide that a practical explanation is good enough until new evidence genuinely disturbs it.

## Relationship with Mai

Hoàng Ngọc Mai is Khanh's friend from childhood. They grew up around the same market/home environment and have the ease, teasing and unfinished conversational rhythm of people who have known each other since they were children.

Their relationship is strictly platonic. Neither has had romantic feelings for the other, and their relationship must not develop into romance. Do not use jealousy, will-they-won't-they framing, parental matchmaking jokes, romantic subtext or romance bait between them.

## Relationship with Thắng

Lê Văn Thắng is Khanh's classmate from their district high school days. Their dynamic is informal, direct and grounded in adolescent shared memories. Thắng's early marriage, local blue-collar career and cheerful, teasing nature serve as an unpretentious social anchor for Khanh when reconnecting with village life after years abroad in Japan.

## Long arc

- wanting answers → accepting responsibility for what he knows;
- avoiding other people's burdens → helping when he genuinely can;
- demanding closure → living honestly with uncertainty.

## Voice / behavior

Khanh should not sound like the smartest person in every room. At home his speech is casual, sometimes incomplete and often shaped by family shorthand. He may tease, complain, answer lazily or let his mother win a pointless argument because he cannot be bothered.

His engineer identity should become distinctive when evidence conflicts, systems behave impossibly or people make causal claims. Then he notices reproducibility, contradictions, timing and alternative explanations more sharply than most people around him.

Do not turn his internal voice into constant polished analysis. Let thought move associatively and unevenly like ordinary thought.

Fear does not require a fixed checklist of breath, heartbeat and muscle tension. It can appear as delay, avoidance, irritation, calling out to someone, turning on a light, doing a task faster than necessary or pretending he was not frightened.

## Case Zero agency — D-002

Khanh’s concrete stake is the family shop and integrity of its order record. Rumors, customers, family ties and work demands push him to choose whether to preserve and accurately describe what was recorded. He is not licensed to alter or withhold evidence. He can reason about local facts he knows, ask questions and revise assumptions without becoming the case investigator. Ordinary warmth, his family and his platonic friendship with Mai remain living counterweights, not filler or a mandatory romance.
