# Writing brief — isolated fixture

Status: READY for a non-canon forward test.

## Situation

At 6:20 on a wet weekday morning, Hạnh is opening her small photocopy shop beside a vocational school. Her younger brother Duy arrives to borrow the shop motorbike for an interview he has not told their father about. A regular customer is already waiting for twenty bound application sets promised at 6:30. The printer has begun pulling two sheets at once.

## Reader experience

Let the scene feel like ordinary family pressure becoming unexpectedly tender. The interest should come from what the people choose while the shop work keeps moving, not from a twist or a speech explaining the family.

## Outcome boundary

Duy leaves with the motorbike and with Hạnh's old raincoat. Hạnh does not solve his conflict with their father. The customer receives a credible practical answer, though the order need not be finished inside the scene.

## Freedom

The writer owns wording, rhythm, dialogue length, micro-order, incidental actions and the exact way Hạnh decides to help. Summary, interiority, humor, overlap and silence are all available. No horror beat is required in this isolated test.
