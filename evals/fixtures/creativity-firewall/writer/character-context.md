# Character context — isolated fixture

## Hạnh

Thirty-four, runs the shop alone most mornings. She has spent years being treated as the reliable child and dislikes being volunteered for family mediation. She loves Duy without finding him especially sensible. When busy, she can hold a conversation while sorting paper, counting change and noticing what another person has failed to prepare.

## Duy

Twenty-four, trained as a refrigeration technician and has been taking temporary jobs since graduation. He wants the interview because it is the first position with steady hours, but expects their father to call it beneath the family. He and Hạnh can tease each other without asking whether they are close.

## Shared history and present pressure

The motorbike is registered to Hạnh but their father paid part of its original cost and still speaks of it as family property. Duy once lent Hạnh his last savings when the shop's cutter broke; neither of them invokes that debt ceremonially. Hạnh's old raincoat is oversized on Duy and has a repaired left sleeve.

## Customer

Ms. Phương teaches at the vocational school and orders from Hạnh often. She expects competence and can be impatient, but she is not an antagonist and has no interest in the siblings' private problem.
