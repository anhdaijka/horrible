# Character seeds — isolated fixture

## Mai

Thirty-one, works the late shift at a neighborhood pharmacy. She is capable and tired, dislikes being treated as the family's default organizer, and has arranged three days off to settle her mother home. She knows the building's daily rhythms but has been sleeping elsewhere during the hospital stay.

## Bà Hảo

Sixty-two, recovering well after a short hospital stay. She wants her daughter to stop hovering and wants to resume feeding the two stray cats near the bicycle shed. She minimizes her own fatigue but is not confused, delirious or cognitively impaired.

## Building life

The lift is slow but currently in service. Several households are reorganizing shared storage before the rainy season. The ground-floor tailor sometimes receives parcels for neighbors. A child upstairs has misplaced a school-project model. None of these details is preselected as a clue or required beat.
