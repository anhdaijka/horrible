# Author locks — isolated fixture

Genre: contemporary Vietnamese ghost horror with mystery. The reader should be able to assemble a stronger hypothesis than any single character, but the opening must not solve the haunting.

Chapter purpose: open with Mai bringing her mother back to their aging six-floor apartment building after a short hospital stay. By the end, the lift opens on a lit corridor labeled tầng 7 even though the building has no seventh floor. Mai and her mother do not stand together when each encounters the anomaly.

Truth/reveal boundary:

- the seventh-floor event is genuinely supernatural;
- do not reveal who or what causes it;
- no character is a paranormal investigator;
- do not introduce police, CCTV enhancement, ritual experts or an explanatory ghost legend in the opening;
- any concrete Vietnamese medical, ritual or legal claim would need support and can be omitted here.

Presentation: close third through Mai for the main chapter. A very short objective fragment elsewhere in the building is allowed only if it strengthens fair mystery without explaining it.
