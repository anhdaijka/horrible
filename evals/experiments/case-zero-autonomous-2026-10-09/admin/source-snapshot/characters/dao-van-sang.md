# Đào Văn Sang (Cò đất Sang)

## Role

A 45-year-old local land broker and property speculator (*cò đất*) operating around Chợ Bến and the surrounding communes. Shrewd, opportunistic, and predatory, he maintains an informal intelligence network monitoring local land disputes, debts, and inheritance feuds.

In Case Zero (**D-049**, **D-050**):
- **The Land Speculation Catalyst:** Sang has inside knowledge of the district's road-widening and culvert project that will transform the frontage along the lotus pond behind Cường's house into prime commercial real estate worth billions.
- **Predatory Circling at the Funeral:** Sang attends the funeral of ông Cường under the guise of paying respects, but in reality he circles Toàn, exploiting Toàn's massive dump-truck debts and post-death panic to pressure him into signing over or pledging the lotus pond land at a fraction of its true value. His machinations stoke intense family tension and provide a dense red herring of mundane human greed.

## Core traits

Slick, calculating, outwardly friendly with a veneer of rural camaraderie, ruthlessly attentive to leverage and vulnerability.

## Physical presence

Around 45 years old, stocky build, slicked-back dyed black hair, sun-bronzed skin, gold ring on his pinky finger.

Key physical anchors:
- Dressed in a semi-casual leather jacket over a polo shirt, shiny leather shoes spattered with dried country mud.
- Constantly chewing gum or smoking luxury cigarettes, checking a smartphone in a faux-leather flip case.
- Shifty, calculating smile that never reaches his narrowed eyes.

## Narrative reference

Referred to in narration and dialogue as `Sang`, `Sang "cò"`, or `ông Sang`.
