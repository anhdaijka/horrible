# Verified Research — Votive-Shop Language & Ordering v1

Status: verified starter note, not story canon.

Scope: practical vocabulary and commercial behavior for scenes in Lan's shop. This note deliberately separates **observed modern retail language** from **religious/ritual truth**.

## 1. Safe commercial observations

### Current sellers use ordinary retail units rather than one sacred unit system

Across current retail/e-commerce listings, observed units include:

- `bộ` — e.g. a clothing set / grouped goods;
- `xấp` — stacks of paper money / paper goods;
- `tép` — subdivision used in some paper-money listings;
- `đôi` — shoes and paired objects;
- `chiếc` — individual modeled objects;
- `túi` / `đóng túi` — packaging / retail presentation;
- `combo` / `set` — modern retail grouping language.

Examples / source observations:
- Hàng Mã Việt Nam product catalogue: https://hangmavietnam.vn/
- Shopee current shop/catalogue examples: https://shopee.vn/vangmadothotuetam
- Lazada listing showing `1 xấp 10 tép`: https://www.lazada.vn/products/hang-ma-vang-ma-giay-cung-ao-binh-quan-lon-i823958555.html

Confidence: high that these words exist in current commerce; **low that any one term is universal in Bắc Ninh shop speech**.

Story implication:
- Lan may naturally say `bộ`, `xấp`, `đôi`, `chiếc` when the object itself makes the unit obvious;
- avoid inventing a single ritualized unit vocabulary for all goods;
- `combo`/`set` may appear in online commerce but would sound more commercial/modern than Lan's older local shop speech, so use only if character/context supports it.

### Goods are commonly segmented by recipient type / clothing style

Current commercial catalogues visibly distinguish products such as:

- male / female;
- `cụ ông` / `cụ bà`;
- young/adult male clothing;
- children / baby goods;
- vest, shirt, áo dài, shoes and accessories.

Sources:
- Shopee `Vàng Mã Đồ Thờ Tuệ Tâm`: https://shopee.vn/vangmadothotuetam
- current clothing catalogue example: https://www.dichvutamlinh.com/quan-ao-vang-ma

Confidence: high for existence of market segmentation; medium for how a local shop labels each category orally.

Story implication:
- a shopkeeper plausibly asks enough about the intended recipient to choose a male/female/older/younger style;
- exact `tuổi âm` is **not yet verified as a necessary retail field** merely for ordinary clothing selection.

## 2. Occasion language is commercially real, but does not define doctrine

Song Hồ reporting documents customer ordering around occasions including:

- lễ;
- giỗ;
- 49 ngày;
- 100 ngày;
- sang cát;
- Tết.

Source:
- VnExpress, 23/08/2023: https://vnexpress.net/oto-moto-vang-ma-cung-ram-thang-7-4643826.html

Current retail sites also sell or label goods for `cúng giỗ`, `49 ngày`, `100 ngày`, `cúng thất / cúng tuần`.

Source examples:
- https://vangmasaigon.com/
- https://shopee.vn/vangmadothotuetam

Confidence: high that these occasion labels exist in current commerce.

Do not infer:
- a universally fixed product package for each occasion;
- that a commercial `combo 49 ngày` represents Buddhist doctrine;
- that every Bắc Ninh family uses the same bundle.

## 3. Custom orders are real

Multiple sources document made-to-order and non-standard paper objects.

Broad craft/reporting sources:
- Nhân Dân 2025, `Bạc vàng hàng mã (kỳ 1)`: https://nhandan.vn/bac-vang-hang-ma-ky-1-post906571.html
- VnExpress on Song Hồ / Đông Hồ production and customer orders: https://vnexpress.net/oto-moto-vang-ma-cung-ram-thang-7-4643826.html
- older VnExpress production reporting: https://vnexpress.net/biet-thu-oto-iphone-vang-ma-cung-ram-thang-7-3268443.html

Current commercial examples advertise handmade custom objects with lead times around 7–15 days for elaborate pieces:
- https://vangmasaigon.com/biet-thu-vang-ma-vip
- https://vangmasaigon.com/bo-golf-lam-theo-yeu-cau-mau-dat-lam-thu-cong-dat-truoc-7-10-ngay

Confidence: high that custom manufacture exists; low that those southern/current lead times transfer directly to a simpler Bắc Ninh item.

Story implication:
- Lan can plausibly coordinate an unusual object with a maker/supplier;
- if the story uses a three-day turnaround, treat that as **specific to the Khanh family's supplier relationship / simple construction**, not as a normal industry standard.

## 4. A paper radio is plausible, but should be treated as an unusual remembered object

Older Vietnamese retail reporting explicitly listed `radio` among paper goods sold for the dead, alongside clothing, vehicles and household electronics.

Source:
- Soha, 08/08/2014: https://soha.vn/xa-hoi/ram-thang-7-dich-vu-chuyen-tien-xuong-ca-coi-am-20140808044527122.htm

Current Song Hồ reporting confirms the broader category of contemporary modeled objects (TV, phones, vehicles, hobby equipment), even where radio is not specifically shown.

Sources:
- https://vnexpress.net/biet-thu-oto-iphone-vang-ma-cung-ram-thang-7-3268443.html
- https://vnexpress.net/oto-moto-vang-ma-cung-ram-thang-7-4643826.html

Confidence: medium-high for existence/plausibility of a paper radio; **not enough to claim every shop keeps one in stock**.

Story implication:
- `đài` / `đài cassette kiểu cũ` is a plausible custom clue;
- its significance should come from the requested shape/detail and the dead family's history, not from the claim that radio-paper goods are intrinsically ritual special.

## 5. Name / age / address fields are NOT yet verified as standard shop requirements

Low-confidence modern spiritual/how-to websites frequently claim names, birth/death information or addresses can be written on paper goods or accompanying documents. Examples exist, but the source quality is not strong enough to establish a Northern retail norm.

Examples used only as **leads**, not cultural authority:
- https://hatvan.vn/cach-cung-dot-vang-ma-cho-nguoi-chet/
- https://hatvan.vn/cach-ghi-do-cung/
- https://scr.vn/cach-ghi-giay-tien-vang-ma.html

Current conclusion:

- **full recipient name:** plausible information in a personalized order, but not proven mandatory;
- **age / birth year / can-chi:** appears in low-confidence ritual guidance, but not verified as normal shop intake data;
- **address:** may appear in ritual documents or identifying information in some practices, but is not verified as a standard field for buying ordinary goods;
- **writing directly on every item:** not verified as a universal practice.

Therefore the novel must not say `người ta đặt mã thì bao giờ cũng phải ghi đủ tên tuổi địa chỉ`.

## 6. Recommended Khanh-family shop convention

To preserve Case Zero clues without inventing a national custom, the following may be used **only if author-approved as family-specific business practice**:

For custom/pickup orders, Lan's household ledger records:

- người đặt / purchaser name when known;
- phone number if provided;
- `làm cho ai` / intended recipient name when personalization matters;
- approximate category (`nam`, `nữ`, `cụ`, etc.);
- requested goods / special dimensions or model notes;
- pickup date;
- deposit / paid status;
- locality/address **only when useful for delivery, disambiguation or a customer voluntarily gives it**.

This is an administrative habit of the shop, not a ritual rule.

A crowded-period shorthand ledger may be incomplete. That gives the manuscript natural variation instead of an implausibly perfect paranormal database.

## 7. Dialogue guardrails for Lan

Prefer object-and-task speech:

- `Chị lấy bộ nam hay bộ cụ?`
- `Cái này có sẵn, cái đài thì phải đặt.`
- `Bao giờ chị lấy?`
- `Chị có ảnh hay mẫu cái đài không?`
- `Tên người nhận chị ghi cho em ra đây, không lát đông khách lại nhầm.`

These are **story-language proposals based on commercial behavior**, not transcribed ethnographic quotations. A writer may adjust them for naturalness.

Avoid unsupported pseudo-ritual dialogue such as:

- `Phải đủ tuổi âm và địa chỉ thì người dưới ấy mới nhận được.`
- `Bộ 49 ngày chuẩn bắt buộc phải có X/Y/Z.`
- `Sai một món là phạm lễ.`

unless a specific character is expressing a researched or deliberately fictional belief.

## 8. Chapter 1 audit note

The current Chapter 1 contains shop texture that should be treated as **family-specific fiction**, not regional documentary truth:

- Lan doing limited alteration/finishing to a paper garment;
- the shop's exact stock mix;
- local shorthand/pricing behavior.

These are compatible with the already approved family business model, but future prose should avoid explaining them as `người bán hàng mã thường...` unless separately sourced.

One current line such as `loại năm mươi hay loại tám mươi` has no verified referent in this research pack. If it remains in the manuscript, the context should make clear whether Lan means price, quantity or product tier; otherwise it reads as invented shop-code rather than lived speech.
