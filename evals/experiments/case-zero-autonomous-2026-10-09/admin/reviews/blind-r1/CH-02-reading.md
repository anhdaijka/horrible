## Immediate experience, in reading order

### Morning in the shop

The opening makes a decisive switch away from Lan Anh’s terror. I initially wanted to stay with her. Khanh’s first few minutes do provide a different physical and social position: he is sleepy, recently returned and newly awkward in his own parents’ shop. “Nhà mình mà đi như nhà người ta” followed by “Chuyển cái kệ từ năm ngoái” makes his absence concrete without a biography paragraph.

Mai’s authority over delivery chaos and her refusal to stand around hearing about Japan give her an independent day. The remark about lifting a suitcase into an airplane compartment is more individual than the later general joking about Khanh being decorative. I do not yet have enough Mai to know whether she will remain more than a capable, teasing friend, but the entry is lively.

News of Cường’s death interrupts an actual morning. Khanh’s inability to find the right face feels convincing for someone who knew, but was not close to, the deceased. “Người ta bảo” and the mother’s admission that she was not in the household preserve the distinction between rumour and knowledge.

The shop comedy echoes CH-01’s mother–adult-child exchanges. “Máy tính nó tự cắt” / “Thế mang máy tính ra đây” is enjoyable and in voice; however, a second chapter beginning with many parental corrections makes the family warmth mechanism more visible as an authorial habit. These families should not lose their banter, but they may need greater differentiation of conversational rhythm as the book grows.

### Thắng, the workshop and family

Thắng has useful bodily competence: he packs the load, gets the fan and handles fastening. Khanh’s encounter with Hòa is genuinely good dialogue. Her straightforward “em nhận ra vì anh đứng cạnh chồng em” defeats his automatic self-deprecation without being a written punchline explaining itself. Hòa’s concern about reliable pickup times gives the family an unresolved ordinary demand; Bống’s mismatched socks are allowed to be small.

The old bicycle story works because they disagree over who did what, and Hòa declines to arbitrate. Friendship briefly exists beyond the death. Khanh’s laughter ending at the turn towards Cường’s house is sufficient on its own; the chapter trusts the change without inserting a moral judgment.

I cannot assess how successfully Thắng appears unsuspect. My prior exposure makes every apparently generous action liable to false retrospective significance. On the actual page, he is shown repeatedly helping people, eating late and disappointing a domestic deadline; that is a plausible person, although the concentration of competence and consideration is conspicuous enough to watch over later chapters. It is not evidence of a revealed answer.

The sentence “Anh thấy mình vẫn đang nói chuyện bằng những câu cũ trong một ngày đã thay gần hết đồ đạc” is memorable, but again supplies an interpretive close after the wife, child and workplace have already embodied his changed relationship to home.

### Work at the bereaved house

The funeral-house activity does not turn into an investigation. Wrongly parked vehicles, a fan with an insufficient reach and misplaced supplies generate obligations whose scale fits the day. Toàn snapping over a blocked gate and apologising immediately conveys grief under practical pressure better than an account of his internal pain could from Khanh’s viewpoint.

Cổn acquires social authority by repeating a question until people attend, not through a dossier. His presence is legible without knowing the larger family map. Thắng’s memory of Cường obtaining his first work adds a specific reason to help and allows Cường to exist beyond the restrictive father-in-law of the opening.

The strongest scene is Lan Anh asking Khanh to remain in place. “Em cứ đứng đấy nhé” is terrifying because CH-01 supplies the missing reason, while Khanh only knows she wants him there. Her speed returning with the towels and refusal to hand them over sustain the aftermath without repeating the apparition or explaining it. This is the point at which the viewpoint switch pays for itself: I can perceive distress that the viewpoint character cannot name.

The brief bread conversation with Thắng is also worth protecting. “Cơm” / “tưởng ăn dây điện” is familiar friend talk, and the absence of the expected salary question makes Khanh feel momentarily at ease. The interruption by someone collecting water lets conversation end naturally.

There is, however, a real middle-section plateau. Successive work units — unload, find fan position, move bikes, fetch chairs, change grip, take a call, answer another request, deliver water, identify the supposedly missing item, prepare more supplies — sustain a convincing network of labour, but several perform nearly the same emotional function. The Lan Anh scene and promised six-thirty return are the new turns. Selective compression around them could preserve the day’s weight while making the chapter feel less like an inventory of helpful work. I would not remove all apparently non-mystery work or require another scare here.

### Return home, work call and preparation for evening

The new job and fixed date prevent Khanh from becoming a protagonist with unlimited leisure. His professional call shows he has a life outside these houses. The mother checking one newly equal piece of string and silently giving him more work completes a modest learning arc rather neatly.

As an ending, however, the chapter arrives twice at a resting place: lunch at home already feels like a completed movement, and the extended sleep/call/packing sequence then settles him again. The work call contains credible details but little friction or change beyond confirming obligations we already knew about. The final image — packed goods awaiting dinner — is deliberately quiet; after an entire chapter postponing the CH-01 aftermath and another return visit, it felt more like a pause between scenes than a charged chapter close. This is my strongest pacing concern at this point. It does not require a fabricated ghost incident; a tighter route to the existing evening commitment might be enough.

## Material texture and explanatory habits

The physical texture is strongest when the object is exact: the bundle ties cut to inconsistent lengths, the fan cord, the chair-stack marks, Hòa’s seam under an arm. Conversely, “hàng”, “gói”, “món”, “một chồng hàng” recur often around the shop’s and bereaved household’s principal business. After a long day spent moving them, I still have only a hazy picture of what this shop mainly sells. This is a local concreteness issue, not a request for factual ritual instruction. A few ordinary, accurate object names could give the labour more distinctiveness; avoid inventing culturally consequential details for colour.

The explanatory-closure habit noticed in CH-01 continues here. “Khách này… cũng không đến để nghe chuyện chết” tells me the scene is independent of the death; the customer asking for another colour already establishes that. “Câu chuyện ngừng ở đó, không ai cần khép lại cho trọn” explains a natural interruption that would work without its endorsement. Such lines intermittently expose the construction principle of the prose. They are not forbidden forms, but the accumulated recurrence now merits a deliberate self-edit for explanations whose removal would leave the experience intact.

## Current expectations and open questions

- The exact six-thirty-to-eight commitment makes me expect Khanh’s evening return to matter. Lan Anh’s unusual gratitude loads it emotionally without identifying a task for him.
- I want to learn what Lan Anh can say, to whom, and how staying in the house costs her. CH-02 proves the fear persists; it has not yet let her deal with it.
- Khanh now has believable reasons both to remain locally useful and to leave for work. I expect that conflict to become lived rather than merely scheduled.
- Thắng’s role cannot be predicted independently by this reader. No claim about his innocence, culpability or reveal fairness follows from these observations.
- I do not yet know whether the chapter’s long ordinary-day span is essential counterweight or the beginning of an overextended setup. Another similarly expansive detour before the evening would increase the latter concern.

## Provisional editorial priorities

Protect the external view of Lan Anh’s fear, Hòa’s independent voice, Mai’s work urgency, Toàn’s brief loss of patience, the friendship’s inconclusive humour, and the existing evening promise.

Most useful revision consideration: compress repetitive service errands and some of the second homecoming/call tail while retaining the specific obligations, employment pressure and changed family relationships. Do not add an artificial scare to solve a length problem.

Secondary consideration: name a few actual goods so the central day’s work is less abstract, and audit explanatory sentences that describe what the immediately preceding scene has already accomplished.
