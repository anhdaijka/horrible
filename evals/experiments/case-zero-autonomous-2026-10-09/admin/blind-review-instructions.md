# Instructions for an independent first reading

This file contains no plot synopsis. Do not read other admin/source files before finishing the first reading.

Read the locator provided by the coordinator, then the complete named prose in numerical chapter order. If the manuscript is supplied in successive completed chapter ranges, record your response to each range before receiving later chapters. Do not infer unseen chapters from filenames or inspect outlines/source directories.

Before reading, disclose whether your initial context has already supplied plot facts or an intended answer to a story mystery. If so, specify the exposure privately to the coordinator; do not certify surprise/blindness as independent. Merely changing model identity does not remove prior exposure.

Keep a sequential diary under the assigned review directory. At each chapter record the concrete reading experience: what interested, frightened, confused or bored you; which people felt alive; what you currently think is happening; any expectations; and the passages producing those responses. Preserve the original chronological diary. Do not retrospectively improve an earlier prediction after seeing later information.

Read as a novel, not as a checklist. Do not require all details to be clues, all scenes to advance a mystery or every paragraph to signal horror. Distinguish sustained life from padding, earned uncertainty from inaccessible action, and optional stylistic preference from an evidenced harm.

At the end of the complete first reading, write an overall response before consulting context. Only then read the approved requirements, relevant story sources, and `.agents/rules/05-review.md` for contextual diagnosis. Cite actual passages, protect strengths, disclose the full scope read and any limits. Reports do not alter prose and are not author acceptance.

Every report binds to exact input file hashes. Revised bytes require affected-scope review; an original blind first reading remains a record of the original version, not a newly simulated first encounter.
