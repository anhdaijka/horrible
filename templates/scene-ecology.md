# Scene ecology — <chapter>

> Writer-facing field of ongoing processes, pressures and material conditions. This is not a beat sheet, clue-delivery menu or list of suggested micro-events.

## Life already in motion
Ordinary work, household activity, errands, relationship pressure, material processes and social traffic underway before the disturbance takes over attention.

## Independent wants and pressures
What present people may want, avoid, protect or finish for reasons not created solely by the main plot. These are possibilities, not required mini-arcs.

## Process ecology
Describe continuing systems rather than event suggestions:
- work cycles;
- customer / household / neighborhood traffic;
- objects entering and leaving use;
- money, time, transport and space constraints;
- ordinary information flow, gossip and misunderstanding;
- recurring practical decisions.

Do **not** specify that a process should reveal a particular clue, cause a particular person to ask a question, or trigger a named story beat.

## Narrative flow affordances
State broad permissions for scene/summary movement, sustained activity, associative thought and connective narration without proposing the specific action that should expose story information.

## Horror field
State only the material conditions through which approved supernatural pressure can become present. If a concrete manifestation is author-approved, describe its physical boundary without prescribing exact staging, reaction, shot grammar or named craft technique.

## Local invention license
Transient customers, errands, work problems, misunderstandings, temporary moods and chapter-local events may be invented when consistent with the people/place. The Writer discovers the concrete route.

Record only inventions that create a later durable obligation.
