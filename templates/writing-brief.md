## Core disturbance and reader experience
What ghostly, dreadful or spiritually uncanny pressure enters the chapter? Why does it hurt or matter to these people, and what feeling/question should remain with the reader?

## Story truth and reveal edge
State only the consequential outcome, chronology, current knowledge, reveal limit and durable state that make the scene true. Prefer positive statements. Move verification detail and long exclusions to qa-card.md.

## Reader-detective field
What observable facts or discrepancies must remain fairly available? What may the reader connect before the viewpoint character does? Do not prescribe the deduction, dialogue or exact staging, and do not turn every atmospheric or personal detail into a clue.

## Dramatic space
What choices remain genuinely open for the people and the writer? Point to the ecology rather than converting its possibilities into required beats. The main plot may interrupt, distort or acquire meaning from ordinary life; it need not occupy every action.

## Concrete reality and safe sources
Useful verified details and lived context already safe for the writer. Do not link to full sources containing future solutions. Do not replace practical needs with vague "things" to avoid research.

## Execution freedom
Local events, transient interactions, mistakes, cooperation, timing, narrative summary/interiority and dialogue length are open within the truth boundary. The writer may select, combine, transform or discard ecology options. Options are not compulsory beats.

New texture and chapter-local life are free. Record separately any new promise, established prior event, custody change, future meeting, distinctive object fact or durable knowledge that creates a cross-scene obligation.

## Deliberately unanswered
Questions the prose may leave implicit or the writer may decide locally. Do not include a hidden expected answer or QA test here.
