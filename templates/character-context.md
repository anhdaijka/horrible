# Character context — <chapter>
Writer-safe selection; no future solution. Copy the needed fact or give a precise safe range. A section title that also contains future material is not a safe boundary.

## Relationships and address
Who is related to whom, social familiarity, age/status where relevant; natural address in this interaction.

## People beyond the plot
Shared history, ordinary priorities, ongoing work/family/social concerns, ways of helping or irritating one another, contradictions and pressures that exist even if the case does not advance today. Give enough lived material for the writer to imagine behavior rather than merely satisfy metadata. Distinguish source facts from optional situational choices.

## Agency outside the case

What can each present person choose, postpone, protect, misunderstand or pursue for reasons not reducible to the main plot? These are affordances, not hidden-agenda requirements or miniature arcs every character must perform.

Do not copy body-language menus, catchphrases, example dialogue, voice recipes or prescribed fear reactions from profiles. Those are optional diagnostic reservoirs, not generative instructions.

## Knowledge and viewpoint
What they perceived earlier, believe now and can plausibly infer here; what narration may access. Separate unknown, mistaken belief and known-but-withheld.

## Targeted sources and gaps
Only generative-safe lived-context excerpts belong here. Source path + exact allowed excerpt/range + purpose. If a safe range cannot be isolated, project the fact here instead. QA sources belong in qa-card.md. Resolve material gaps before writing; no mandatory length.
