# Worldbuilding

## World Overview

Contemporary Northern Vietnam. The opening story world is a fictional semi-urbanizing hometown on the former Bắc Giang side of the contemporary Bắc Ninh region, roughly 1–2 hours from Hanoi. Ordinary family commerce, market logistics, ancestor practice, Buddhist life, folk belief and modern work coexist without being collapsed into one occult system.
