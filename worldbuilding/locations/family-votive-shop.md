# Nhà và tiệm vàng mã của gia đình Khanh

## Description

A long-established family home and votive-paper shop on a main road beside a busy market crossroads in a fictional semi-urbanizing hometown on the former Bắc Giang side of the contemporary Bắc Ninh region.

The shop sells ordinary stocked votive goods and also handles custom orders and limited finishing/customization. Generic stock may come through wholesale supply networks; the family does not manufacture every item itself.

## Story function

This is a warm, practical home base before it becomes a source of uncanny incidents. Customers, funeral and memorial orders, market traffic, family routine and oral stories naturally bring information into the household.

Culture and commerce here must remain ordinary life first. The presence of incense, ritual goods or spiritual language is not supernatural evidence.
