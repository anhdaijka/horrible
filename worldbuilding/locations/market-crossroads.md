# Ngã tư chợ quê Khanh

## Description

A busy crossroads and market environment immediately around Khanh's family home and shop. Food and produce logistics begin before dawn, often around 3–4 a.m., so trucks, shutters, chopping, vendors, dogs, motorbikes and loading noise are normal parts of the soundscape.

The wider hometown mixes older lanes and houses with warehouses, new roads, industrial growth, remaining fields, waterways, cemeteries and low hills.

## Story function

The market provides social density, movement and plausible mundane explanations for sounds or sightings that might otherwise feel uncanny. Crowds can hide a strange presence more effectively than an empty haunted place.
