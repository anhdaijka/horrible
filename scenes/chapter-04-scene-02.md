# Trong rạp tang xóm Trại

## Purpose

Khanh enters the funeral tent at Cường's house. He navigates mourning etiquette, encounters former high school classmate Thắng helping out at the wake, exchanges social small talk, observes lime powder masking bloodstains around the stairs, the open coffin awaiting the khâm liệm hour, and overhears village elders discussing the horrifying 180-degree neck twist, fractured cervical vertebrae, clawed plaster, and the ritual specialist's warning about dangerous death hours and trùng tang taboos.

## Continuity Notes

Early afternoon, 14th of Giêng. Verified contemporary Northern funeral practices (Decision 116/2025/QĐ-UBND, lime powder disinfection, kèn bát âm, coffin preparation).
