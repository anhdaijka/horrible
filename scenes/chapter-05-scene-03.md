# Dị tượng trên mặt kính

## Purpose

At dusk (approx 17:45), the market empties as evening loudspeakers play. Examining the paper radio and blueprint sketch, Khanh notices the radio dial is inscribed with FM 102.5 MHz from the left-handed sketch. An eerie supernatural auditory manifestation occurs: an unmistakable static hiss of an empty cassette magnetic tape against a playback head emanates from the empty paper compartment, and the paper door pops open as if an invisible finger hit Eject. A frantic phone call from cô Thủy punctures the quiet: sobbing in sheer terror, she screams that Cường's letter reveals Thoa has returned to reclaim her cassette radio, followed by shattering porcelain and an abrupt dial tone.

## Continuity Notes

Dusk, 14th of Giêng. Climax and high-pressure cliffhanger leading to the night before Rằm.
