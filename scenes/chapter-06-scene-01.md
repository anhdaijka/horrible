# Khúc rẽ lầm lạc

## Purpose

Following the abrupt cutoff and shattered porcelain scream on cô Thủy's phone call, Khanh and bà Lan launch an immediate, desperate emergency response into the freezing night of the 14th (Đêm áp Rằm). 

Crucially executes the author's mandate on realistic human panic errors:
- Bà Lan drops the key ring in shaking terror; Khanh's frozen fingers fumble with the padlock on the folding iron gate.
- The cold Honda Wave RSX engine stalls repeatedly, requiring the choke lever and frantic kickstarter stomps before sputtering to life.
- Transporting the paper cassette radio wrapped in black plastic bag, bà Lan sits behind shivering and clutching the occult cargo, jumping at every flutter of paper.
- Nocturnal disorientation: Khanh has only walked through xóm Trại once during daytime; bà Lan has not ventured deep into xóm Trại's night alleys for years. Dense winter fog and dim yellow headlight obscure vision.
- Clumsy route errors: Bà Lan gives conflicting directions (*"Rẽ phải... ơ rẽ trái chỗ cây rơm to"*), plunging them into a dead-end alley with hanging clothes flapping like ghosts; while backing up, the rear wheel nearly skids off an uncovered foul ditch.
- Rural atmosphere: Village dogs bark persistently from house to house, chasing the sputtering motorbike as if sensing an invisible passenger clinging to their luggage.

## Continuity Notes

Evening of 14th of Giêng (approx 19:00 - 19:25). Strict Limited 3rd POV via Khanh. Heavy sensory immersion: cold diesel fumes, alkaline dust, rotting ditch stench, damp winter mist.
