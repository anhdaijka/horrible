# Ngọn đèn dầu bên áo quan

## Purpose

Grounding Khanh and bà Lan in the nocturnal wake house of ông Cường at approx 19:30 on the 14th of Giêng.

Key dramatic beats:
- The chilling sensory atmosphere of a rural wake at night: mournful wind rattling the blue canvas tent, pungent smell of lime dust mixed with cold embalming herbal incense and faint decay. The dark dổi wood coffin rests heavy and unsealed in the central hall under the feeble yellow flame of a single oil lamp.
- Exhausted kin: The son-in-law (wearing a white mourning headband, dark circles around hollow eyes) lies curled on a straw mat near the veranda, startled awake by their arrival.
- Bà Lan's shrewd social cover: Rather than blurting out ghost stories, she maintains social composure, claiming cô Thủy phoned sounding exhausted and faint, so they brought medicated eucalyptus oil to check on her before nightfall.
- The son-in-law explains cô Thủy retreated to the rear bedroom at dusk, locking the door and insisting on not being disturbed due to an excruciating headache.
- Tension mounts as they move to the back bedroom: a heavy wooden door with an internal bolt. Banging on the door produces no response, only the eerie sound of winter wind whistling through the window slats.

## Continuity Notes

Evening of 14th of Giêng (approx 19:25 - 19:40). Strict adherence to Northern mourning etiquette and Limited 3rd POV.
