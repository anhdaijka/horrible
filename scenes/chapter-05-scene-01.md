# Bụi vôi trên đế giày

## Purpose

Following his departure from the wake in xóm Trại, Khanh navigates the spatial and sensory transition back to the market crossroads. He cleans thick white lime powder (used to cover dried blood from ông Cường's fatal fall) from his sneaker soles on a ditch rock. His programmer's logic undergoes intense cognitive dissonance: cô Thủy's genuine deformed, clawed left hand and factory alibi completely disprove the living-impersonator theory, leaving the CCTV entity an impossible horror. Contrast with the lively pre-Rằm market bustle sharpens the dread before he re-enters the shop and sees the paper radio.

## Continuity Notes

Late afternoon, 14th of Giêng (approx 15:30 - 16:00). SOLID spatial transition and sensory grounding.
