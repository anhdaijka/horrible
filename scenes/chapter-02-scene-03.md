# Chiếc đài nằm lại

## Purpose

Midday market pressure brings neighborhood gossip confirming Cường's sudden death. Oral folklore/trade beliefs around pre-death votive orders emerge. Khanh confronts the uncanny physical presence of the uncollected paper radio and notices the shop's CCTV camera overhead, setting up Chapter 3's investigation.

## Continuity Notes

The paper cassette radio remains in the shop uncollected. The shop CCTV camera is established as an ordinary commercial security device above the entrance.
