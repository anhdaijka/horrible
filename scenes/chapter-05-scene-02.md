# Bàn giao mặt sau quầy

## Purpose

Khanh returns to the shop and debriefs bà Lan behind closed half-shutters. He reports the visceral funeral realities (wooden splints holding the fractured neck, lime covering blood, taboos against trùng tang, cô Thủy's deformed left hand, and the unstamped envelope under the pillow). Bà Lan counters with oral history gathered from tea-stall matriarchs: thirty years ago (1996), the Vũ family had a forgotten middle sister named Vũ Thị Thoa who was naturally left-handed. She drowned in 1996 amid bitter, buried family disputes; the Confucian patriarch erased her name from the genealogy, burned all photos and belongings, but her treasured foreign cassette radio was never found.
