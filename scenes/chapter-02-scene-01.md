# Không phải người nhà

## Purpose

Return to the present line on the morning of the 14th of Giêng. Bà Lan's natural mundane hypothesis (advance preparation for an expected death) is raised and immediately dismantled by the relative's testimony: ông Cường was completely healthy, died suddenly during the night from an accidental stair fall, and no one in the household knows any "Vũ Thị Thoa".

## Continuity Notes

Scene occurs on morning of 14th of Giêng. The relative purchases ordinary male clothing and paper money for immediate khâm liệm, but refuses the custom cassette radio because no one ordered it.
