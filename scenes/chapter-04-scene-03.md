# Người về từ Yên Phong

## Purpose

The real cô Thủy arrives in grief from Yên Phong by motorbike taxi. Khanh observes her lighting incense: she uses her right hand while her left arm hangs limp with a severely deformed, scarred wrist and rigid fingers incapable of drawing or writing. Cường's daughter-in-law whispers about an unstamped envelope found under Cường's pillow addressed to cô Thủy, plunging her into fresh terror.

## Continuity Notes

Early afternoon, 14th of Giêng. Physical verification completely refutes human identity overlap between cô Thủy and the CCTV woman. Forward hook planted without leaking letter contents or family drowning history.
