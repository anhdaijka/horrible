# Bát nhang quầy mã

## Purpose

Following cô Thủy's terrified phone disconnection, bà Lan lights incense at the altar and tasks Khanh with going to Cường's wake in xóm Trại. Khanh balks, challenging the premise since the shop has no kinship or closeness with Cường and only received a passing polite invite this morning. Bà Lan reveals the visceral votive-trade taboo: holding an advance custom order for a sudden-death victim ("đồ đoạt mệnh") that the family refused will curse the shop if held over Rằm. She tasks Khanh with using the morning invite as cover to pay respects, appease the spirit, verify if cô Thủy arrives from Yên Phong, and tactfully probe if the radio can be consigned to the coffin, while bà Lan stays behind to investigate the name Thoa at the market tea stalls.

## Continuity Notes

Early afternoon, 14th of Giêng. Mother-son dialogue grounded in northern vernacular, trade taboos, and psychological realism.
