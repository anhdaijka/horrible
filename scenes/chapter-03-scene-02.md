# Gương mặt người sống và bàn tay trái

## Purpose

Khanh and bà Lan examine the footage on the mezzanine. Bà Lan recognizes the woman's face as cô Thủy (Cường's younger sister), but Khanh points out that she writes effortlessly with her left hand, whereas cô Thủy is strictly right-handed. The woman ends by looking directly into the camera lens.

## Continuity Notes

Midday 14th of Giêng. Visual resemblance shock establishes the doppelgänger/uncanny tension. Left-handed trait breaks the mundane assumption. Direct gaze into the lens breaks psychological safety.
