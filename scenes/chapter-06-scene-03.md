# Tin nhắn lúc nửa đêm

## Purpose

Breaking the lock to cô Thủy's rear bedroom and confronting the dual-layer horror and detective revelations of Case Zero.

Key dramatic beats:
- Khanh and the son-in-law breach the door, splintering the old wooden latch.
- The macabre tableau: Cô Thủy lies sprawled cold on the tiled floor beside the bed, teeth clenched, unconscious. A shattered porcelain teacup lies scattered in a puddle of cold brown tea. Her withered, scarred left hand claws at her collar, while her right hand clutches her smartphone (screen active on Zalo conversation with "Anh Cường (xóm Trại)").
- Dual-Layer Window Anomaly (Detective-Horror Synergy):
  - The window facing the old lotus pond is unlatched and swung wide open, winter wind billowing floral curtains.
  - Plausible acoustic explanation: A broken chinaberry branch (*cành xoan gãy*) taps rhythmically against the wooden bars in the wind (explaining the tapping sounds).
  - Fair-play physical impossibility / Dread: On the peeling whitewashed sill are damp, black pond-mud smears shaped like five slender fingers — an unmistakable **left-hand print**, smelling intensely of stagnant pond rot.
- The Epistemic Core — Ông Cường's 38-Second Midnight Voice Message:
  - While bà Lan and the son-in-law tend to cô Thủy, Khanh retrieves the smartphone from her clenched fingers.
  - On the screen is a 38-second Zalo voice message timestamped **22:54 — 13 Thg 01** (barely 10–15 minutes before ông Cường's fatal fall down the stairs).
  - Khanh plays the audio: background wind/rain static gives way to the chilling tape hiss of a Czech cassette player warbling Thoa's song out by the lotus pond; ông Cường's terrified, breathless whisper (*"Thủy ơi... mày thức không... Đêm nay tao lại nghe tiếng cái đài Tiệp của con Thoa ngoài bờ đầm... Nó về thật rồi mày ơi... Chuyện năm ấy ở đầm sen không giấu được nữa đâu... Cái đài năm xưa tao giấu ở..."*).
  - Chilling acoustic intrusion: wet dragging steps (*bạch... bạch...*), sharp fingernails scratching down the wooden staircase rail (*kẹttt... kẹttt...*), a strangled gasp, a heavy thud of a body tumbling down the stairs, and abrupt silence.
  - Confirms ông Cường's death was sudden, violent, and uncanny (*chết bất đắc kỳ tử*), while linking cô Thủy's hysteria and phone call directly to having just listened to this recording in the quiet back room.
- The chapter closes with an uncanny physical resonance: The black plastic bag holding the paper radio on the wooden table puffs up, emitting a faint dry rustle of paper and a whisper of tape static. Cô Thủy regains shallow consciousness, her eyes rolling in terror, pointing her deformed arm toward the black package: *"Nó... nó ngồi... trên nóc đài..."*
