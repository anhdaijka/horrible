## A. The governing principle: distinguish people by WHAT they are doing in conversation

A Vietnamese line of dialogue is not “natural” just because it is short, colloquial, includes mày or includes a hedging filler. Each exchange requires **the exact listener, an independently motivated purpose, the speaker's actual information, what they risk by speaking, and something they might do instead of answering**. A character may answer only one word if that one-word choice changes the other person's options, stops a conversation, conceals a reason or truthfully meets a practical demand. A character should not answer only one word because the writer needs a straight line for the next speaker's joke.

**Do not clone rhythm:** the inherited S01 A/B tendency “one complains → the other says one line → the first caps the exchange with a punchline” is NOT the default engine. Alternate between exchanges in which somebody has the last word, exchanges terminated by paid work, exchanges where the supposedly submissive person defines a boundary, somebody choosing not to answer, an incorrect assumption getting practically corrected, a speaker genuinely revising a plan, and exchanges that conclude through mundane coordinated action. Humor is welcome **when earned by this particular pair**, but may be absent for an entire serious scene without losing naturalism.

**Speech act inventory, not line formula:** to ask for help, instruct, negotiate deadline, check responsibility, save face, console, coax, deny having seen something, limit liability, reframe the question, force a witness to choose, deflect through a practical task. Map which act a person uses in THIS scene, not a fixed global profile. Two people both saying “mày” can create care in one room and oppression in another.

**Information firewall:** dialogue is LIMITED by actual established D-011–15 viewpoint knowledge, never expository gifts:
- In S01 bà Lan/Khanh don't know recipient **Vũ Văn Cường** is Lan Anh's father-in-law or that he was alive when ordered; the busy paper-radio sale was ordinary to the seller. Trại is distant from Bến; three different people are named Cường in Trại. S03 is the FIRST earned family/full-name identification. Keep A's ordinary shop pressures, NOT its accidental clairvoyance.
- Toàn and Khanh have NEVER met or directly interacted before story time. Toàn knows part of the **real 1996 death of aunt Thoa**, used it as pressure to obtain the deed from his father; his wife Lan Anh does NOT share his inside knowledge. After S03 she reports customer-name/shop link, he fears exposure and one-sidedly envies the never-met Khanh. Do not accidentally make his suspicious speech proof of an affair or ghost testimony.
- Cổn's deliberate use of Thủy's authentic 2026 hoaxes to discredit her true *narrow* 1996 discovery is AFTER G6a credible remains identification and before full G6b; do not transplant that exchange into an early generic money dispute.
- Long reaches shop through public Chợ Bến rumor, confirms identity independently and doesn't solicit Khanh to hack. None of the living knows the author-only G6c spectral mechanism by verbal intuition.
- Black neighbor cat objectively jumps across the returned family coffin, causing grave fear and wrong inference, but **NO quỷ nhập tràng** or corpse animation; superstition is attributed to fearful speakers, not narratorial law.

## B. Relationship-specific register rather than a global list of Vietnamese words

**Bà Lan → Khanh:** intimately, ordinarily can call him **mày**, sometimes **con**, sometimes **anh** when annoyed or pointing out an adult obligation; can use his name or omit the subject. Choose from state and gesture, not a quota of mày per scene. She may ask, delegate or simply hand a box to someone. Never write every “mày” as anger; familiarity can be affectionate. She can recognize he owes the Japanese employer work time without treating him like a child, while still wanting him to help at home. **Khanh → bà Lan:** can keep **con/mẹ**, argue over his schedule with substantive information, act on a compromise, say he cannot cover a whole delivery route, yet still voluntarily help for a bounded interval. He is a 27-year-old wage-earning adult, NOT a model student answering “vâng ạ” after each maternal jab.

**Bà Lan → younger customer:** the same mother who calls her son mày might call a younger customer **cháu**, **em**, or **chị** depending on age, habit, familiarity and street-market service context. She does not necessarily use the same “chị” she might use for a near-peer. Young customers likewise can say **cô/cháu** or **chị/em** depending on who they truly address. An unpaid order, a complex custom build or a fixed delivery time naturally changes register and the degree of explanation. A young customer may know what she wants but NOT have final say when family requested it; that doesn't mean she exists only for a punchline.

**Khanh ↔ Lan Anh (when later met):** childhood/school acquaintance, she senior by 1–2 cohorts, once liked him; their old school register, adult courtesy and her current funeral state may conflict. Don't make their actual interaction romantic simply because Toàn fears it. At S01 they DO NOT meet; cannot paste any later chemistry into bà Lan's interior.

**Lan Anh ↔ Toàn:** established marriage with tenderness, fatigue, financial realities, uneven control and real consequences for truth-telling. Who addresses whom as anh/em may be stable while *control* changes through insistence on detail, interruption, silence, moving away, offering to do a practical task or refusing to testify to something unwitnessed. Lan Anh can defer on a harmless funeral task yet firmly say **what she will not say to officials**, even after her culpable shirt concealment. Toàn can genuinely love her or grieve Cường but questions/accusations about Khanh remain **his unsupported interpretations**; do NOT give him a cartoon villain's uninterrupted speech.

**Cổn ↔ Thủy:** father/daughter with a long financial history and unequal power. Cổn's “mày” can convey entitlement, reinterpret daughterly care as lifetime obligation, take credit for work, assert family precedent and sometimes express practical affection. It must feel distinct from bà Lan's ordinary mother-son rough familiarity. Under D-023 Thủy uses father-address **thầy/con** (mother **u** only if relevant), may withhold agreement, ask to inspect a present-day account, repeat an exact financial distinction, make a choice about her own labor or walk away. She has lived decades of this relationship; she can be fluent in its tactics, not trapped eternally in submissive monosyllables. She cannot claim a violent 1996 blow she did NOT witness.

**Long ↔ shopkeeper / colleague / suspect:** police work demands finite, documentable questions and careful listening, but Long has workday habits outside interrogations. With bà Lan he first establishes actual customer/payment/hand-off source, not supernatural confession; with a colleague shorthand may be concise; with a possible suspect he can return to a chronological gap rather than trading sarcasm. No universal stiff “tôi yêu cầu...” speech or magically accurate first theory.

## C. Voice profiles: stable priorities, variable spoken realization

### 1) Bà Trần Thị Lan — commerce first, filial warmth without always softening

- **Own conversational goal:** keep work moving, preserve reputation and paid customer commitments, get enough specific information for the actual next action. She can care about Lan Anh without knowing her in-laws’ surname.
- **Natural operations:** triage interruptions; choose a quicker feasible option; repeat a detail only when it risks mistakes; sometimes quit an unfinished story to serve another customer. She is not constantly auditioning jokes.
- **Tempo:** may give a long answer explaining why a delivery promise is impossible, issue a brisk instruction while hands are busy, or decline speculative conversation with “để lát”. Not mechanically a three-line banter sequence.
- **By audience:** with Khanh can use mày/anh/con within natural relationship; with younger customer explains cost/required information; with a peer trader checks credit and timings; with Long switches to exact remembered business facts, correcting his inference when it exceeds them.
- **Pressure shift:** after S03 credible identification, legitimately checks slip/order dates and worries about making an ill-timed paid delivery; BEFORE S03 she cannot be haunted by knowledge of living recipient.
- **Test for uniqueness:** Could another character replace her line without knowing a craft process, a customer handoff or a household obligation? If yes, give the line an action/context rather than add a catchphrase.

### 2) Nguyễn Minh Khanh — independent adult temporarily back in his mother's work

- **Own goal:** preserve short holiday/family connection while staying reliable to legitimate remote Japan-facing work; get shop errands done where possible without falsely promising unlimited time.
- **Operations:** specify “I can do X before the scheduled call but NOT drive the whole route”; follow through on one physical action; ask a practical clarification because he is less expert in making paper objects than mother; occasionally let a minor pointless argument go.
- **Tempo:** can be lazy/light at home, but becomes specific/structured when facing a true software handover or time conflict. Do NOT give him generic slick comebacks every time mother scolds.
- **By audience:** con/mẹ and candid disagreement; concise work talk with colleagues; adult respect with a customer he doesn't know; caution if first confronted by hostile Toàn (never treat as old rival already met).
- **Pressure shift:** if the shop's good faith is doubted he values exact original records, yet cannot claim police-only phone evidence or spectral diagnosis.
- **Unique test:** Does this line preserve the difference between a competent engineer, a not-always-dutiful son, and a man on finite holiday? If only “respectful obedient boy”, rewrite the BEHAVIOR, not just pronouns.

### 3) Lan Anh — attentive to costs of speech, with her own irreversible decisions

- **Goal:** protect immediate practical funeral obligations, navigate Toàn/Cổn pressure, decide when protecting Toàn means hiding something versus refusing an unwitnessed statement.
- **Operations:** she may anticipate criticism, buy time by doing a needed task, ask Toàn specifically what he is accusing her of, decline to repeat a name in public under family pressure, or insist on a factual boundary where it matters. She is capable of flawed concealment and later costly refusal, not automatically a perfectly innocent truth-teller.
- **Tempo:** public market denial can be abrupt because the radio genuinely destabilizes her; private marital speech may be slow, measured or briefly sharp when her husband overreaches. Generic “em sợ lắm” cannot substitute for specific thought.
- **By audience:** with bà Lan old acquaintance and shop familiarity; with Khanh old school familiarity plus adult distance; with Toàn actual marriage history; with Cổn respectful but not a blank obedient daughter-in-law; with Long partial knowledge and precise unwitnessed-boundaries.
- **Unique test:** If she had no agency in the exchange but only conveyed Thoa's name to trigger Toàn's reaction, write her own urgent funeral or marital objective back in.

### 4) Vũ Văn Toàn — coercive when threatened, but not a one-note shouter

- **Goal:** financial survival/deed leverage, hide the degree of his 1996 knowledge and the fatal-night scuffle, hold his wife close without confronting his own jealousy toward a man he has never met.
- **Operations:** focus on exactly who saw which record, ask who spoke to whom, test whether a story changed, close or narrow an uncomfortable topic, bargain to redirect a conversation, feel momentary tenderness or panic without treating every situation as an interrogation.
- **Tempo:** ordinary with trusted people until his personal secret/jealousy is touched; shifts from specific questions to control rather than automatic shouting.
- **By audience:** with Lan Anh has history, fear of her independent information and actual tenderness; with Cường earlier debt/deed leverage and real filial feeling; when FIRST meeting Khanh, irritation must emerge from new face-to-face behavior, not a shared backstory.
- **Unique test:** Never let Toàn voice a *living Thoa* belief in honest interior — he knows she died, though not full specifics. His insinuation about wife/Khanh must be *an inference of Toàn*, not evidence of their affair.

### 5) Vũ Cổn — authority in procedures and narrative, occasional practical care

- **Goal:** maintain control over family decisions, elder status and history, while genuinely having the skills to organize funeral necessities and sometimes caring for Thủy in bounded ways.
- **Operations:** issue an assignment as though prior consent is settled; reframe someone's contributed money as duty; quote family history selectively; use an apparently kind intervention to make later obedience harder to refuse. He can be brief when power is uncontested and verbose when challenged, never speeches just to deliver exposition.
- **By audience:** with Thủy intimacy and entitlement make “mày” a possible vector of ownership; with paid funeral helpers he may be practical/polite because he needs them; with Toàn can invoke inheritance but exact legal status must not be invented.
- **Pressure shift:** after G6a identification he strategically treats Thủy's **true discovery after Thoa died** as unreliable by citing her separately genuine **2026 staged threats**, without saying the bones were never identified.
- **Unique test:** Distinguish his “mày” from bà Lan's: what freedom would each adult child lose if they refuse this speaker? Cổn’s authority has serious financial/social consequences.

### 6) Vũ Thị Thủy — precise about labor/records because vagueness has cost her years

- **Goal:** make her actual decades of labor and current household finances visible without surrendering to the family debt myth; maintain legitimate obligations at her real fictional Yên Phong garment job and protect her voice about what she witnessed in 1996.
- **Operations:** distinguish old wedding loan from later different payments; ask to see ONE disputed CURRENT account page; choose to document what she can while paying for doing it despite Cường's objection; limit what she will testify to. She carries genuine culpability for recent fake threats but REAL overlapping factory alibi at ~22:54.
- **Tempo:** can hold long-contained anger, negotiate by concrete examples, or end a circular argument by choosing what labor she will no longer provide. She need not always cry, whisper or yell.
- **By audience:** with Cổn fluent in old patterns and potential restraint; with Phượng coworker the precision is production wages/output not metaphysical family history; with Long she must mark **discovery of dead sister**, not claim to have seen 1996 blow.
- **Unique test:** If her only function is to receive Cổn's scolding, give her one independent choice that changes next week's work, finances or care.

### 7) Đặng Việt Long — professional questions with human stakes and limits

- **Goal:** distinguish a living investigative lead from testimony/assumption, document where each piece of information comes from, preserve process and not ruin a person's work through unsupported speculation.
- **Operations:** restate a witness's specific assertion and ask its source; check dates/order identifiers; pursue independent corroboration; concede when shopkeeper genuinely doesn't know. May disagree with peers or quietly protect time to do ordinary police paperwork.
- **Tempo:** context-dependent: efficient with colleague, patient with a busy older shopkeeper, targeted when two accounts contradict; not universally deadpan, professorial or sarcastic.
- **By audience:** professional with bà Lan, non-investigative with family, controlled with Toàn, appropriately limited with Khanh (no seized-phone data or police database access).
- **Unique test:** If he already knows the full answer because of a clever rhetorical question, his point of view exceeded the evidence firewall.

### 8) A younger one-scene shop customer — independent agency even without a long character file

- **Goal:** obtain a suitable product by a deadline *she actually cares about*, satisfy requirements she may not fully control, avoid paying twice or receiving the wrong goods.
- **Operations:** bring an image, ask about variations and realistic lead time, call a relative for permission if that's genuinely her situation, decide not to purchase today if schedule/price doesn't work, give a longer answer than “mười giờ” when explaining a hard deadline.
- **Register:** age and relationship to bà Lan matter. A woman much younger might naturally say cô/cháu and elaborate on the practical reason for urgency; do not force all customers into either “chị/em” or quaint peasant patter.
- **Unique test:** Remove bà Lan's humorous reply — does the customer's decision still make sense and have an outcome? If not, the character was merely a joke setup.

## D. How to design a dialogue exchange before writing prose

For each **significant** exchange, keep a private editor-only trace (do NOT print this in the novel):
1. **Ordinary task** already in progress; if the characters never discussed the hidden case, would the scene still exist?
2. **Participant aims:** each wants an observable different outcome; not everyone wants to explain a clue.
3. **Different information:** each knows precisely what they personally saw/was told, when. Guard D-015 shop identity and Toàn insider knowledge.
4. **Leverage, costs and interruption:** if one person refuses, what happens to shipping, wages, mourning work, cohabitation, a marriage, investigation or family accounts? Who can leave?
5. **Register transitions:** specify why a person says mày/con/cháu/chị/anh at THIS moment; optional zero or one shift, never check-box quota.
6. **Termination by changed world state:** a deadline is accepted/refused, a parcel changes custodian, one person decides to stop a false statement, an accounts page is photographed or an elder loses expected labor — not “somebody wins the joke.”
7. **Clone test:** mask names and roles and read the transcript. If bà Lan, Cổn, Long and Toàn all do identical dry teasing after every line, give each a new independently motivated speech act or let an action replace speech.

**Baseline:** write a good Vietnamese novel, not a glossary or theatre exercise. Avoid scripted overlap, repeated filler and self-conscious dialect seasoning. Sentence length varies with actual moment; emotional contradiction is permitted if the scene earns it.
