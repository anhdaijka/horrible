# Case Zero — Next design gate V1: Cổn's 2026 choice and an independent late Cường-haunting climax

**Candidate for author review after D-013; NOT D-014 canon or approved prose.** All approved historical causes, effective pond-outlet binding, G6a→G6b→G6c reader-knowledge partition, independent early/late Cường haunting and the five-movement volume remain unchanged. Physical test derives from [house-space preflight](case-zero-house-space-and-setpieces-v1.md) and [Cổn character trials](case-zero-con-character-tests-v1.md). The S01 same-scene A/B packet is not affected.

## 1. Cổn needs one avoidable fresh wrong in 2026, not another undated old crime

**Keep two independent ordinary scenes before the fault:** (a) During funeral preparations after legitimate site/body handling, Cổn really knows how to organize visiting relatives, a borrowed cooking vessel and spaces currently cleared for use; he takes too much credit for the actual labor of Thủy/Lan Anh. (b) He remembers a concrete preference of Thủy's and expresses genuine personal care in a private moment, then assumes she can cancel part of her own work to keep caring for him. These are contradictory truths, not absolution or villain-signaling clichés.

**C-2026-1, specific candidate AFTER credible G6a identification but before full G6b reconstruction:** Thủy has been forced to acknowledge her genuine 2026 threats and fake ghost tactics; her **real factory alibi at ~22:54** is independently supported. At a **small family meeting** (not a spectacular village trial), she states only what she actually saw as a 19-year-old: she found her sister Thoa already dead by the pond in the rainy night of 1996, and begged Cổn/Cường to provide a proper funeral. She does **NOT** assert she witnessed Cường hit Thoa or saw the exact later burial. Cổn can confirm the funeral plea and his own order to conceal; he deliberately substitutes a recent factual wrong for a blanket dismissal: Thủy fabricated scares this year, therefore her older firsthand account cannot be trusted. He cannot deny G6a's identified bones, so the lie targets **who saw what and who silenced whom**.

**Agency and accountable price:** Toàn already knows part of the dead-Thoa history and sees a second family obligation to silence being manufactured before his eyes. Lan Anh recognizes the same mechanism Toàn has used to seek her agreement about a scuffle she never saw; she need not become a 1996 murder witness to grasp this. Thủy refuses nonessential unpaid family work while arranging ordinary alternative support where the elder cannot be left without care. Cổn must now ask/purchase help instead of simply issuing orders; initial practical costs and lost intimacy outlast any ghost incident. His later fear of Cường cannot automatically buy absolution. G6b still requires independent material/human corroboration, not merely a louder statement from Thủy.

**Primary vulnerability:** If the audience has seen no actual family competence or warmth, Cổn becomes a cartoon patriarch. If Thủy's fake scares are not substantiated before the meeting, his manipulation lacks social force. If the scene reads like a court with dozens of relatives instead of household control, shrink it to people personally affected.

## 2. Late daylight return: ordinary work forces them together, not a haunted-house dare

**Premise already approved at volume level:** After G6a and substantial G6b, and AFTER a short G6c confirming Thoa's ghost to READERS, Lan Anh has temporarily separated and plans in daylight to collect her necessary work clothes and private possessions. She asks Thủy to accompany her by choice. Toàn is told in advance because shared household items must be sorted, and he is asked to be there for actual heavy boxes, not coincidentally teleported into a rescue. Cổn still lives at the house and has a practical reason to oversee what he considers household property; his recent mistreatment of Thủy affects every small interaction. The room remains ordinary domestic storage, not the previously sealed death scene and not an invented 1996 evidence cache.

**Spatial preconditions before this day:** The house already has a real stair/landing where Toàn and Cường scuffled on night13. Do NOT place a retired staircase ghost scare there. A separate ground-floor narrow side passage leads from the courtyard past the kitchen to a rear household storeroom whose **door opens outward onto that passage**. Kitchen opens via a normal side exit to an ordinary work strip; storeroom has an earlier-established shuttered ventilation/loading window large enough to pass a person once internal shelves are shifted. The window and kitchen exit MUST have been seen used in mundane work earlier (S05 and later chores). No hidden door, miraculous removable grille or new corridor in climax.

## 3. Late haunting beat test: Cường controls an ordinary route, then the living stop obeying

**Beat 0 — settled normal work:** Lan Anh identifies her actual items rather than seizing a dramatic deed. Thủy uses her right hand to loosen an ordinary shutter or move the known shelf because her left wrist has its established limitation; Toàn starts sorting a heavy carton with Lan Anh. Cổn insists one box belongs to the family and tries to delay them. Their awkward conversation is allowed to remain social and unresolved for several minutes.

**Beat 1 — distinct bodily manifestation:** In the narrow passage, Cường's recognizably adult form **appears bodily**, not as a familiar voice or repeating stair shadow. He is recognizable through an ordinary gesture of taking possession of the path where everyone has historically brought work to him, but his damaged neck makes the gesture physically impossible to mistake for the living father. Witness access differs: Cổn near the front sees the profile/gesture of his own dead son; Toàn closer sees more of the neck; inside the storeroom, Lan Anh first experiences a real obstruction and only partly glimpses him. Do not claim simultaneous full-body omnipresence at front and rear.

**Beat 2 — local cost, not infinitely flexible ghost physics:** He stays near the outward-opening storeroom door and obstructs the ordinary exit. A known, top-heavy storage unit in the narrow room starts to tilt under frantic efforts to free the door; it can actually injure someone if they pull the wrong side. Thủy and Lan Anh are both inside and must make different choices, not simply cry to Toàn. If the same storage unit was used for early HA injury, **replace this late object** with another previously introduced ordinary stack to avoid recycling exactly the same scare.

**Beat 3 — human counterdecision:** Toàn could flee into the courtyard to avoid seeing his father again. Instead, he uses the **already-known kitchen side exit** to reach the work strip and exterior window. This is courage and practical familiarity, not proof he is redeemed. Lan Anh chooses what to abandon and which inner shelving to move; Thủy works the known latch using her functioning right hand and coordinates safe clearance, without supernatural powers. Cổn attempts to command the embodied Cường to let them pass and realizes neither his son nor the terrified living family will obey him; he must yield room to the rescue rather than fix the scene with a ritual.

**Beat 4 — one bounded physical price, under test:** Toàn braces a familiar heavy shutter/partly displaced stored item to stop it falling inward while Lan Anh exits with Thủy's help. He strains/injures his shoulder badly enough to interrupt driving work and require competent medical evaluation, but **no predetermined irreversible injury** is imposed. This converts his real truck income/debt and future household support into continuing costs. Alternatively, if an injured-rescuer beat feels too convenient, put the lasting cost in destroyed necessary work equipment/temporary relocation instead; do **not** injure Thủy's working right hand merely to escalate the punishment of an already exploited person.

**Beat 5 — observable withdrawal without magic law:** After every living person gets through ordinary exits and refuses to re-enter on Cường's terms, at least two finite witnesses observe his embodiment **withdraw to a known interior part of the house** and cease active pursuit. This is actual scene-level withdrawal but not courtroom proof of eternal exorcism, a universal rule triggered by telling truth, Thoa coming to referee or instant family reconciliation. The living make practical choices about lodging, care for the elder, treatment and who pays; this is where the emotional book closes.

## 4. Two separate payoffs that must not contaminate one another

**Cổn's human accountability:** He survives having seen his dead son and cannot reclaim automatic custody of everyone's version of events. His manipulative 2026 attempt to discredit Thủy remains a concrete act that he must answer for; an apparition is NOT an enforceable apology. He might still reject a detail in G6b or cling to clan pride while losing practical control.

**The ghost's autonomy:** Cường's household haunting starts early while the truth of Thoa is still obscure, causes independent bodily/social costs that Thủy's fake campaign cannot reproduce and culminates **after** G6c. The binding object that once restrained Thoa **does not control Cường**, who is another ghost with his own domestic relationships and source of terror.

## 5. Five pass/fail checks before this branch can be scene-canon

1. **Geography:** one fixed ground-floor plan supports door opening direction, normal kitchen side exit, adult-passable window, shelf placement and each person's path. Preserve the night13 staircase in an *entirely different* part of the geography.
2. **Visibility:** no authorial camera assigns every character the same ghost information; use physical positions and distinct fear.
3. **Continuity of harm:** if HA/HB already hurt a helper or Lan Anh, their treatment/recovery must be reflected in this daylight return rather than forgotten for convenient rescue.
4. **Motive:** Cổn's wrongdoing has social force only after his credible everyday competence and tenderness, and a sourced acknowledgment of Thủy's staged scares; none of this makes him a secret all-knowing monster.
5. **Finite ending:** rescue succeeds by ordinary skills and choices but costs time, income/care/housing; ghost withdrawal is observed, not guaranteed permanent, and no new death is introduced.
