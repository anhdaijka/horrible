## 3. Physical geography: late rescue cannot depend on an invented exit

**Candidate diagram, illustrative and NOT architectural canon:**

```text
                             |

```

Actual building might be a one-story extended rural house or another layout that fits established xóm Trại. Do not silently revive the old rejected staircase as a supernatural corridor. Show the side corridor, an awkward threshold and the store's ordinary access **well before** the final rescue through a mundane chore. Choose whether the rear room has only one actual usable doorway; if it has a vent/window, it must genuinely permit or *not* permit rescue according to a preflight physical sketch, not change size mid-action. Place ordinary rescue tools in a realistic place, e.g. Toàn keeps basic vehicle tools on his truck/house property if actually plausible, not within miraculous reach during a ghost encounter.

**Spatial stress tests:**
- How does Lan Anh need the work items in this room for a genuine life decision? Are they her own or family-managed goods she must negotiate for, rather than a magic ledger meant to expose 1996?
- Why do Toàn, Thủy and Cổn have independent motivations for being present **at a prearranged daylight time**, even though they already know the house is unsafe? What have they each already tried to solve the practical problem without returning?
- Who sees the entrance to the rear room from the courtyard or front room? Can a person inside hear the kitchen? Does an apparent "wall between people" have a credible ordinary mechanism before a ghost escalates it?
- If a panel/door is damaged to save someone, exactly what forces/tools/human strength are plausible? A realistic rescue maneuver should be staged with *ordinary* tools and a limited physical solution; no unexplained iron lock suddenly unlocks because Thoa is named.
- Who is injured/why is this care burden consequential in the following days? Does Cổn need alternate living arrangements without narrator declaring his ghost-proof exile?

**Link to authorship:** Treat the above as *constraints* that prevent episodic scares or improbable rescue, not an instruction to put a house tour or engineering diagram in the novel. Readers should absorb spatial relationships while ordinary characters cook, move goods and argue about storage. A late ghost occupying the narrow path can then terrify because the reader already knows ordinary routes.

## 4. Cổn's three developmental relationship proofs before deciding the new wrong

- **Competence:** in a costly practical funeral arrangement, Cổn recalls a real relationship or does something genuinely useful for others. Show why people once relied on him.
- **Affection and leverage:** alone with Thủy he remembers a small detail, gives a concrete kindness, then frames her time/service as his prerogative. Her reaction has a personal stake and can be mixed rather than a lecture.
- **Face versus fidelity:** when a third party recognizes Thủy's economic contribution, Cổn appropriates praise into the family-name narrative, without making every utterance a shouted threat.

**Candidate modern culpable choice (not canon):** after G6a, he uses Thủy's admitted fake scares to discredit her **truthful eyewitness discovery and coerced silence**, because recognizing that one limited truth forces him to acknowledge his own past orders. This mirrors his 1996 mechanism of coercion and costs him her cooperation. It is a concrete possible event for author approval, not a mandated additional assault, new crime or guaranteed paranormal cause. Late ghost Cường acts independently of the choice; his visible withdrawal cannot be a magical confession reward.
