This map is an **editable preflight/architecture proposal**, NOT fixed chapter numbering, approved precise new acts, an approved paranormal manifestation list, or prose. ~39 **scene movements** below are dependency nodes: combine, expand or resequence by causal integrity; do not mechanically write one chapter per node. Character viewpoint is never a reason to suppress a fact its focal person is currently actively using.

**S07 — Long has a real lead (later mùng14/15).** Long's limited professional POV; independent market gossip with several versions, then legal/shop-appropriate questions to bà Lan/Khanh about the mùng10 order.
