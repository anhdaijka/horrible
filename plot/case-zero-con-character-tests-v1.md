## Trial 1 — Why the household still lets him direct things (S05, after official reporting)

**Unrelated practical situation:** people arrive to help with funeral cooking and accommodation while parts of the house/scene have access limits. Someone proposes using an area not yet appropriate for use; Cổn refuses that *correctly* and rearranges tasks so visitors can be fed elsewhere. He actually knows which neighbor has a spare large cooking pot and who is currently too ill/busy to provide labor. His competence is earned through ordinary lifetime village social networks rather than total control over police.

**Counterpressure:** Thủy and Lan Anh had already prepared supplies and sorted shifts under hard physical conditions; to people outside, Cổn explains that "nhà tôi đã bố trí đâu vào đấy" without giving either credit. A neighbor who personally saw their work can make a small factual correction rather than a theatrical denunciation. Neither women needs to confront him immediately; one may have no energy left and the other may need his help with something else.

**Character truth:** he materially helps the household and appropriates the structure of care as his own achievement. He is not merely a cartoon autocrat hitting the floor with a cane. This episode should move family labor and relationships forward even if no ghost appears.

## Trial 2 — The intimacy that makes Thủy's choice painful (S20/S25)

**Private scene without the investigative cast:** after a difficult Yên Phong night shift or actual funeral work, Thủy visits Cổn to deliver a practical item and check whether he has eaten. He recognizes a concrete sensory preference from her youth, such as how she disliked food gone cold while caring for someone else, and prepares/remembers that detail sincerely. There is space for an old family memory involving Thoa **only after G6a if it directly confirms Thoa dead**, otherwise keep ordinary older-sibling warmth referenced externally.

**Pressure turn:** he then assumes she will rearrange *another* work shift to remain with him or handle tomorrow's house chores, even though he can see she is tired. He means his kindness and his demand, and may sincerely view the two as consistent. Thủy wants him to ask rather than assign; she can accept one small task, refuse another or leave without giving a sermon.

**Character truth:** her long compliance has a reciprocal emotional tether; if his demands were pure hate, her breakaway would be too easy. His occasional tenderness is not atonement for silencing her about Thoa. Do not make her impaired left wrist shorthand for helplessness; she has ordinary work skills and independent friendships.

## Trial 3 — A choice to preserve narrative control at a concrete price (C-2026-1 candidate AFTER G6a)

**Preconditions:** credible independent identification establishes Thoa dead (G6a); Thủy has admitted and is accountable for **2026** threats/fake hauntings, without losing her genuine death-window factory alibi. G6b is still under reconstruction, especially who knew what and what was done to Thoa after the head injury. Cổn can no longer credibly insist Thoa simply eloped; that part of the old myth has physically collapsed.

**One narrow and accountable choice:** at a small family meeting Thủy offers only her actual eyewitness account: she found sister's already-dead body that rainy night and begged father and brother to give Thoa a funeral. Cổn **could** corroborate that specific observed event while contesting any claim she didn't witness. Instead, he treats her known 2026 staged haunting as blanket proof she is unreliable about what happened to Thoa thirty years ago, strategically declining to confirm his own orders of concealment.

**Human consequences:** Toàn's belief in the tale that everything was done for him cracks further when Cổn can acknowledge identified remains but still use Thủy's misdeeds to suppress her distinct truthful testimony. Lan Anh can hear this exchange without magically knowing the 1996 blow; she recognizes the present request to validate a story she didn't see, mirroring her own marital loyalty trap without announcing the parallel. Thủy may refuse nonessential caregiving or require another relative to take responsibility; if so she must arrange ordinary replacement care where needed rather than neglect a vulnerable elder on command of the plot.

**Late spiritual distinction:** Cổn's later direct encounter with hồn Cường is terrifying because he recognizes a grown man still repeating their family's habit of treating other people as extensions of household authority. This is not a universe judging him fairly, nor a guaranteed rule that his belated confession dismisses the spirit. His possible new wrong was already an ordinary socially effective act; the ghost does not need to invent or prove it.

**Reject or change C-2026-1 if:** G6a occurred too early for him to plausibly deny anything meaningful; Thủy's fake scares have not been established credibly in family hearing; he is made a lawyerly mastermind with unrealistic knowledge of evidence; or the choice is staged as a huge village crowd confession instead of the intimate control mechanism approved in the volume.
