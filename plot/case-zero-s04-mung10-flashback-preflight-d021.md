**Status:** scene design for later manuscript prose, NOT authored S04 and NOT new magic canon. The user delegated planning/review without per-scene approvals; keep D-011–20 knowledge gates and [D-021 S01–S07 integration](case-zero-s01-s07-integration-preflight-d021.md) binding.

## Why this scene must exist

S01 shows the prepaid red Czech cassette-paper-radio as one routine rằm job without its full transaction; S03 first identifies its named recipient as Lan Anh's father-in-law, **Vũ Văn Cường / xóm Trại**, who was still living mùng10 and died mùng13. S04 now has an earned reason to focus a **limited bà Lan recollection** on the mùng10 customer and the exact paper artwork. Its purpose is the **reader's reinterpretation of a normal paid transaction**, not fresh ghost spectacle, new crime-scene evidence or a complete 1996 explanation.

## Candidate 2-time narrative frame

- **Frame mùng14, after S03:** Bà Lan returns home with the **unaccepted** boxed radio plus ORIGINAL ledger and customer drawing. Her legitimate immediate goals are finishing the afternoon seven-seat-paper-car pickup and protecting the separate prepaid order from accidental delivery or someone opening the box without a receipt. The buyer's drawing is among the papers she must keep with the unfulfilled order.
- **Memory mùng10, 2026-08-22, ordinary daytime:** A woman gives her name as **Vũ Thị Thoa**, commissions the custom red Czech cassette-paper-radio for **Vũ Văn Cường / xóm Trại**, and pays **in full**. Bà Lan works under pre-rằm shop pressure and asks the useful craft, money, date and receiving details; she does not ask for her family history or whether the recipient is living, newly dead or being remembered on an anniversary.
- **Craft-specific physical action:** Thoa uses her **LEFT hand** to adjust the hand-drawn reference, with distinctive **WHITE correction fluid** to fix one portion and point out the desired red/cream edge/mark. Bà Lan notices because of a minor practical shop interaction — she must move a ruler or let the correction fluid dry before placing the sheet beneath another order. No “ghost hand,” unusual body temperature, missing reflection, impossible sound or prophetic line. Distinguish **the fluid on the PAPER DRAWING** from the red-painted decorative stripe ON the crafted paper radio.
- **Handoff (provisional exact details):** the tested premise inherited from approved S01 says customer agreed on an ordinary **mùng14 Chợ Bến regular drop/pickup**, recipient name and a simple order code. S04 may illustrate how bà Lan wrote that usual instruction, but **do not backfill a new mobile number, full house address, eyewitness or home courier request** merely to simplify S07. If whole-volume outline later replaces this seam, separately version any revision to D-018 S01.
- **Back to mùng14:** Bà Lan now knows only from Lan Anh's earned public confirmation that this precise receiver was her father-in-law and he died mùng13. She can notice that the customer **knew the exact mark** on that old radio and chose **mùng14 delivery four days before the recipient died**, but she cannot assert “the customer knew he was going to die” as proven. More precise question: Why did a woman she does not know pay for THIS custom replica for THIS man while he was living, and why does the family apparently not expect it?
- **Physical endpoint:** Keep ORIGINAL ledger, the hand-drawn fluid-corrected sheet and the uncollected radio safe, without prematurely framing them as police evidence. Bà Lan may take an ordinary practical next step (e.g. note “chưa giao” or keep the packet aside), but the exact action should be justified by her shop habit, not investigator instinct.

5. Do NOT make a dated night time, impossible parcel route or promise that the buyer will personally return unless a natural earlier mùng10 exchange supports it.
6. Do NOT introduce a new fake CCTV “forensic” recording, fingerprints or independently authenticated signature without its own earned plot and legal preflight. Visible fluid correction is a craft detail and optional clue, not a solved identity match.
7. Thoa objectively acts after mùng8 release; the memory chapter depicts the outside face of that act. **Only G6c** shows the reader what she was and that she actually killed Cường/removed visible nail scrap evidence.

## Independent literary reader lens once S04 is drafted

The paid order must have a busy shop scene's own small problem (paper stock, a queue, correcting design, time estimate) so the ghost plot does not make every exchange portentous. The woman need not speak in half-sentences or riddles; bà Lan must have business reasons to retain the drawing and remember the left hand. Let the changed emotional meaning arise from **the mùng14 fact the reader now has**, not from a narrator explaining that the earlier order is eerie. Avoid heavy flashback headers that feel like detective reconstructions unless time separation is otherwise ambiguous.
