# Case Zero — House geography and physically staged horror setpieces V1

**2026-09-26; D-011 author-approved volume, physical floor plan and exact ghost actions still CANDIDATES.** Use this before locking HA–HD or writing any household horror. It tests whether S06, S12, S32 and S36 can actually happen without arbitrary new doors, impossible witness sight lines or a ghost that suddenly becomes omniscient. Do NOT use prior discarded calling-voice, staircase or test-manifestation staging.

## 1. Ordinary house, ordinary reasons to stay

**Working layout only:** Cường's house occupies a compact village lot; a front gate opens to a paved courtyard and common/front room. **Preserve an actual stair/landing at the edge of the existing front/central household space because the canon Toàn–Cường fight happens at the foot of stairs and Cường's phone falls near the landing.** The proposed HA/HB/HC/HD haunting happens elsewhere on the mapped ground-floor route; do not reuse the retired staircase calling scare. The house is not automatically single-storey even when its rescue uses ordinary ground-level access. Along one side is a narrow **straight** passage from courtyard to an ordinary kitchen and then to a **rear storage/work room**. The kitchen has an existing side exit to a small yard/back work strip; rear storage has a normal large shuttered ventilation window overlooking that same strip, partly obstructed by shelves and packed family household goods. The rear storage entrance opens **outwards onto the narrow side passage**, so a crowd and an unsafe cabinet can obstruct the route. The window has an ordinary removable wooden latch/shutter, **NOT a concealed secret tunnel or welded security bars that become magically removable later**. Actual dimensions and whether each door opens in/out must be frozen as one consistent floor plan with real walking and load-bearing limits.

Before a scare the prose must let readers use this place ordinarily: bà Lan might only know front courtyard during delivery, not the rear interior; Lan Anh routinely carries food from kitchen to front room, Toàn knows where work clothes were stored, Cổn insists on sitting in front to direct family errands, Thủy arrives when called to help and knows the side passage from years of labor. The storeroom contains normal cloth/work items, ordinary household papers and temporary funeral-related storage; it **does not conveniently reveal a new incriminating deed** just because the final setpiece happens there.

**Legal access timing:** no one rearranges the actual cordoned death scene, moves body or enters secured rooms before authorities permit. Family hospitality/food preparations can happen in accessible areas while formal work continues elsewhere; use specific released-space status when horror is staged.

## 2. Candidate HA — first violation as an extension of work

- **Place:** ordinary room being cleared for visiting relatives, not the death landing unless released. Two neighbors and a household person move a known awkward piece of furniture. Readers have already seen its cracked leg or unreliable hinge for a fair natural hypothesis.
- **Lines of sight:** one helper at narrow gap, another lifting from front, one household onlooker at threshold who never directly sees the pinning surface.
- **Source-consistent supernatural possibility:** the helper first feels a pressure like a hand or grip persisting after the other movers have released that side. The force shifts against the direction of their corrective lift; do not narrate an objective universal new ghost-force rule or show an impossible cinematic camera angle. If the invisible pressure is too common, rebuild around a more personal bodily gesture genuinely associated with Cường's treatment of laborers.
- **Immediate human cost:** forearm injury from real material contact, family must rearrange hospitality and repay/compensate injured helper. Cổn first blames carelessness and suppresses talk of ma; Lan Anh and/or another witness retains a finite contradictory perception.
- **Carry-forward:** reduced help and physical instability of that room change subsequent funeral nights. Avoid repeating the furniture as final H4 hazard.

## 3. Candidate HB — duty of care as trap, separate sensory channel

- **Place:** familiar kitchen-to-courtyard route, safely outside unreleased scene. A prior ordinary serving movement shows who usually takes a heavy vessel from whom, with a turn where line of sight is briefly blocked by a real corner/shelf.
- **Action:** Lan Anh, already burdened by care and shirt concealment, hands over a hot vessel because she feels the **specific familiar way Cường habitually seized a handle or directed a task**; the intended living receiver physically has not turned and never touched it. She can choose to hold longer and still be hurt when the vessel slips; no narrator converts one tactile experience into police proof of a spirit.
- **Witnesses:** living receiver sees her release too early from one angle, someone behind sees an ordinary stumble, Lan Anh felt the opposite. No one sees an impossible complete ghost apparition yet.
- **Cost:** serious but bounded scald/hand damage with appropriate care, no automatic disfiguring injury; relatives reorganize cooking and caregiving while doubting testimony. Revealing Thủy's old false scares later cannot account for *this particular* close bodily moment. If scalding reads like a too-mechanical scare, replace HB with a non-kitchen caring task without duplicating HA physical compression.

## 4. Candidate HC — direct Cổn/Cường recognition without instant contrition

- **Place:** Cổn's ordinary position in a front/side room where he sorts a household container, requires nobody to reveal a buried item. Spatially stable, no magic stairs or replayed calling voice.
- **Prior human substrate:** at least three unrelated mundane situations show him competent, privately relying on Thủy and publicly absorbing credit from her work. The private scene can carry genuine familial tenderness; manipulation and affection remain simultaneous.
- **Direct encounter:** from Cổn's finite line of sight, Cường makes a familiar domestic demand silently through gesture and an ordinary physical task, but his body/neck cannot move correctly after death. The impossible biological detail develops **while Cổn stays present** rather than a one-frame jump. Make Cường recognizable through habits/memory, not a scripted speech about 1996.
- **Afterward:** Cổn's confidence in dismissal collapses privately, yet public face remains; later fear may lead to a bounded attempt to organize a rite but cannot erase haunting or become a universal spirit rule.

## 5. Candidate HD — autonomous late crisis, real exit and rescue geometry

**Predetermined lived reason:** after G6a/G6b and brief reader G6c, Lan Anh plans a *daylight, prearranged visit* to retrieve her necessary belongings while separated; Thủy voluntarily accompanies, Toàn comes because marriage/home matter, Cổn still lives there. Nobody is magically summoned by the dead. The storeroom normally holds Lan Anh's seasonal work clothes and items she has managed for the family, so she needs to sort ownership rather than snatch a single plot-relevant record.

**Ordinary entry and route:**
- Lan Anh and Thủy use front courtyard and side passage to rear storage. Toàn can access both courtyard and side strip via kitchen's standard exit; Cổn remains near the front room but can come as far as entrance to side passage if alarmed.
- Rear storage's standard side-yard ventilation window is visibly used earlier in S05 for drying/airing goods; old shelves normally block full opening. It is not a sudden second exit invented under threat.
- Within storage, Lan Anh physically shifts her own boxes/clothes, can climb/clear the interior opening and can shout limited instructions to people in side strip. If there is a fall or injury, identify one real material surface and where it traps her.

**Hazard and apparition (one trial mechanism, NOT canon):** a *fully embodied* Cường appears in the narrow passage between the only ordinary room door and courtyard, physically prevents a quick retreat and presses the outward-opening storage door toward a pile of realistic heavy stored goods. His damaged neck is visibly wrong and his ordinary living behavior—taking ownership of the passage as if everyone's access is his—provides character-specific fear. A helper at the courtyard sees a partial profile, Toàn closer in passage sees more, Cổn may see only a familiar hand/body line from his vantage, Lan Anh inside perceives a blocked door and material threat first. Ghost cannot inexplicably occupy every entrance at once: the rescue route is **ordinary side yard via kitchen exit and previously introduced large window**, cleared by Toàn and Thủy while Lan Anh independently moves shelves and finds a place to climb. Avoid a physically absurd 50-cm grille; require a plausible adult-size opening that residents have long used for airing bulky household objects.

**Human pressure:** Toàn must decide whether to enter near the father he dreads to help Lan Anh. Thủy cannot lift with a miraculously restored left hand; she can communicate, remove a wooden latch with her good right hand, brace a board and coordinate Toàn while participating in an ordinary physical rescue. Cổn can face the son he helped teach controlling behavior and must cope with rescue happening **without his commands**, but his new deliberate 2026 wrong remains separately author-gated. Lan Anh acts to free herself, not a static sacrificial victim. No forced forgiveness or hidden Thoa supernatural intervention.

**Finite ending:** after a physically successful rescue and a sustained interval of presence, Cường's embodied form is seen withdrawing into his familiar part of the house and stops actively pursuing people. This cannot certify permanent exorcism, nor should the prose claim that speaking truth mechanically disperses all spirits. One bounded real nonfatal injury or damage to stored work clothes/room makes the house unsafe for a while; someone must fund lodging/care for surviving Cổn.

**Potential design failure:** passage ghost plus window rescue may become a videogame obstacle course unless normal house/household ownership and character histories remain dominant. If it reads like a mechanically solvable boss fight, replace the specific apparatus; **retain only approved autonomy, late timing, multi-person rescue and serious consequence.** The supernatural should resist explanation without arbitrarily changing house geography.

## 7. Preflight before prose

Lock a ground-floor diagram and scene duration in real time. For each hazard write *actual ordinary task before intrusion*, who stands where, what they handle, what can break without magic, what **only one character** can see or feel, what the others legitimately dispute, what local care/repair follows, and how a prior scene already introduced the physical route/tool used for resolution. If any answer needs a new corridor, new ghost omniscience, magically timed power cut, sudden medical certainty or a new death to raise stakes, rebuild scene instead of forcing it.
