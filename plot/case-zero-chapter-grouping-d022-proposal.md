## Specific POV/time transfer warnings

1. **S02 Hảo → S03 bà Lan** must have a visible prose break: market voice already formed *before* bà Lan visits Hảo's receiving stall and publicly offers radio. The S03 reader still hears the market's residue without a narrator explaining gossip theory.
2. **S04 mùng10 flashback** is told from bà Lan's present mùng14 purpose and only after S03's verified recipient ID; don't misdate a living Cường order as a postmortem parcel.
3. **S13 mùng6 retrospective** can appear in movement II after scenes taking place mùng14 onward because its human source Bình recounts bounded past through an actually earned time shift. Give specific date/ordinary paid-job cue; do not write the whole murder night as all-knowing montage.
4. **S14 mùng13 afternoon** external witness then **S17 full mùng13 shift** from Phượng later are different observations and disclose distinct facts; no pre-G6a Thủy guilty inner POV.
5. **S26 Thủy allegation** is explicit testimony to Long before remains recovered, NOT author-certainty or a redacted memory chapter. **S29 full Thủy personal flashback** occurs after G6a.
6. **S28 G6a, S30 G6b, S34 G6c** have separation sufficient for family, procedural work and human choice. G6c doesn't let Long or courts certify a spectral killing.
7. **S35–S36 physical geography** is identical to S05/S06 ordinary-use seeds and [D-022 house blocking](case-zero-house-action-blocking-d022.md). Toàn cannot be simultaneously at blocked storage door and outside its window; he reaches exterior via known kitchen side exit.

## Do not infer novel length from this chart

A short S04 mùng10 memory can be nested with household S05 if a strong POV break preserves its retrospective function; S34's brief ghost confirmation can share Ch19 with ordinary daytime return precisely because the dissonance is narrative material. Chapter numbers and titles are **workshop labels only**, not a new author verdict about optimal volume word count. Scenes may be split or grouped if prose rhythm requires it, provided all source-knowledge/firewall and material handoff constraints are preserved.
