## Cường's three consistent traits as a spectral *source*, not supernatural rules

1. **Familiar territory:** newly dead Cường's haunt is tied to household/family/social routines he occupied. He does not know every external witness, read police phones, or spontaneously target Yên Phong across the province.
2. **Continuity of living character:** he genuinely loved Toàn and demanded dependence, warmth and obedience in the same relationship. The haunting may seem like returning to his familiar ways of allocating tasks, managing rooms or refusing an exit; **this is interpretive dramatic continuity, not an occult law**.
3. **Epistemic separation:** he was actually killed by ghost Thoa (reader G6c late); haunting by dead Cường after his death is **another supernatural plot** with distinct danger and terminal crisis. Real human hoaxes can be exposed without explaining him away. A religious specialist's beliefs belong to characters, not certified metaphysics.

### Escalation is qualitative, not a quota

- **First occupation:** ordinary labor blocked in an impossible and costly way; the family still has plausible mundane partial explanations.
- **Invasion of care:** someone personally trusts a familiar routine and becomes physically unsafe; disagreements over what was witnessed turn into compromised household cooperation.
- **Loss of control by patriarch:** after sustained prior denial and household care dynamics, Cổn must endure direct presence in a social arrangement his command cannot change; his **subsequent human choice remains author-gated**.
- **Post-truth terminal confrontation:** after G6a, substantial G6b and brief G6c, the household must do an *ordinary* task despite well-founded fear. More than one character needs to act under extreme bodily danger; a single ritual or admission cannot reset the room.
- **Residue:** physical damage/injury, who now lives elsewhere, obligations to care for Cổn, the family's relation to household belongings and long-term mistrust survive the withdrawal.

## Environment draft, not yet canon house plan

A fictional north-Vietnamese house needs reproducible spatial coherence before any action scene. **Working geometry only:** front courtyard enters common/front room used for receiving mourners; a narrower side passage reaches kitchen; a rear storage room and lived sleeping space connect to side passage; a gate affords outside help. Avoid arbitrarily hidden second entrances or precise old-house architecture before a ground-floor diagram is approved. On any scene card explicitly track who can see/hear the front room versus rear storage, where hot objects and heavy loads sit and which spaces official staff have released for use.

Do NOT turn wet electrical faults, rain or "tháng Bảy" into guaranteed supernatural laws. They are natural environmental constraints characters may interpret; every unusual event needs finite witness/source access and physical consequences. Set piece site should be established in banal earlier activity before any hazard so readers can visualize it without exposition inserted at a climax.

## Candidate HA — The work that cannot stop (first tang, S05–06)

**Ordinary task:** household must free usable floor space for out-of-town relatives after authorities have released the relevant access. Two helpers shift a large, genuinely heavy household object Cường used daily (bed frame, armchair and a cupboard OR another scene-appropriate burden); one person directs, another reaches through a narrow gap to secure a leg/hinge. The object *already* has ordinary instability or excessive weight — establish it rather than physics-contrive supernatural proof.

**Proposed extraordinary layer:** the helper's sleeve/forearm becomes pinned to the furniture by a pressure that **changes direction** when rescuers counter-lift. From outside it looks like an unfortunate crush. From that helper's close experience the trapped hand is held *in exactly the kind of grip Cường used when impatiently demanding help with housework*; it remains wrong even when ordinary contact points are freed. A third relative feels a distinct second pressure in an empty place, but there is no all-seeing camera or spirit portrait in the first event.

**Bodily and mundane aftermath:** painful but nonfatal forearm injury, interrupted guest arrangements, fees/time lost seeking care, Cổn's insistence that careless movers damaged his son's property. Family must pay a favor or debt to the injured helper; a relationship changes, not only their belief in ghosts. This fact still matters a few scenes later.

**Escalation role:** invisible occupation and responsibility-shifting; Cường's controlling household presence feels specific without revealing anything about 1996. **Reject if:** helper's identical grip memory requires unnatural narrator certainty or crushed-limb moment reads like stock haunted-chair trope. Alternative: break the setpiece on the **social insult after injury** rather than making the spectral physics increasingly elaborate.

## Candidate HB — A task of care becomes a threat (after fake scare exposure, S12)

**Ordinary task:** Lan Anh and another household member serve hot food/water or assist an older relative while the family is exhausted; routes are familiar and lit. A mundane tension has already made them distrust Thủy's noises/false scares.

**Two alternative hazard designs: CHOOSE ONE, NOT BOTH:**

- **HB1: Hand-over that nobody performed.** Carrying a heavy full thermos/food vessel, Lan Anh reaches to pass it to an adult just ahead in the narrow kitchen passage, **believing a familiar hand has taken the weight**. The person ahead never turned, and a different witness saw no hand between them. Lan Anh's release leads to serious but nonfatal scalding, not merely spilled crockery. From her own sense of touch, someone with Cường's habitual way of abruptly taking over a task pressed the handle for an instant. Witness disagreement is socially credible because of confined angles and interruptions; readers still know an independent spectral source is active. This is **not** a forensic ghost fingerprint, paranormal omniscience or an absolute "ghost can touch only utensils" law.
- **HB2: The old seating habit.** During a night of reallocated sleeping arrangements, someone helping the exhausted family instinctively clears a place Cường used to command. A loved one sitting down is forcibly made to move in a physically dangerous direction while everyone else thinks a living hand did it, causing a real fall/injury and false accusation between two family members. More relationally targeted, but its optical staging is harder; reject if too generic.

**Aftermath:** a care task is reallocated, burn/fall treatment takes effort, a relative refuses night shifts or blames another. When later fake devices/messages are exposed, that knowledge **does not end the real dangerous phenomenon**, increasing mistrust.

**Key distinction from HA:** intimacy of care, mistaken trust and body vulnerability rather than physical compression of shared labor. **Reject HB1 if** readers cannot follow who should have received the vessel; stage ordinary prior handovers and clear spatial positions. No magical repeatable route-loop effect unless physically convincing and uniquely valuable.

## Candidate HC — Cổn and the son he taught to command (after initial denial; S32)

**Prior human scenes required:** (a) Cổn competently solves a mundane funeral allocation; (b) alone he remembers Thủy's small preference and relies on her for a real task; (c) in front of outsiders he calls her contribution merely duty. At least one shows him affectionate without qualifying his culpability. **Do not newly approve a fresh 2026 misdeed yet.**

**Ordinary task:** Cổn sits to sort an ordinary household container with practical content (documents or reusable funeral supplies) and recover a familiar item he knows his son kept. His manual habits matter: he lays things out by seniority/ownership and expects other people to follow instructions. Another person can place the items earlier; don't teleport historical evidence into the container.

**Proposed sight at human distance:** Cường appears not as a sudden screaming face but in a tiny habitual action Cổn has seen for decades — adjusting a chair, taking a bowl, moving a household item as if someone else should do the labor. Then his damaged neck/upper body becomes physically impossible *while the ordinary movement continues*, and Cổn must recognize it is this specific son rather than a traditional "ma" costume. There may be a physical obstruction/accident but do not make his frightened old body a medical diagnosis, a new fatal injury, or a general uncanny-mirror gimmick.

**Psychological/physical cost:** Cổn cannot trust a domestic ritual of certainty that once organized the family. He may quietly pay for social help/ritual or ask Thủy to stay with him for a specific practical reason, while publicly still controlling the family version. **This episode is not his newly approved 2026 deliberate wrong**. His later accountable decision needs a separate author gate.

**Key distinction from HA/HB:** full embodied apparition and father–son history, not generic object movement, sound, staircase or new clue. No ghost dialogue delivering backstory.

## Candidate HD — Last household confrontation *after* truth (S35–37)

**Entry test (treatment-approved rationale, detailed staging candidate):**
- Lan Anh has separated temporarily after refusing to support Toàn's unwitnessed account; she **agrees in advance to a daylight visit** to retrieve personally needed clothes/work belongings. She does not sneak back at midnight for a supernatural artifact.
- Thủy chooses to accompany her after each has risked speaking to another woman honestly. She knows her 1996 observation, not every 2026 event; she has no special medium power.
- Toàn comes because marital/property loss is now real, not because all suspects conveniently gather to confess.
- Cổn remains attached to the familiar household by power, belongings and practical need. His concrete *new* 2026 choice is still subject to author approval; do not script him burning records or physically locking the door in advance.
- **Geography to design first:** a real narrow point between rear storage and courtyard, how the inward-opening door is secured, which floor/objects can block access, where helper can stand safely, where mobile signal/ordinary outside help can reach. Not a novel law of impossible rooms.

**Candidate direct threat:** While Lan Anh sorts her work clothing and family-managed items, someone in the narrow rear work/storage area is endangered by an overt Cường apparition whose **physical presence takes up the only immediately usable route** and produces material crushing/impact danger. Not a copy of HA: here **the source is seen at human scale**, impedes a route and challenges relationships under sustained sight, rather than invisible sudden pressure on moving furniture. Toàn sees only enough of his father to know he cannot leave his wife behind; Cổn has another line of sight with recognition of his son's ordinary gesture distorted by death; Thủy may first see only obstruction/its consequences. Witness access is finite but direct enough to justify author-level overt haunting.

**Rescue not ritual:** Toàn and Thủy use practical means to move a genuinely heavy obstruction or create a normal side route; a prior mundane scene must establish the relevant storage latch/panel and ordinary tools. One actor chooses to help another despite the relational cost. Lan Anh is an agent in her own rescue when possible, not a silent distress prop. **No secret underground tunnel, magical property-transfer paper, required spoken Thoa name, universal 'tell truth → ghost departs' mechanism or Thoa arriving as an emergency referee.**

**Visible withdrawal:** after rescue the apparition visibly retreats into Cường's own familiar occupied part of the house and no longer actively pursues them in this confrontation. This **does not certify eternal banishment**, a general soul-physics rule or forgiveness; the family temporarily does not inhabit the damaged/unsafe space and must make realistic care/housing arrangements for the surviving Cổn. If a narrator's line promises Cường is permanently gone without basis, rewrite it.

**Continuing cost:** one bounded significant nonfatal injury or damage + relocation/care/debt; the marital situation remains consequential and Thủy does not get immediate clean land title or social forgiveness by heroism. Don't injure every named person or inflate a larger body count to signal climax.

**Intensity floor:** must be more sustained, bodily, relationally costly and spatially legible than HA/HB. An "empty room feels cold" after G6b is **insufficient** as a post-truth autonomous ghost climax.
