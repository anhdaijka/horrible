# Volume 1 — Những chuyện tưởng đã kết thúc

## Purpose

The opening volume establishes Khanh's ordinary home and work lives, then progressively weakens his assumption that every anomaly can be cleanly closed by finding enough data.

## Stable architecture

- opening homecoming and family/shop life;
- Case Zero as the first full mystery;
- genuine aftermath and return to ordinary life;
- a later workplace mystery in Hanoi;
- one shorter spiritual haunting that Khanh deliberately does not turn into an investigation;
- a quiet ending in which grandfather's old records reveal a recurring pattern of refusals without explaining why.

Chapter counts and exact later-case mechanics remain development navigation rather than immutable canon.

## Ending state

Khanh has not chosen a paranormal career. He has, however, begun to keep a private evidence-oriented record distinguishing what seems certain from what remains uncertain.
