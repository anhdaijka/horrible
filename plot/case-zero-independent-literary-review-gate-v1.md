# Case Zero — Independent Literary Review Gate V1 (D-020)

**Authority:** [Author decision D-020](../author/decisions.md). Effective for S03 V2 and every new Case Zero prose scene; this is a CRAFT GATE, not a new plot event.

## Why this gate exists

The author liked the *second rewritten S03* in chat for its **credible daily rhythm, natural speech and exchanges**, meeting their expectations on those dimensions, yet identified a recurring literary defect: the narration tells the audience how to read a moment even though speech/action has shown it. Specific diagnosed example (from assistant's S03 V2 review text, NOT author-endorsed prose):

> Cách Lan Anh nói khiến bà hiểu cô không từ chối vì không thích món hàng, mà vì có những điều lúc này cô chưa đủ sức đứng ra nhận thay cả gia đình.

The author said this is **telling without showing** and “agent tự giải thích thay cho khán giả.” Their acceptance **does not** approve complete S03 V2 manuscript prose and is **not** carte blanche to rewrite its successful dialogue. Another assistant-identified candidate from that same draft:

> Chợ gần rằm, những câu chuyện như thế thường đi xa hơn người trong cuộc muốn.

This pre-interprets rumor propagation for readers even though S02 already shows the rumor changing as retold. These exact quoted lines are **examples for review**, not mandatory future dialogue/canon.

## Procedure — one genuinely independent literary read AFTER each completed draft

### 1) Preserve before reviewing

Give each draft a version, route/POV and status. Preserve raw and author-approved versions unmodified. For S03: **S03 V2 in the immediately preceding writing exchange is APPROVED AS BASELINE FOR PACE/VOICE ONLY**; do not silently replace it with the *previous* S03 after-rumor review file [S03 V1](../revisions/chapter-work/CASEZERO-S02-S03-D019-V1/S03-AFTER-RUMOR-REVIEW.md), whose clipped dialogue/over-expository narration drew criticism. If V2 text is saved to GitHub later, save separately with its real provenance and do not claim a mechanically exact transcript unless checked against the chat.

### 2) Independent reader’s input: text and minimal guardrails, not the writer’s intention

Give the reviewer the whole draft, its ONE focal POV, and minimal binding continuity facts for that scene. **Do not** give them the author's thematic explanation, intended effect or earlier per-line self-audit; these can prime them to rubber-stamp. Ask them to diagnose passages as an attentive first-time reader of Northern Vietnamese supernatural family mystery with understated everyday dialogue. Later compare review findings with author-approved direction, never promote the reviewer’s subjective preferences to binding canon.

### 3) Mark only observed, falsifiable issues with short quotes and precise spans

- **Redundant explanation / telling after showing:** narration decodes a meaning already carried by dialogue, concrete behavior, selective silence or stakes. Usually delete the explanatory sentence completely if causation stays legible. The Lan Anh line quoted above is the standard test.
- **Intrusive omniscient/synthetic narrator:** broad morals about rumor, land, grief or characters’ inner states appear without credible focal-person perception; an author lecture, moral or prediction slips into third-person limited text. Prefer observable social action and a rumor’s actual changed retelling.
- **Clipped, AI-rhythmic prose:** sequences of short uniform “X said/then X did/then Y saw” sentences lose texture and scene time. Vary clause length and syntactic cadence according to the focal character’s attention and live action. Short sentences remain valid at genuinely abrupt moments.
- **Exposition-in-dialogue / forced recap:** two people restate known dates, family facts or previous scenes chiefly to tell the reader; an authentic personal need should cause any questions and details. Eliminate scripted interrogation/checklist beats. Avoid all characters sharing a clever rejoinder template.
- **Point-of-view leakage and epistemic shortcuts:** bà Lan can know her own order slip but not Thoa’s 1996 death or Cường’s living status at the time of order until she learns it through S03; Lan Anh does not know the real 1996 secret; outsider market death stories are hearsay, not certified fact. No pre-G6a hidden-insider selective interior, no premature spectral proof.
- **Agency and emotional specificity:** each participant is doing something consequential in the scene independent of delivering a clue. Keep Lan Anh’s real funeral purchases, right to withhold acceptance, Hảo’s receipt decisions and the consequences of gossip; do not explain their emotions after an action already communicates them.
- **Continuity/physical causality as a separate pass:** precisely TWO parcels leave approved S01; original mùng10 Thoa-prepaid slip and red radio remain real. Chợ Bến receiving-point details are a TEST seam, not silently made permanent without S01→S02→S03 preflight. Distinguish three local people named Cường by actual full-name/family confirmation. Public gossip reaches Long independently later.

### 4) Output a short editorial report, not an unsolicited rewrite

For each finding, quote the shortest unique phrase (or paragraph locator), indicate **what the first-time reader would experience**, identify whether the surrounding action already conveys the intended meaning, and choose **delete**, **surgical cut**, **leave**, or **needs factual/continuity fix**. State uncertainty when subjective. Include at least a few **LEAVE** examples of successful sustained dialogue/action, to prevent overediting. Do NOT supply blanket grades or force global short-sentence bans. If no flaw is observed, report none rather than inventing a quota.

### 5) Surgical revision by the writer after review

Return to the author-approved baseline for voice and pace. Make only justified targeted changes. Prefer deletion of redundant narrator explanation to paraphrasing it in pretty language. Keep normal tell/narration where it performs scene transitions, factual orientation or necessary interior proximity and has not been sufficiently shown. Compare reviewer comments with actual text; reject changes that would flatten voices, add theatrical folk sayings or prematurely reveal the supernatural. Run the separate factual/POV/logistics check before publishing a new V3 review candidate.

### 6) Author gate

Present a **new version** alongside a concise change log focused on what was cut/kept and unresolved continuity seam. The author must explicitly approve the new prose before it is copied into `scenes/` as canonical; the D-018 verbatim S01 remains untouched. S02 D-019 is direction-approved but verbatim prose still open; S03 V2’s **pace/dialogue** are accepted, complete text remains **unapproved pending independent review**.
