## A/B invariants — the exact same scene for both writers

**Narrative unit:** S01 "Tiệm vẫn phải mở": morning of the mùng14 **after Cường's death night mùng13**, at bà Trần Thị Lan's family votive-paper shop close to Chợ Bến in a contemporary northern/Kinh Bắc community. The later long-form volume has an independent domestic ghost-horror thread, but **this opening unit has no paranormal manifestation or proof**.

**One close limited viewpoint only: bà Lan.** She is a working owner/mother focused on actual orders, time, customers and how news affects her trade. Khanh is her adult 27-year-old son: after 1–2 years at a Japanese company he spent three years onsite in Japan and has JUST returned home for a few weeks' leave before returning to the company's Hanoi office. He may still have genuine Japan-facing remote work while away; he helps mother as a son, not as an investigator. His old district-high-school senior Lan Anh (1–2 school years above), who once openly liked him, is known to this family, but bà Lan and Khanh know only fragments of her marriage into the distant xóm Trại household ruled by Cổn, NOT her father-in-law's full name or even surname. Their dialogue and forms of address must follow this actual mother–adult-son relationship, northern everyday register and changing intimacy/annoyance, not a fixed dialect catchphrase. No limited POV jump into Khanh, Lan Anh, Thủy, Cường or a ghost; no author-certified spiritual statements. Bà Lan can remember a few objectively observable details from the mùng10 customer but this is **not** the complete later flashback S04.

**Working shop floor must carry the narrative without becoming an inventory list:** one or two current customer jobs with actual stages of preparation/finishing, an interruption that has a cost in time/reputation and a practical reason to consult the handwritten/digital order notes. Pick ordinary plausible craft operations and local social encounters; do not make bà Lan recite folklore, genealogy or lesson-plan-like exposition. Practical inputs (paper, scissors, glue, packaging) are physically usable, not endless decorative noun lists.

**Event premise and hard facts:**
