## A/B invariants — the exact same scene for both writers

**Narrative unit:** S01 "Tiệm vẫn phải mở": morning of the mùng14 **after Cường's death night mùng13**, at bà Trần Thị Lan's family votive-paper shop close to Chợ Bến in a contemporary northern/Kinh Bắc community. If proposed lunar-seven calendar is later approved, the Gregorian equivalent is 26 August 2026; BEFORE that decision, use **mùng14** alone and don't invent a Gregorian day. This is a **single sustained opening movement**, NOT all of CH-01; stop *before* bà Lan/Khanh and Lan Anh encounter one another at public Chợ Bến. The later long-form volume has an independent domestic ghost-horror thread, but **this opening unit has no paranormal manifestation or proof**.

**One close limited viewpoint only: bà Lan.** She is a working owner/mother focused on actual orders, time, customers and how news affects her trade. Khanh is her adult 27-year-old son and real Japan-returned/Japan-facing software engineer on a short break; he helps with errands but also has genuine remote handover/work distractions. Their dialogue and forms of address must follow this actual mother–adult-son relationship, northern everyday register and changing intimacy/annoyance, not a fixed dialect catchphrase. No limited POV jump into Khanh, Lan Anh, Thủy, Cường or a ghost; no author-certified spiritual statements. Bà Lan can remember a few objectively observable details from the mùng10 customer but this is **not** the complete later flashback S04.

**Working shop floor must carry the narrative without becoming an inventory list:** one or two current customer jobs with actual stages of preparation/finishing, an interruption that has a cost in time/reputation and a practical reason to consult the handwritten/digital order notes. Pick ordinary plausible craft operations and local social encounters; do not make bà Lan recite folklore, genealogy or lesson-plan-like exposition. Practical inputs (paper, scissors, glue, packaging) are physically usable, not endless decorative noun lists.

**Event premise and hard facts:**
- Morning mùng14 the shop learns from an ordinary local trade/social contact that Vũ Văn Cường died unexpectedly during the previous night. The speaker only knows/relays what they could know as gossip; no forensic findings or supernatural verdict.
- On mùng10 morning **while Cường was alive**, a woman calling herself **Thoa** placed and paid for a custom paper replica of a **red Czech cassette radio**, addressed to Cường. It includes a personal repaired/drawn detail the woman specified; the exact design may remain visually understated in this scene.
- bà Lan can recall the customer's ordinary appearance, calm conversation, **fluid left-handed action** and perhaps vague resemblance to family; she **does not know** purchaser's objective ghost identity or objective 1996 death. This left-hand clue is observation, not a forensic identity match.
- The finished order is now physically **in shop custody** and ready for a genuine attempted delivery. It was NOT already complete on mùng10; it has NOT yet been burned, delivered or returned from the market.
- Bà Lan and Khanh decide on an **ordinary delivery route to Chợ Bến**, not because Khanh launches a murder investigation. Their two routes may be separate ordinary errands and converge later, but **this scene ends before** meeting Lan Anh, before public refusal, before gossip leads Long to shop and before any ghost scare. Khanh's interest in order may be reluctant; mother has final practical say.

**Reveal and inference boundaries:**
- The reader sees why the mùng10 order feels wrong **now**, but is not authoritatively told Thoa died in 1996 or that the customer was a ghost; that is reserved for earned G6a and G6c. Bà Lan may be unsettled, suspicious of malicious practical possibilities, or focused on money/work and human decency; never has clairvoyance.
- No drug, counterfeit customer/identical woman, hidden cassette evidence from 1996 or guessed exact manner of Cường's death; the eventual real Thoa spirit order and supernatural killing remain author truth outside this scene.
- Bà Lan does not disclose official crime-scene records and Khanh cannot access police data. Long's later independent arrival is from public market gossip, never a direct Toàn/Lan Anh invitation.
- Keep emotional valence: ordinary funny/irritable shop work and genuine mother-son affection coexist with gradual unease. Do not make all dialogue oblique, continuously portentous or mechanically short; sustain action and conversational floor across multiple utterances without narrator reset.

**Required change at scene end:** a completed paper radio originally just another troublesome custom order has become an **ethical/business and social delivery decision**; bà Lan and Khanh are committed to approaching Cường's family via the market in daylight. The reader is unsettled by the mismatch between order date and death date, **not** provided a solution. They have not yet met Lan Anh or caused the public rumor.

**Suggested length test:** about **1,700–2,300 Vietnamese words** *for both drafts*, with natural deviation allowed for scene movement and no filler. Same intended length in both trials. A scene this size is a test unit, NOT a mandated chapter length or volume count.
